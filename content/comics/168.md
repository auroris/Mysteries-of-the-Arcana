2. A close view of Theresa’s upside-down face is surrounded by images of William, BbuzZ, and the Surgeon. “I thought about dog boy and BbuzZ and even that stupid robot.”
3. Theresa opens her eyes. “But mostly, I thought about what Colonel Poulter said when he presented me your flag.”
