1. Theresa looks at a wall-mounted control panel. “Dead end.”
2. She looks down a long corridor. “We go this way.”
3. Theresa and Chrystalline enter the corridor together, seen from behind.
4. They continue side by side.
5. They recede into the distance.
6. An unidentified source makes a mechanical *WHRRRR!*
7. Theresa and Chrystalline turn toward the sound. Theresa asks, “What was that?”
