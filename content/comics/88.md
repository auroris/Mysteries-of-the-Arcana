1. Against a black field, Chrystalline’s voice says, “Wake up.”
2. Naked Theresa wakes in a green-white glow. “Wha? Huh?”
3. Naked Chrystalline smiles and offers Theresa a red apple. “Eat this.”
4. In a dreamlike allegory of Original Sin, Theresa and Chrystalline stand beneath an apple tree. Deer, rabbits, a boar, a lion, birds, and a green snake coiled around the trunk surround them. Theresa holds the apple uncertainly. “I’m pretty sure we’re not supposed to eat that apple.”
