1. Empty ground lies against the base of the giant prison tower. Melody narrates, “THEY DID NOT REST QUIETLY, THESE CAPTURED GIANTS.”
2. The Wanderer lies curled on the ground. “THEY DID NOT SLEEP. THEY THRASHED AND SPIT AND STRUGGLED.”
3. A huge hand grips the Wanderer between two fingers. “THEY STRUGGLE TO THIS DAY.”
4. Melody stands before flames with a tiny figure cradled in her mechanical hand. “LOCKED IN THE CENTER OF THE ARCANA.”
