1. William rides before the emblem of the Knights of the Broken Highway. “THE KNIGHTS OF THE BROKEN HIGHWAY SERVE WITH HONOR. A HUNDRED STRONG, THEY DOTH PATROL THE OLD ROADS, GIVING AID AND SUCCOR TO TRAVELERS. WHEN A HORROR ’TIS ENCOUNTERED, THE KNIGHTS DRAW BLADE AND SLAY IT. THEY GIVE NO QUARTER AND FIGHT UNTO DEATH.”
2. Theresa looks toward William. “I GET IT. YOU KNOCKED ME OUT BECAUSE...” William explains, “ON RARE OCCASION, A LONE KNIGHT SEEKS TO CAPTURE A HORROR SO IT MIGHT BE STUDIED. SO WE MIGHT LEARN HOW TO END THEIR EVIL EXISTENCE ONCE AND FOR ALL.”
3. Theresa stares ahead in realization. “YOU KNOCKED ME OUT. PRETENDED I WAS A CAPTURED HORROR. YOU LIED SO THEY WOULDN’T KILL ME!”
