1. Theresa leans against the truck. “So, Kludge and Surgeon are hostages.”
2. She asks, “What’s lockdown?” Chrystalline explains, “An emergency protocol. All the inn’s entrances are sealed with impenetrable security shields.”
3. From above, Theresa, Chrystalline, William, and Mandrake stand around the tracked truck. Theresa asks, “How did they do all this?” William tests the garage door with a *THUNK*. Chrystalline answers, “Except the garage, I suppose.” Theresa corrects herself, “Why did they do all this?”
4. William stands at the open garage door. “It matters not. Our friends and our very home art imperiled. We must take action!”
5. The garage door slams shut with a *BLAM*.
6. William clutches a bleeding arm. “We must, perhaps, take a different action.”
