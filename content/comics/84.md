1. Above the group’s tunnel, a Doomwalker scans the ground with descending green rings. Theresa asks, “How do you suppose they could find us? Vibrational analysis?” William replies, “If so, thou hast given our position away. Nay, ’tis more likely thermographic in nature.”
2. The scan expands around the travelers’ silhouettes below the Doomwalker. Theresa says, “Could be ground penetrating radar. Who cares? I want to know what happens if they find us.”
3. A spinning drill bursts through the tunnel wall with a *WHRRRRR!* and strikes Theresa’s shoulder. “Gah!”
4. The drill pins Theresa against the ground. She looks at it. “Oh. That.”
