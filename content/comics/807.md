1. The pawnbroker gestures with one hand. “I’m told you’ve been looking for this for a very long time. Wait here.”
2. William watches him. “Aye. And who doth share my interests with thee?”
3. The pawnbroker walks farther down the aisle as William remains behind. “Oh, no one of consequence. Just one of my little birds.”
4. Reaching into the shelves, the pawnbroker looks back. “Now, of course you are willing to agree to my price?”
5. William’s expression hardens. “Your price?”
