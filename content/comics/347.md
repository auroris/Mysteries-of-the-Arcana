1. Chrystalline touches her shoulder. “I am sorry, father, but I cannot stay.”
2. He says, “I have things I must do. Chrystalline, this matter concerns our people. Quite possibly our very survival. What can be more important than that?”
3. Chrystalline folds her arms. “Apologies.”
4. Her father asks, “Apologies?”
5. She answers, “Profuse ones. Nothing could be more important.”
6. Mandrake reappears behind her with a *POP!* and speaks in picture symbols showing a row of six hand gestures.
