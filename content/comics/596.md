1. Chrystalline touches one of the garden’s flowers. “I cannot.” Theresa asks, “What do you mean you can’t?”
2. Chrystalline replies, “You are the one the garden responds to. Not me. Perhaps if you recited a poem?”
3. Theresa says, “No!”
4. She raises her hands in frustrated protest. “You...”
5. Theresa throws up her hands. “Gah!”
6. She presses her fingers to her temples. “Fine!”
