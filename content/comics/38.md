1. Chrystalline stands below William on the wooden stairs and looks up at him. “Take heed, William. I will rescue my mother.”
2. She advances until she stands directly before William, who keeps his arms folded. “Nothing short of death will keep me here and I know your idiotic code of honor forbids you from doing harm to a woman.”
3. Chrystalline points at William's chest while he looks down at her. “Now, either remove yourself from our path or lend us your sword arm. I am calling your bluff, William. What will you do?”
