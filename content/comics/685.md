1. A green, red-crested attacker grips Mandrake while a blade extends from his mechanical forearm with a *SHLING*. The horned attacker aims his weapon from the foreground.
2. The horned attacker fires a mass of green slime with a *GLOMP!*
3. Slime engulfs Mandrake’s body and wings. She broadcasts her face, two cage-like objects, and an exclamation mark.
4. The two attackers watch the slime-covered Mandrake. She broadcasts two linked rings, her face, and a question mark, followed by a black-and-yellow bristled object and her face.
5. Mandrake looks toward a neon outline of a jester painted on the wall.
6. The neon jester’s pale face smiles beneath a purple cap.
