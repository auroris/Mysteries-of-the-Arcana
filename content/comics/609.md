1. Ead returns. “I’m back!”
2. A tall black-suited figure with a round yellow face looms behind him as Chrystalline asks, “Ead, have you tried to talk to the Red Clan?”
3. Ead replies, “Talk to the Reds? You can’t do that!” Mister Mist puts an arm around him. “Indeed, chieftain! Everyone knows the Reds can’t be reasoned with!”
4. Ead says, “Chrystalline and Theresa, this is our friend, Mister Mist.” Mister Mist answers, “Ah, the famous children of the Inn!”
5. Theresa murmurs, “Now that’s just creepy.”
