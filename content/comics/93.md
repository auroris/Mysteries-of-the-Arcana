1. Chrystalline lies close to Theresa in the dream and clasps her hands. “So...”
2. Theresa smiles with her eyes closed. “*MMMMM...*”
3. Chrystalline’s face merges with Debbie’s freckled face. Chrystalline says, “...tell me...” Debbie finishes, “...are you going to kill her too?”
4. Theresa wakes in a tent, clutching her cross. “Debby!”
