1. The shop door swings beneath its bell with a *DING*.
2. William steps inside and raises a hand near his face. “HELLO?”
3. Behind the counter, the pawnbroker lowers a jeweler’s loupe from one eye and smiles. “AH, MISTER BLACKE. I’VE BEEN EXPECTING YOU.”
4. William touches his chest in surprise. “HOW DOST THOU KNOW MY NAME?”
