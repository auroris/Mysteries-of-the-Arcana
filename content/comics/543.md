1. LEGO Kludge stands beside a fan of illustrated tarot cards. “I should probably take a moment to mention the Tarot. You know what the Tarot is, right? A series of images on cards used to divine information? Every arcana has some native variation of the Tarot.”
2. Kludge gestures. “In fact, a lot of the terms used by people who visit here regularly are Tarot-inspired!”
3. Kludge faces forward. “Arcana, Royal Road, Keys... all words from Tarot mythology. Even the arcana are named after Tarot cards!” The footer reads “KLUDGE’S GUIDE FOR NEW KEYS, PT 8.”
