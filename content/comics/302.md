1. William and Theresa stand beside the large black mount. William warns, “I WARN, I AM NO STORYTELLER.” Theresa replies, “STILL WANNA KNOW.”
2. William gestures. “AYE.”
3. They mount up with a *SHUFF*. William says, “THE HORRORS WERE ONCE AS *THOU* ART NOW.”
4. As they ride, William’s story appears in pale silhouettes against the stars. “THEY RULED THIS LAND. THEIR BUILDINGS REACHED INTO THE CLOUDS. THEIR VESSELS FLEW OVER THEM.”
5. Figures fight and work among scientific equipment. William continues, “WITH THEIR BODIES, THEY CONQUERED THE REALMS OF CHALLENGE AND SPORT. WITH THEIR MINDS, THEY CONQUERED THE REALMS OF MEDICINE AND SCIENCE. WITH THEIR SOULS THEY DID LITTLE. PERHAPS IF THEY HAD, THEY MIGHT STILL BE *MEN*... INSTEAD OF *MONSTERS.*”
