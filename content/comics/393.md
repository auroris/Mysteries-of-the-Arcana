1. Chrystalline walks beside Theresa. “HE DOES KNOW HOW TO RUIN A MOMENT, DOESN’T HE?” Theresa looks down. “YEAH, WELL...”
2. They stop at a pair of wooden doors. Theresa looks toward them. “WHAT’S THIS THING WE’RE GOING TO? ALL I KNOW IS THERE’LL BE A BONFIRE.”
3. Chrystalline puts an arm around Theresa’s shoulders. “I BELIEVE I TOLD YOU BEFORE. MOTHER TRAVELS THE ARCANA, COLLECTING TALES. SHE TAKES THOSE TALES; SEARCHES FOR COMMONALITIES AND CONNECTING THREADS.”
4. Chrystalline continues as they push through the doors. “OVER TIME, SHE HAS DISTILLED THESE TALES INTO THE ORIGIN STORY OF THE ARCANA.”
5. Chrystalline smiles. “WE CALL THE PERFORMANCE OF THAT STORY THE TELLING.”
