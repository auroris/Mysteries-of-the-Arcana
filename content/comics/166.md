1. A drop box releases through the Deliverer’s hatch with a *PLOCK!*
2. A green targeting display shows the drop boxes falling away. Theresa says from off panel, “They’re away.”
3. Theresa says from off panel, “Your turn.” Theresa kicks the surgeon off the aircraft with a *KICK!* Its display reads “00:47.”
4. Theresa sits at the open hatch. “My turn.” The Surgeon cries from below, “[PANICKED EXCLAMATION] AGGGGGGHHH!!!”
5. Theresa and the Surgeon fall through the gray sky together. The Surgeon’s display reads “00:44,” and the aircraft recedes behind them.
