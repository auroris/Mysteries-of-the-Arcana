1. William’s armored hand passes beside Theresa’s boot. Theresa asks, “WHAT’S THE PLAN?”
2. William picks up Theresa’s pistol. “WE LEAVE THIS CAMP. WE GATHER THE VITAMIN X. WE RETURN TO THE INN.”
3. William and Theresa walk together carrying their weapons. Theresa asks, “HOW DO WE STAY AHEAD OF YOUR FRIENDS?”
4. William points to several giant ants tethered around a dead tree. “WE RIDE.”
5. Theresa grins. “SWEET!”
