1. A close view shows the back of Theresa’s boot, with William’s armored foot in the background. Theresa asks, “What’s the plan?”
2. William hands Theresa her pistol and holster. “We leave this camp. We gather the Vitamin X. We return to the Inn.”
3. Theresa attaches the holster to her belt as she and William walk together. “How do we stay ahead of your friends?”
4. William points to several giant ants tethered around a dead tree. “We ride.”
5. Theresa grins. “Sweet!”
