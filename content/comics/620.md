1. General Isc and a guard wait while Chrystalline and Theresa sit on a bench.
2. The two Red Viquirrels confer with a rapid *CHIR-CHITTER*.
3. The guard points a rifle at the women. “Prisoners! Why are you here?” Theresa warns, “Hey! Watch where you point that thing!”
4. Chrystalline says, “We are here to invite you to a peace conference with the Grey Clan.”
5. The guard objects, “Peace? With the Greys? Impossible!”
6. A voice behind him says, “Impossible indeed, General Isc.”
