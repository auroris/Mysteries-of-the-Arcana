1. In black and white, Theresa glances sideways while lying bound.
2. One of her boots jerks a stake free with a *POP!*
3. Theresa bends her knees while her right wrist remains tied to its stake.
4. Theresa plants both boots on the ground.
5. Theresa swings both legs over her head.
6. Theresa rolls onto her side and begins pushing herself upright.
