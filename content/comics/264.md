1. Still lying on her back, Theresa glances toward her other bound foot.
2. Theresa pulls that foot free of its boot with a *POP!* The empty boot remains tied to its stake.
3. With both feet free, Theresa bends her knees while her wrists remain tied to their stakes.
4. Theresa plants her socked feet on the ground.
5. She jumps and flips her legs over her head, pivoting between her bound wrists.
6. Theresa lands right-side up and pushes herself onto her knees.
