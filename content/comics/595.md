1. Chrystalline recognizes the flowering place while a plant figure watches them. “Hmm? Oh. We are in the Garden of Epic Manly Poetry.”
2. She explains, “Young Viquirrel males come here to shout poetry to the sky, hoping to gain a word of power. The garden shapes itself to the poet’s thought to provide inspiration.”
3. Chrystalline leans against a blushing Theresa. “Apparently, the garden believes you to be a young Viquirrel male.” Theresa snaps, “Shut up!”
4. One of the plant figures embraces another behind them. Chrystalline asks, “My, my, Theresa. Whatever could you have been thinking of?” Theresa says, “Just shut up and make it stop!”
