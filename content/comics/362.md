Theresa stands against a black background while images recall her pain, William speaking to her, and Chrystalline kissing her. She responds:

“I’m not gonna lie. It hurt, what happened. All those memories. All that pain. I wasn’t ready to face it. You lying to me and pushing me into it made it worse. But... crap, I hate doing this.

“But, yeah, someone pointed out to me that friendship is important. Friends make mistakes. Sometimes, they make big ones. Your apology sounds real to me, so I’m gonna trust you.

“I forgive you.”
