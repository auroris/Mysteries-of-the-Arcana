1. Theresa settles into a wooden pew with her crutches drawn close against her body.
2. Theresa bows her head and clasps her hands over a crutch.
3. An older priest approaches the pew. “Do you have a safe place to stay?” Theresa looks up in surprise. “What?”
4. The priest faces her and repeats, “Do you have a safe place to stay?”
