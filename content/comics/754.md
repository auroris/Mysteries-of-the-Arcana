1. In loose pencil linework, Theresa sits in the foreground while Chrystalline watches from behind her. “I LIKE MY LIFE HERE.”
2. Theresa rises into the foreground as Chrystalline smiles in the background. “I LIKE THE ADVENTURE AND THE CRAZINESS OF TRAVELING TO WEIRD ARCANA.”
3. A close view centers Theresa's clenched hand at her side while Chrystalline remains seated behind her. “I WENT DOWN INTO THAT PLACE TO DIE AND INSTEAD I FOUND A REASON TO LIVE.”
4. Chrystalline continues smiling up at her. Theresa says, “YOU KNOW WHAT I MEAN.”
5. Chrystalline wraps her arms around Theresa from behind and rests her face beside hers. “I KNOW. I LOVE YOU, TOO.”
