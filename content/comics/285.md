2. A tennis racket smashes into the ground beside Theresa with a *CRACK!*
3. Theresa looks up from the dirt. “OH, CRAP.”
4. The racket strikes Theresa with a *CRUNCH!*
5. The blindfolded female horror stands over Theresa holding her racket. “I TOOK BALLET UNTIL I WAS NINE!”
