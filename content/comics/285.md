1. In the foreground, William wields his black hole blade against the male horror. In the background, Theresa has tumbled from her dead ant while the female horror looms over her. The man says, “My lawn really needs a trim!”
2. The female horror swings and misses, smashing her racket into the ground beside Theresa with a *CRACK!*
3. Theresa rolls onto her back and looks up. “Oh, crap.”
4. Theresa dodges the next swing. The racket digs into the ground where she had been lying with a *CRUNCH!*
5. Theresa pulls the female horror’s sweatband down over her eyes. Oblivious, the horror says, “I took ballet until I was nine!”
