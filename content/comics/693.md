1. Kietra sights along a large rifle while Aleena works at a wall console. Aleena explains, “THE GOLDEN PARACHUTE HAS THE SELF-DESTRUCT ACTIVATION SEQUENCE SYNCED TO HIS HEARTBEAT. SOMETHING ABOUT THE SHIP GOING DOWN WITH THE CAPTAIN.”
2. A diagram equates Captain Featherbeard's crossed-out heart with a red timer reading “5:00.” Below it, the Golden Parachute's crew runs toward escape pods while a projected image shows Kietra and Aleena inside a vertical-beamed chamber. Aleena continues, “ONCE THE SELF-DESTRUCT SEQUENCE ACTIVATES THE CREW WILL PANIC AND RUN FOR THE PODBAY TO ESCAPE FIERY DEATH. WHERE WE'LL BE WAITING, SINCE THIS SLINGSHOT CHAMBER I'M FINISHING NOW WILL TELEPORT US THERE.”
3. In a close view of their eyes, Kietra asks, “CLIENT SAYS NO SURVIVORS?” Aleena answers, “CLIENT SAYS NO SURVIVORS.”
