1. William presses a button on his belt with a *CLICK!* and *HSSSSSSS* as Theresa, Chrystalline, and the large plant creature stand nearby. Chrystalline explains, “Like us, BbuzZ traveled the Arcana. They could not, however, ignore the plight of their people.”
2. The button triggers a static discharge that cleans William’s armor. Blue electricity spreads over it with a *HSSSSSSSSSSSSSS* while Chrystalline gestures toward the plant creature. “They returned here to battle the Machine Intelligence. BbuzZ remains our point of contact when we visit The Devil.” Theresa replies, “Well, I guess plant people make as much sense as dog men and fairies.”
3. A close view shows the creature’s pitted green body. Chrystalline continues from off panel, “You misunderstand. The plant is merely a beast of burden. A tool used to manipulate the world.”
4. Five small winged insects hover above the creature. Chrystalline says from off panel, “Look higher. Those five insects? They are the BbuzZ.”
