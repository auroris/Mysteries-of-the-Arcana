1. Chrystalline clasps her hands. “MANDRAKE!”
2. She pleads, “I AM SO SORRY! PLEASE, FORGIVE ME?” Mandrake responds with picture symbols showing a raised middle finger above a circuit board.
3. Chrystalline raises a hand. “I PROMISE! I HAVE LEARNED MY LESSON!”
4. She hugs Mandrake. Mandrake answers with a thumbs-up pictogram.
5. Chrystalline asks, “NOW, WILL YOU HELP ME RETRIEVE MY DIGITAL STORAGE UNIT SO WE MAY GO HOME?” Mandrake again gives a thumbs-up. Her father protests, “CHRYSTALLINE, WAIT!”
