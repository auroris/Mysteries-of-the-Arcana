1. The front of a parked green car sits before a wall of red, blue, and green blocks. In a square inset, the radio screen displays a yellow, catlike eye. The radio plays, “...On Blueberry Hill...”
2. The car remains still. The inset radio eye watches as the song continues, “...When I found you...”
3. Inside the car, Theresa’s hand rests on Chrystalline’s bare waist as their bodies press together. The radio-eye inset remains open. The song continues, “The moon stood still...” Theresa says, “...Know we’re supposed to be... working on the... beast.”
4. Theresa and Chrystalline lean close enough to kiss. The radio plays, “...On Blueberry Hill...” Chrystalline replies, “We are. We are currently ensuring the functionality of the driver’s seat.”
5. A vast orange explosion fills the panel with a *THOOM*.
