2. Blue stands beside the Surgeon. Circe identifies them: “BLUE AND THE SURGEON.”
3. Melody stands beside Kludge. Circe continues, “MELODY AND KLUDGE.”
4. Theresa raises her hand as Circe says, “THERESA AND...” Theresa cuts in, “I’LL TAKE DOG BOY!”
5. Circe turns a page. “VERY WELL...”
6. Mandrake sits on Chrystalline’s shoulder as Circe says from off panel, “THEN CHRYSTALLINE AND MANDRAKE ARE THE FINAL PAIR.” Chrystalline scowls. “DAMNATION!”
