1. Melody and Kludge sit with their backs to the sea. Melody says, “THERESA WAS HURTING. WE ALL SAW IT.” Kludge replies, “I KNOW.”
2. Melody raises her glass as Kludge says, “CHRYS JUST WANTED TO HELP THERESA.” Melody asks, “BY DECIDING SHE KNEW BEST? WITHOUT CONSULTING ANYONE ELSE OR GIVING THE CONSEQUENCES REAL THOUGHT?”
3. Kludge glances back while Melody drinks. “PRETTY MUCH.” Melody adds, “*THAT* TRAIT SHE INHERITED FROM HER FATHER.”
4. Kludge raises his glass. “WELL... NOTHING WE CAN DO NOW. THE GIRLS NEED TO WORK THIS OUT FOR THEMSELVES.” Melody agrees, “TRUE.”
5. Melody and Kludge sit silently with their drinks.
6. Melody looks toward Kludge. “SHOULD WE GO GET CIRCE’S THINGS NOW?” Kludge answers, “...NAH.”
