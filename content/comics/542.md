1. LEGO Kludge raises a hand. “See, each Key also has another power. What that power is varies from Key to Key. Through meditation and concentration you can draw your Key power out to help you.”
2. An illustrated card marked “5” is overlaid with LEGO figures. Kludge explains, “You’ll know you’re using your Key power because you’ll feel a sense of euphoria and surety.” Beside it, text introduces the list:

Example Key powers include:

- Knowing the weakness of your enemy and how to exploit it.
- Knowing your enemy’s strengths and patterns.
- Being able to tell when someone is lying.
- An innate understanding of how multiple items can work together in new ways.
- Making random chance work in your favor.
- Being so still others forget about you.
- And others!
