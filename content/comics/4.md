1. Theresa enters a dim corridor marked “Subway Maintenance.” She narrates, “No note. I didn’t want a lot of drama.”
2. Theresa pulls off her green overshirt. “No pills or razor blades. Those might not get the job done.”
3. A close view shows the pistol holstered at her hip. “Just me and the first gun daddy ever gave me.”
4. Theresa reaches the maintenance area and finds Chrystalline firing blue-white magic at a massive blue Machine Intelligence robot. “I went into the darkness to end my life. Instead, I found a reason to live.”
