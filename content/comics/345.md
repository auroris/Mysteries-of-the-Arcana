1. Her father raises a hand. “YES. I HAVE DEVELOPED A NEW TECHNIQUE FOR SCRYING BETWEEN ARCANA. USING—”
2. Chrystalline interrupts, “FATHER, WE NEED OUR ALLIES!” He replies, “I AGREE, BUT WE NEED THEM STRATEGICALLY.” She asks, “WHAT?”
3. He explains, “TOO MUCH ABOUT THIS ENEMY REMAINS UNKNOWN. GOALS. CAPABILITIES. ORIGIN. IN THE ABSENCE OF FACTS, ASSUMPTIONS MUST BE MADE. THAT OUR ENEMY THINKS STRATEGICALLY. THAT ANY BEING CAPABLE OF OBSERVING A DREAMWALK CAN ALSO WATCH THE ROYAL ROAD.”
4. Chrystalline stares at him in horror. “YOU... ...WANT TO USE OUR FRIENDS... ...MY MOTHER... AS BAIT?”
