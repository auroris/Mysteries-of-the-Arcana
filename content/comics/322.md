1. William examines his clawed hand. “WITH VITAMIN X, I REMAIN MYSELF.”
2. He throws his head back. “WITHOUT IT, I AM NAUGHT BUT A DUMB BEAST.”
3. Theresa watches him skeptically as William touches his muzzle.
4. Theresa smiles. “WELL, EVEN WITH IT...” William snaps, “THOU STILL ART NOT FUNNY!”
