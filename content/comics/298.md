1. Chrystalline looks over her shoulder at Mandrake while flying. “Hang on tight! I’m activating the turbo boosters!”
2. The saucer’s rear lights glow red as its engines make a *WHIRRRRR*.
3. The engines sputter *PUT PUT BEEEWWWWWWW* as the saucer slows.
4. Chrystalline reaches for the controls while Mandrake speaks in picture symbols showing Mandrake hanging on a bar above her head, and a closeup of her hands on the bar emphasized, with an exclamation mark: “I'm hanging on tight!” Chrystalline cries, “No no no no no!!!”
5. The tentacled ship looms behind the stalled saucer. Chrystalline says, “This is not the time for sarcasm, Mandrake.”
