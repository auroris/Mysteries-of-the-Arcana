A handheld device labeled “Kludge” displays “Notes on the Tower.” Kludge’s notes read:

“I’ve seen my world’s future if we aren’t careful. The Tower’s bleak landscape appears to be a result of a nuclear holocaust.

“Whatever species began the nuclear catastrophe wasn’t around to see the end of it. Instead, several different animal species evolved into sentience to take their place. I’m talking about anthropomorphized mammals. It would be a furry lover’s dream if the place wasn’t such a freaking nightmare.

“Females are treated both as chattel and as a precious commodity. Objectively, from an anthropological viewpoint, I can see why. From what I saw and heard, pregnancies carried to term are rare and as many as one out of every three children is born horribly deformed. The continuation of the various species almost seems to demand females be kept from harm so they can perform an important biological function: giving birth.

“The treatment of women there sucks, but I can understand the psychology of it.”
