1. Aleena’s face reflects in Surgeon’s red display. “[INFORMATIVE CORRECTION] The Asimov bolt which impairs this unit does not function inside cyberspace.”
2. Aleena raises her arms. “Oh, no.”
3. Surgeon continues, “[CONFIDENT DECLARATION] The intruder may indeed possess superior skills compared to most biologicals. This is irrelevant.” Aleena shouts, “No!”
4. Surgeon charges with a *WHRRRR*. “[GLEEFUL REVELATION] This unit is pure machine superiority. While the intruder...”
5. Aleena’s digital form is dragged away across the grid. Surgeon finishes, “...Is nothing but meat.” Aleena cries, “Noooooooooooo—” A system message reads “User dropped carrier.”
