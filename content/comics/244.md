1. Mandrake speaks in picture symbols showing Theresa’s face followed by a question mark.
2. Chrystalline sits with her hands folded in her lap. “I do not know.”
3. Chrystalline looks down. “My mother once told me, the true mark of love is when you care more for the well-being of the person than for the well-being of the relationship.”
4. A close view shows the pendant at Chrystalline’s neck. “I believe the visit to the knower helped her.”
5. Chrystalline smiles faintly. “In time, she will forgive me.”
