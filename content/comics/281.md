1. Theresa and William ride side by side. Theresa says, “OKAY, THANKS. I APPRECIATE YOU SAVING MY LIFE.” She thinks, “JUST WISH PEOPLE WOULD STOP MAKING DECISIONS FOR ME.”
2. William remembers Theresa striking him inside the Devil. “HAST THOU FORGOTTEN RENDERING ME UNCONSCIOUS IN THE DEVIL?”
3. Theresa looks down. “...YEAH, WELL. SORRY.” William replies, “I’VE NO NEED FOR APOLOGIES. IN THE FIELD, DECISIONS MUST BE MADE.”
4. William turns toward Theresa. “IN HINDSIGHT, THOU DIDST WELL AND SAVED OUR LIVES. HOWEVER, ’TIS NOT MY ACTIONS THAT VEX THEE, I BELIEVE.”
5. Theresa closes her eyes. “THE KNOWER OF TRUTH CAN BE... UNSETTLING.”
