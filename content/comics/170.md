1. Theresa sits in a wheelchair at a desk, writing. She looks back over her shoulder. “Huh?”
2. Theresa sets her pen down on the page. “Oh, yeah. Food sounds good.”
3. Chrystalline stands with folded arms in the doorway while Theresa turns toward her. “You know, most keys know not to pass through the road while in free fall.”
4. Theresa’s cast-covered legs rest on the raised supports of her wheelchair. “Didn’t have a whole lot of choice. The feather slowed me down enough.” Chrystalline raises one hand. “I suppose two broken legs are preferable to the alternative. Still, a few hours with Blue...”
5. The desk and open page remain in the empty room as Theresa calls from off panel, “I am not laying down buck naked inside an intelligent blob of goo!”
