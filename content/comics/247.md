1. Chrystalline gestures toward the crowd as Mandrake flies beside her. Chrystalline says, “SPLIT UP! WE MUST LOCATE THAT THIEF!”
2. Chrystalline questions a blond elf, who replies, “NO.”
3. A gray alien scratches his head. “UH-UH.”
4. A catlike person looks upward. “NOPE. SORRY.”
5. A green person points through the crowd. “LITTLE GUY IN AN ISLAND SHIRT? YEAH, LIKE, THATAWAY MAN...”
6. Two men watch Mandrake fly past. One asks, “DUDE, WAS THAT A FAIRY?” The other holds a rainbow lollipop and says, “THIS IS SOME GOOD SHIT, MAN...” Mandrake answers in picture symbols with a thumbs-up and an exclamation mark.
