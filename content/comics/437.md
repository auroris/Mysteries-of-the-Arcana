1. Melody holds her mechanical hand to her chest. “They cannot escape.”
2. She lowers both hands to her stomach. “But as they struggle, pieces break off.”
3. Red, yellow, green, and blue spheres float above Melody’s mechanical palm. “Bits of essence bubble away into their reality and beyond, throughout all the Arcana.”
4. The colored spheres rise before the flames. “Seeking. Finding. Merging.”
5. Melody smiles broadly. “With us.”
