1. Inside the cellar, Mandrake hunches and presses her hands to her ears near the candleholder and other living objects. A bright burst of scattered marks appears above her by the door.
2. Outside, the wolf-man pounds one fist against the crossbarred door. Mandrake's glowing symbols above him show her face, lips, the smiling wolf's face, and a question mark.
3. The wolf-man lowers his fist and turns with a sly grin as Mandrake's face, lips, and the smiling wolf's face remain above him.
4. He faces the door holding a small key-like object. A trophy marked “#1” and an exclamation mark appear above him.
