A handheld device labeled “Kludge” displays “Notes on the Kn of S.” Kludge’s notes read:

“I’ve been to many a strange arcana. Universes full of cyberpunk fairies, talking animal people, magic, advanced technology, and sentient blobs of blue goo. The strangest place I’ve ever been, however, is the Knight of Swords. It isn’t the sentients there. They’re more or less human-like. No, what makes the Knight of Swords so odd is the entire population’s obsession with puzzles. Everyone does them and puzzling seem to be as much a part of daily life as conversations.

“‘That’s a nice coat. It reminds me of a puzzle! Here, let’s see if you can solve it.’

“‘Three clergymen from different religions walked into a bar. Here’s the puzzle they found.’

“‘You’re sinking in quicksand? I’ll find something to drag you out with. In the meantime, here’s a puzzle to keep your mind off things!’

“I’m not exaggerating. Well, maybe just a little, but it isn’t far from the truth.

“While I was there, I met a fellow professor with a top hat and his young ward. It seems they wander their world having adventures, which always involves solving puzzles. He was quite the gentleman, though.”
