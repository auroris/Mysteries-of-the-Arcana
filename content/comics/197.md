1. Trees fill the background as Theresa cries and swings her pistol from side to side. “Get away from me!”
2. Theresa fires five times. *BLAM! BLAM! BLAM! BLAM! BLAM!*
3. The empty pistol answers with four clicks: *CLICK! CLICK! CLICK! CLICK!*
4. The green slime strikes Theresa in the stomach and slams her against a tree with a *WHAAAAAM*. “Remember.”
5. Theresa releases her pistol and lets it fall from her hand. “No!”
6. Tears stream down Theresa’s face. “Please, no...”
