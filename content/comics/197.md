1. Theresa swings her pistol from side to side. “Get away from me!”
2. Green tendrils lash around Theresa as she fires five times. *BLAM! BLAM! BLAM! BLAM! BLAM!*
3. The empty pistol answers with five *CLICK!* sounds.
4. A thick tendril slams Theresa against a tree with a *WHAAAAAM*. The creature commands, “Remember.”
5. Theresa begins to lower the pistol. “No!”
6. Tears stream down Theresa’s face. “Please, no...”
