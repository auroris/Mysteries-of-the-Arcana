1. Theresa raises one hand toward the plant creature while the tiny BbuzZ hover overhead. “Nice to meet you. I guess.” The BbuzZ answer, “Pleasure. Must hurry. They seek.”
2. Theresa scratches her head and looks upward past the plant creature. “They seek? Who, the robots?”
3. Above ground, several towering machines converge across the wasteland. The BbuzZ identify them in red caption boxes: “Bringers of death.” “Doomwalkers.”
