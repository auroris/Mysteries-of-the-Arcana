1. Sitting beside her father in the cell, Chrystalline says, “I refuse to believe these third-rate pirates are actually holding you against your will, father.”
2. He replies, “One never knows. I *could* be a true prisoner.”
3. Chrystalline and her father exchange a sideways glance.
4. They smile at one another.
5. They clasp hands. Chrystalline says, “I have missed you, father.” He replies, “And I you, little one.”
