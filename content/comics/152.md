1. The Surgeon’s long black arms loom over the table. Theresa looks up. “Who said that?”
2. The many-armed Surgeon answers, “[Reply] This unit reported the condition of its test subject.”
3. Chrystalline rises and confronts The Surgeon. “You did this?!?” The Surgeon replies, “[Reply] Affirmative. [Boast] Any other surgeon unit would have terminated the subject with the first amputation.”
4. Purple energy blazes around Chrystalline’s eyes and fists. “Let me show you how much I appreciate your skills!”
