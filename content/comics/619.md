2. Isc points at another soldier. “Soldier! Go get our friend!” The soldier salutes. “Sir, yes sir!”
3. Isc leaves Chrystalline and Theresa seated together. Chrystalline says, “They have a ‘friend’ too, it seems.” Theresa replies, “Arms dealers starting a war for profit. I feel like I’m in an 80’s action show.”
