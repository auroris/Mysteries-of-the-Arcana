1. William looks toward Theresa as they ride. “LOOK ABOUT THEE.”
2. Their ants cross the barren landscape beneath a glaring sun. William continues, “’TIS HARSH, THIS ENVIRONMENT. THE SUN, BRIGHT. POTABLE WATER, RARE.”
3. Behind William are images of a giant insect and armed animal-like people. “OTHER DANGERS ABOUND. MONSTERS... BRIGANDS...”
4. William and Theresa ride side by side. “...AND MOST TERRIBLE OF ALL, *HORRORS.*” Theresa asks, “AND, WHAT? I LOOK LIKE ONE OF THESE ‘HORRORS’?”
5. In a close view, William answers, “AYE.”
