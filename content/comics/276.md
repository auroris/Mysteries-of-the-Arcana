1. Chrystalline’s small saucer lifts away across the desert while Uncle Mork waves.
2. Back inside, a musical-note symbol appears above Uncle Mork.
3. He sets a beige communications terminal on the counter with a *CLUNK*.
4. Uncle Mork presses a control labeled “TRANSMIT” alongside the “ENTER” and “OFF” controls.
5. Uncle Mork speaks into the terminal. “S’ME. YA’LL GET MY SIGNAL?” The terminal answers with *Bzzz bzzzzz*. “YEAH. JUST LIKE YA’LL SAID. GIRL WITH POINTED EARS AND A LITTLE WINGED THING. THEY’RE ON THEIR WAY.” Another burst of *Bzzz bzz bzzz bzzzz bzz* follows. “JUST REMEMBER, I EXPECT PAYMENT AND MY SHIP BACK, YA’HEAR?”
