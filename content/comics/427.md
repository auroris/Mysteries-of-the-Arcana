1. A volcano erupts. Melody narrates, “THE FOUR FAMILIES FOUGHT FOR DOMINANCE.”
2. Soldiers and elemental figures clash among giant roots. “THE NEWCOMERS FOUGHT TO CREATE.”
3. A captured figure is drained into a swirling pool. “IN BATTLE, ESSENCES WERE HARVESTED.”
4. Glowing family sigils spread across a curved surface. “AFTER BATTLE, ESSENCES WERE SEWN.”
5. A luminous cosmic skull forms inside an explosion of blood and sigils. “AND THUS, NEW ARCANA WERE BORN.”
