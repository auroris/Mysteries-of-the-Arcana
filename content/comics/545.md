1. A bright object lies on a dark instrument panel. Kludge narrates, “The Fool is an interesting place with very unusual physical properties.”
2. A wheeled measuring device stands in darkness. “Take, for example, how light acts while outdoors.”
3. Kludge wears an elaborate helmet covered in wires, coils, plugs, and other improvised electronics. “Artificially generated light only seems to travel a few feet here but some objects, such as fire and living beings and the Royal Road, can be seen at a distance as if fully illuminated.”
