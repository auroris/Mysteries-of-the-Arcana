1. Theresa leaves her gray-walled bedroom with a towel over one shoulder.
2. Carrying a green bag, Theresa steps through a rounded doorway into the Inn and looks upward.
3. Melody, Kludge, William, Chrystalline, Circe, and Blue spring up behind the bar in party hats and shout, “SURPRISE!” Mandrake flies above them and projects an orange burst followed by three green exclamation marks. Beside Blue, the Surgeon states, “FORCED EXCLAMATORY STATEMENT OF INSINCERE WELL-WISHING SURPRISE.”
