1. William raises a weapon toward the dark mass above him.
2. He bares his teeth. “NAY!”
3. His battered face hardens. “ONE DAY, THOU MAYEST DEVOUR ME...”
4. William lowers the weapon with a *CLICK!* “...BUT NOT TODAY!”
5. Kneeling amid the attackers’ feet, he repeats, “NOT TODAY.”
