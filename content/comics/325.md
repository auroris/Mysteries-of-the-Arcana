1. A skeletal hand holds up three fingers.
2. The hand lowers to two fingers.
3. The hand lowers to one finger.
4. William yanks open the freight-car door and roars at a skeleton in a blue uniform with a *RRRRRRUMBLE*.
5. Theresa raises a pistol and fires with a *BANG!!!*
6. The bullet blasts through the skeleton’s skull, scattering bone fragments and teeth.
