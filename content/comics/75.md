1. Chrystalline places a hand on Theresa’s shoulder from behind. “Worry not.”
2. Theresa spins and strikes Chrystalline with a *WHAM!*
3. Theresa seizes Chrystalline’s arm and throws her to the tunnel floor with a *WHUMP!*
4. Theresa keeps one boot on Chrystalline’s neck while aiming her pistol. Chrystalline says, “They are a friend. Ow...”
5. Theresa stands as Chrystalline rises beside her. William laughs, “Ha! Ha! Ha! Ha!” and applauds with *CLAP CLAP CLAP*.
6. William grins with his hands clasped. “Clearly, I erred. Thou art indeed funny!”
