1. In a black-and-white sequence, Theresa lies bound on the ground with her eyes closed. She thinks, “OKAY. OKAY. STEP ONE. STAY CALM.”
2. Theresa raises her head and checks the campsite around her. “STEP TWO. MAKE SURE NO ONE’S WATCHING.”
3. Theresa lies still with all four limbs stretched toward the stakes. “STEP THREE. TEST MY BONDS.”
4. Theresa flexes one arm, and its stake comes free of the ground with a *POP!*
5. Theresa stares at the loose stake. “YOU GOTTA BE KIDDING ME...”
