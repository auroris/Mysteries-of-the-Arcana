1. Theresa remains bound spread-eagle with her eyes closed. She thinks, “Okay. Okay. Step one. Stay calm.”
2. Theresa raises her head and checks the campsite around her. “Step two. Make sure no one’s watching.”
3. Theresa pulls against all four stakes while lying spread-eagle. “Step three. Test my bonds.”
4. One foot slips free of its boot with a *POP!* The rope remains tied around the boot’s ankle.
5. Theresa stares at her boot. “You gotta be kidding me...”
