1. A BioHunter manipulator presses into Melody's glowing barrier as a sustained *KRRRRRR...* begins.
2. White cracks spread outward from the manipulator across the barrier.
3. The cracks multiply and widen under the continued pressure.
4. The barrier shatters around the manipulator with a *KRRRASH!!!*
5. Chrystalline narrates, “The BioHunters broke through Mother's barrier just as I finished the formula.” A jagged white tear divides the image below. On one side, a BioHunter clamps onto Melody's raised hand with a *SNAPT!!!* On the other, a second BioHunter grips Chrystalline's cloak while she lies beside the completed travel sigils.
