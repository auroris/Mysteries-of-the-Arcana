1. Mandrake leans toward a nervous five-armed candleholder and gestures with one hand.
2. In a close view of the candleholder's worried face, Mandrake projects her face, lips, the smiling wolf's face, a gesturing figure with a trailing wisp, a rain cloud, and a question mark.
3. Mandrake folds her arms, narrows her eyes, and projects a barred circle and an exclamation mark.
4. Mandrake hovers among the living objects as bright bursts appear around them. The visible marks include a small curled shape, musical notes, a thumbs-up hand, a knife, and an exclamation mark.
