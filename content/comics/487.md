1. Kietra speaks into her radio while holding a rifle. “Boss, we have a problem.”
2. She continues, “The dog guy. I winged him.”
3. Kietra closes one eye and looks through a green lens. “And boss?”
4. Over the radio clipped to Baron Sir’s waist, Kietra reports, “He didn’t come from the Royal Road.”
5. Baron Sir grabs Kludge by the collar and shouts, “You *lied* to me!”
