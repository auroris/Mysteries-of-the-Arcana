1. Members of the four crowned families dance, embrace, and reach toward one another. Melody narrates, “Four families. Individuals experiencing individuality for the first time. With individuality came...” Words around them read “Sanity,” “Laughter,” “Defeat,” “Hope,” “Euphoria,” “Wonder,” “Weariness,” “Hatred,” “Obsession,” “Sickness,” “Forgetting,” “Tempest,” “Indifference,” “Joy,” “Love,” “Health,” “Remembering,” and “Curiosity.”
2. The pale psyche figure watches the orange-haired emotion figure. “Longing.”
3. The pale psyche figure kisses the blue watery figure. “Passion.”
4. The shadowy psyche figure raises a glowing bow while the watery figure reaches upward. “Anger.”
