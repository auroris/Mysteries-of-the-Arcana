1. Chrystalline points at her father. “NO! YOU WAIT! WHILE YOU HID HERE, MOTHER WAS CAPTURED AND TORTURED! CIRCE INSISTED I SHOULD WAIT FOR YOU TO MOUNT A RESCUE BUT WELL, I DID NOT!”
2. She faces him closely. “HAD I WAITED... ...MOTHER WOULD BE *DEAD* INSTEAD OF SIMPLY MAIMED AND HALF-BLINDED.”
3. Folding her arms, Chrystalline says, “NOW, I KNOW YOU CAN OPEN THE DOOR, FATHER.”
4. Her father puts a hand on her shoulder. “CHRYSTALLINE...” She replies, “OPEN THE DOOR, PLEASE, FATHER!”
