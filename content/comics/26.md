1. Chrystalline reaches toward her mother while a BioHunter holds her cloak and the completed travel sigils crackle beneath her. She narrates, “Once a dreamwalk begins, it cannot be stopped.”
2. Chrystalline strains toward Melody as the glowing opening closes, but the BioHunter keeps its grip on her cloak. “That left me trapped with a killer robot...”
3. The dreamwalk completes, carrying Chrystalline and the BioHunter into another universe. Chrystalline strikes the ground with her fist. “...a universe away from my mother.”
