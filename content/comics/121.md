1. Chrystalline presses chewed gum onto her boot. “Stick the chewed gum on the bottom of your shoe.”
2. She raises her right foot. “Hop on your right foot once...”
3. She hops again. “Twice...”
4. She pushes off with her left foot. “Now push off with your left foot!”
5. Chrystalline sails across the minefield with a *WHOOOSH!*
6. Safely across, Chrystalline looks back. “Now, your...”
7. Theresa slams into the building’s corner in front of Chrystalline with a *WHAM!* Chrystalline finishes, “...turn.” Theresa says, “Ow.”
