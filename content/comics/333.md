1. Holding her pistol, Theresa says, “FOCUS THERESA. HE’LL BE FINE. GET THIS THING STARTED OR WE’RE BOTH DEAD.”
2. She pours liquid from the Vitamin X bottle onto the ground. “ADDED THE WATER. RIGHT. NEXT?”
3. Theresa connects a tangle of wires to a rusty plate reading “NEVER READY.” “THINK IT’LL WORK, THERESA?”
4. Climbing into the truck with the pistol and wires, she says, “IT’LL TAKE A MIRACLE.”
