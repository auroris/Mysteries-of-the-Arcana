1. Chrystalline and Theresa climb a steep wooden slope. Theresa asks, “What happens when someone breaks the prime directive?” Chrystalline replies, “There’s no way to officially sanction, but keys police their own.”
2. Chrystalline says, “Once, a dictator from your Arcana discovered he was a key. He used the Royal Road to smuggle in advanced technologies with which he destroyed his enemies.” Theresa asks, “Really? What happened?”
3. Theresa helps Chrystalline over the edge. Chrystalline answers, “Father, Mother and Kludge took to arms. The problem was dealt with. Permanently.”
4. Chrystalline raises her hands. “I believe we’ve arrived.”
5. Theresa raises her hands too. “Yeah. You’re right.”
6. An overhead view shows armed Red Viquirrels surrounding them. Theresa says, “Here we go again.”
