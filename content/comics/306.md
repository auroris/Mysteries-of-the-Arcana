1. William looks ahead. “We approach our destination.”
2. Theresa dismounts. “This place’s full of horrors?”
3. A sprawling factory stands beneath the moon, with smokestacks, pipes, and biohazard symbols. William says, “Aye. ’Tis a haven for them. Not even the Knights of the Broken Highway dare approach.”
4. Walking beside William, Theresa asks, “We’re supposed to just waltz in there and carry the stuff out?”
5. Theresa insists, “We need a better plan.”
