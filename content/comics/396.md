1. Chrystalline rests a hand on Theresa’s shoulder. “THERESA.”
2. Chrystalline clasps her hands. “THIS LAST WEEK, WORKING ON THE BEAST WITH YOU. AFTER ALL THE ANGER I FELT. AT MY FATHER. AT MYSELF. IT WAS JUST THE THERAPY I NEEDED. THANK YOU.”
3. Theresa rubs the back of her neck. “YEAH, WELL...”
4. Theresa smiles. “...YOU WEREN’T SO BAD TO HAVE AROUND YOURSELF.”
5. Chrystalline leads Theresa onward. “COME ON. WE DON’T WANNA BE LATE.”
6. Golden light illuminates Theresa’s startled face. “WOAH!”
