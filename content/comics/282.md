1. Theresa grips her ant’s reins with her eyes closed. “I REALLY DON’T WANT TO TALK ABOUT IT.”
2. Theresa and William ride in silhouette beneath a purple sunset. William says, “THEN SIMPLY LISTEN. I KNOW NOT WHAT HAPPENED UPON THE KNOWER, NOR IS IT MY PLACE TO JUDGE THY UNNATURAL RELATIONSHIP. HOWEVER... LONG HAVE I KNOWN CHRYSTALLINE. AS A FRIEND, LOVER, AND RIVAL. TRUE, SHE IS GIVEN TO IMPULSE, OFTEN ACTING WITHOUT THOUGHT AS TO FULL CONSEQUENCE. RARELY, THOUGH DOES SHE ACT WITH MALICE. I BELIEVE HER FEELINGS FOR THEE TO BE GENUINE. MISGUIDED... BUT GENUINE.”
3. William raises one hand as Theresa looks down. “SO, I ASK THEE THIS.”
4. In a close view, Theresa looks away. William asks from off panel, “IS LOVE SO ABUNDANT IN THY LIFE THAT THOU WOULDST REJECT IT WITHOUT THOUGHT OF FORGIVENESS?”
