1. Theresa, Melody, Chrystalline, Kludge, William, Circe, and Mandrake gather around a table. Theresa’s recorder plays Quest’s final word: “Help...”
2. Melody holds the recorder. “That’s all there is?” Kludge answers, “Theresa recorded it all, yeah. Quick thinking on her part.”
3. Melody mutters, “Figures. He’s even overly secretive about asking to be rescued.” Chrystalline protests, “Mother!”
4. Melody hugs Chrystalline. “I’m sorry, honey. I’m just worried.” At the side of the panel she tells Kludge, “I just figured the ‘baleful gaze’ was paranoia.” He replies, “I can’t blame you.”
