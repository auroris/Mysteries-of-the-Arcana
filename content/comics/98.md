1. Chrystalline stands with Theresa and William before the tent. “A safe haven created by the biologicals. The mushrooms defeat the radar somehow.”
2. William steps close and places a hand on Theresa’s shoulder. Chrystalline watches between them.
3. William puts an arm around Theresa. “Outstanding work... thou art a worthy rival for her affections, man-woman.”
4. William walks away as Theresa gestures furiously. “*I am not a man-woman!*” Chrystalline laughs behind one hand. “I think maybe he likes you.”
