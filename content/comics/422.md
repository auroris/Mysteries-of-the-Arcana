1. The Wanderer faces eight seated family rulers. Melody narrates, “The newcomers met with the four families, wanting help to create their own Arcana.”
2. Holding a dark orb, the Wanderer raises a question mark. “The newcomers asked.”
3. Two crowned family rulers look stern. “The four families refused.”
4. The Wanderer clasps its hands beneath a question mark and exclamation mark. “The newcomers pleaded. The four families refused.”
5. The Wanderer clenches both fists beneath an exclamation mark. “The newcomers threatened. The four families refused.”
