1. Theresa watches Blue depart as Chrystalline sits up in the bathtub behind her. “I must've been truly and well injured if Blue had to use its healing powers on me.”
2. Chrystalline rises naked from the tub while Theresa looks up in surprise. “You're alive!... And naked...”
3. Standing behind a blushing Theresa, Chrystalline covers herself with one hand. “Heh. We never exchanged introductions. My name is Chrystalline.” Theresa hunches forward. “I'm Theresa... Umm...”
4. As Chrystalline dresses, Theresa stands rigidly beside her. A dense field of Theresa's unanswered questions fills the background: “Where am I? What's going on? Why were you fighting a robot? How did you do that energy blast thing? What was that energy blast thing? What's up with the pointed ears? Is this outer space or something? Are you an alien? Because I get this isn't a dream. It's way too real to be a dream. And how did I get here and where did I pass out and right now everything seems so weird I feel like my head's going to explode from all the strange...”
