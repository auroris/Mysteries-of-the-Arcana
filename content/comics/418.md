1. A pair of immense eyes watches a small creature swim through bands of colored liquid. Melody narrates, “THE FOUR FAMILIES WATCHED, FASCINATED BY THE ENDLESS VARIETY OF LIFE THAT EVOLVED IN THEIR ARCANA.”
2. The small creature crawls from the liquid. “LIFE THAT GREW.”
3. It becomes a muscular humanoid holding a spear. “CHANGED.”
4. Sitting on a stump, the humanoid rests its chin in one hand. “THOUGHT.”
5. The humanoid sleeps beside the liquid. “DREAMED.”
