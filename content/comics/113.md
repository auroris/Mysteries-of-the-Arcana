1. Theresa, Chrystalline, and William lie prone behind a rock overlooking the distant facility. Theresa reaches for Chrystalline's binoculars. “This doesn’t make sense.”
2. Theresa scans the facility through the binoculars. “These things are pretty hi-tech. All I see is a single robot guard and a mounted camera.”
3. Theresa lowers the binoculars. “There’s got to be hidden defenses.”
4. Chrystalline smiles beside her. “Time for another lesson.”
