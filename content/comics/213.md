1. The sequence inside the green slime has ended. Theresa lies curled on the ground as white shattered lines radiate around her. Chrystalline calls from off panel, “Theresa? Where are you?”
2. A close view shows Theresa’s open eye with a thin blood-red tear at its corner. “Chrys?”
3. Theresa raises an arm over her face and curls tighter. “Chrys, no! Stay away!”
