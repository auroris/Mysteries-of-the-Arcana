1. Theresa and William face the narrow cracks glowing in the Wands pillar while William raises a hand toward them. Chrystalline observes from off panel, “’Tis a small crack...” Kludge replies from off panel, “A small crack in an engine block still means the car’s screwed.”
2. Kludge checks a handheld device. “I don’t know either way. This is unprecedented. Unfortunately, the only way to know for sure if the Royal Road works is for one of us to try it.”
3. Chrystalline rests a hand under her chin. “Even if no one here uses the Road, someone will eventually travel through it from the other...”
4. Chrystalline’s eyes widen. “Mandrake! Mandrake was out visiting friends! She should be home any...”
5. The group stands in silhouette before the Royal Road’s white, cracked red, blue, and green pillars. Purple energy forks upward beside the damaged pillar.
