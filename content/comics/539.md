1. LEGO Kludge gestures. “So, you know how you got here. By the Royal Road! But how did you activate it?”
2. A LEGO corridor marks a single location. Kludge explains, “You can only enter the Royal Road from one specific location in each Arcana. That location’s also the exit point. We call these nexus points.”
3. Beside an image of a smiling LEGO jester, Kludge continues, “So, you must have been at your Arcana’s nexus point. And while you were there your mind wandered to jesters or clowns or something like that. It happens more often than you’d think!”
4. A blue lightning-shaped portal tears open with a *KRASH!!* Kludge continues, “There was a crash of lightning...”
5. Kludge stands before the Royal Road crystals. “And you were transported across the Arcana to here!” The footer reads “Kludge’s Guide for New Keys, PT 4.”
