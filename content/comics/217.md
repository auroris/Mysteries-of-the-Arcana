1. Theresa examines the bite on her hand. “Ow! Son of a...”
2. The small turtle stands beside Theresa’s boot as Knower of Truths speaks through it. “Sands fall, Daughter of Eve. Your quest runs unfinished.”
3. Theresa crouches and points at the turtle. “Quest? This isn’t a quest! This is fricking torture!”
4. Theresa and the turtle stare at one another in silence.
5. Theresa throws up her hands. “How!? How do I save her?” Knower of Truths replies through the turtle, “You know.”
6. Theresa looks toward the forest while the turtle walks away. “When this is over I’m making turtle soup.”
