An open scrapbook page lies beneath a black pen and four photographs. Theresa narrates, “I will make you proud. I promise.” One photograph shows Colonel Poulter presenting Theresa with a folded flag. Another shows Theresa seated in her wheelchair, holding green fabric to her chest. A third shows Kludge and two armored figures working on the Surgeon. In the last, Theresa poses with Chrystalline, Melody, and William. Theresa finishes, “I love you, dad.”
