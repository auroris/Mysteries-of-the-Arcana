1. The group stands beside the open window while Chrystalline produces more items from her pendant. Theresa asks, “So how do we get down from here? Rope ladder?” Chrystalline answers, “Not at all.” In a circular inset, she produces a white feather from her pendant.
2. Chrystalline holds the feather up. “We use this!”
3. Theresa stares past the feather in disbelief. “A feather?”
4. In a close view, Chrystalline grins broadly. “Not just any feather. A Float Fall Feather.”
