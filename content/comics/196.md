1. Theresa’s eye hardens. “Fine.”
2. A memory overlays her: Theresa sits restrained in a chair as an unidentified person says, “Let’s see what your weakness is!”
3. Facing the featureless green pillar, Theresa refuses. “No!”
4. The pillar reshapes itself into a glossy green woman. Theresa cries, “NO!!!”
