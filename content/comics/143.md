A handheld device labeled “Kludge” displays “Notes on the Moon.” Kludge’s notes read:

“Just came back from the Moon. I’m not sure what to make of it. The only way I can think to describe it is that Tolkien, the Sunday comics page, and Gibson got together and had the weirdest baby ever.

“The dominant species appear to be fairies. Three apples high, wings they can fly with despite the aerodynamic impossibility of it all. They come in all the colors of the rainbows and talk not in words but in pictures which float over their heads in comic-like speech bubbles.

“What’s most disturbing? Despite the seemingly magic inhabitants, the society and technology level comes straight from a cyberpunk novel. Mega-corporations operating above the law, cybernetics, a world-wide computer network you can project your psyche into. 80s fashions gone wild, the works.

“Fairies with cybernetic body parts. I kid you not.”
