1. Chrystalline raises one finger while Mandrake sits behind her. A small gray alien in a red flowered shirt reaches toward her cloak. Chrystalline says, “We have what we need. Let us return home.”
2. The alien reaches beneath Chrystalline’s cloak.
3. The alien yanks Chrystalline’s pendant away with a *SWIPE!*
4. The alien slips away with the pendant while Chrystalline reacts with surprise.
5. Chrystalline turns while Mandrake leaps into the air. Chrystalline cries, “Our supplies! Circe’s items!” Mandrake speaks in picture symbols showing her own face, a pair of black pumps, and an exclamation mark.
