1. Chrystalline raises one finger while Mandrake sits behind her. A small gray alien in a red flowered shirt reaches toward her cloak. Chrystalline says, “WE HAVE WHAT WE NEED. LET US RETURN HOME.”
2. The alien reaches beneath Chrystalline’s cloak.
3. The alien yanks Chrystalline’s bag away with a *GYANK!!!*
4. The alien slips away with the bag while Chrystalline remains unaware.
5. Chrystalline turns while Mandrake leaps into the air. Chrystalline cries, “OUR SUPPLIES! CIRCE’S ITEMS!” Mandrake speaks in picture symbols showing her own face, a black bag, and an exclamation mark.
