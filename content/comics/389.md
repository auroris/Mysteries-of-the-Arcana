1. Mandrake broadcasts Theresa’s face, a dress, and an exclamation mark.
2. Theresa stands in the open doorway. “*SIGH* YEAH, I’M WEARING A DRESS. THAT A PROBLEM?”
3. Mandrake circles past the front of Theresa’s dress.
4. Mandrake completes the circle behind Theresa.
5. Mandrake broadcasts a thumbs-up and an exclamation mark. Theresa points through the open door. “I’M GLAD YOU LIKE IT. *OUT!*”
