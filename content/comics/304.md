1. Theresa explains, “IT TURNED THEM INTO THE *HORRORS.* PROBABLY NOT ALL AT ONCE BUT AFTER REPEATED DOSES. *COATED* THEM IN SOME SORT OF PROTECTIVE VARNISH. MADE THEM *FAST. STRONG.* LONG-LIVED AND TOTALLY *INSANE.* ANYONE WHO WASN’T CHANGED WAS PROBABLY *SLAUGHTERED.* POOR BASTARDS.”
2. She sticks out her tongue in disgust. “TOTAL YUPPIE ZOMBIE APOCALYPSE.”
3. Still riding behind William, Theresa asks, “WHAT ABOUT YOU GUYS? YOUR PEOPLE, I MEAN. THE *ANIMALS?*” William replies, “ALSO VITAMIN X. ’TWAS TESTED ON US AT FIRST AND LATER, IT SEEPED INTO THE *WATER SUPPLY.*”
4. Their mount carries them onward beneath the stars. Theresa says, “WOW. STRONG STUFF.”
