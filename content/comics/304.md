1. Theresa begins to understand. “It turned them into the horrors. Probably not all at once but after repeated doses. Coated them in some sort of protective varnish. Made them fast. Strong. Long-lived and totally insane. Anyone who wasn’t changed was probably slaughtered. Poor bastards.”
2. She sticks out her tongue in disgust. “Total yuppie zombie apocalypse.”
3. Still behind William, Theresa asks, “What about you guys? Your people, I mean. The animals?” William replies, “Also vitamin x. ’Twas tested on us at first and later, it seeped into the water supply.”
4. Their mount carries them onward beneath the stars. Theresa says, “Wow. Strong stuff.”
