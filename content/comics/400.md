1. A dark-skinned hand closes over a bloodied skeletal hand as the surrounding world breaks apart. Melody narrates, “In loneliness the Void shattered. Crumbled. Splintered. Tore.”
2. Rainbow-colored fragments spread across the clasped hands. “The pieces of the Void spun. Melted.”
3. Bright fragments radiate from a colorful center, forming many small silhouettes. “Reformed.”
