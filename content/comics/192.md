1. Theresa and Chrystalline walk across loose rocks at the top of the cliff. Theresa asks, “Why here?” Chrystalline answers, “The cloaks enjoy leaping from the ledge and floating down.”
2. Chrystalline sits on a log while Theresa stands beside her. Theresa asks, “So, to catch one of these things you have to sit here...” Chrystalline answers, “Yes.” Theresa continues, “...for hours...” Chrystalline says, “Yes.” Theresa asks, “...and do zilch?” Chrystalline replies, “Yes.”
3. Theresa looks aside. “That’s...umm... interesting and all but I think I’m gonna go explore a little.”
4. Chrystalline watches her go. “Certainly. Be careful!”
5. Chrystalline’s expression tightens. She thinks, “I’m sorry, Theresa...”
