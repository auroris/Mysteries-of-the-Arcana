1. Kietra stands guard with her pistol raised while Aleena crouches at the bomb beneath the red and green light of the crystals.
2. Aleena presses a glowing green component into the bomb with a *SQUELCH*.
3. Aleena stands beside the red crystal and clasps her hands. “Done. We need to go.”
4. Kietra raises a hand to her brow, her eyes wide. “Are we... is this the right thing to do?”
5. Aleena looks back at her uneasily. “We... we’re...”
6. Aleena shuts her eyes and frowns as an unidentified source makes a *SSSSHHH*. “We’re doing the job. Come on.”
