1. Theresa and Chrystalline leave Triple Signpost behind. “One of us? You mean a key.” Chrystalline replies, “Likely, yes.”
2. Theresa says, “And the sign thing can’t “see” whoever it is.” Chrystalline explains, “The Triple Signpost can see past, present, and future but only for beings from this Arcana.”
3. They follow two sets of tracks across the snow. Chrystalline says, “I find the idea disturbing. At the inn we have a rule. We who travel the Royal Road should avoid influencing local affairs as much as is possible. Uncle Kludge calls it the Primary Directive.” A small inset shows Theresa and Chrystalline in science-fiction uniforms. Theresa corrects her, “Prime Directive.” Chrystalline replies, “Whatever, nerd girl.”
