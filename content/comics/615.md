1. Chrystalline and Theresa cross a blasted landscape of craters, snow patches, and dead trees. Theresa says, “Well, the cease-fire Ead called for seems to be holding.” Chrystalline replies, “Assuming Mister Mist does not convince him to end it prematurely. We should hurry.”
2. A dark, blue-eyed figure watches from the edge of the panel as Theresa asks, “Shouldn’t we know this... guy? Thing? Whatever?” Chrystalline says, “Not all who travel through the Royal Road stop at the Inn but, yes, it is strange to find an aware key I’ve never seen before.”
3. Theresa says, “The jackass doesn’t seem to be buying into the prime directive.” Chrystalline answers, “No. He does not.”
