1. A puzzled Featherbeard lifts his hook to his beak with a *Klink*.
2. Featherbeard says, “I did not.” His shoulder companion adds, “Didn’t!”
3. Chrystalline tosses her hair. “Liar!” She makes a *HMPH!* and insists, “You clearly did!”
4. They glare at one another.
5. They lean across the railing, arguing. Featherbeard repeats, “Did not! Did not! Did not! Did not!” Chrystalline leans forward from the saucer cockpit repeating, “Did too! Did too! Did too! Did too!”
