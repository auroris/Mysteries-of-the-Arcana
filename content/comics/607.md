1. Chrystalline and Theresa sit beside Ead. Theresa asks, “The ‘outsider’?” Chrystalline replies, “I believe so.”
2. Chrystalline asks, “Ead, may we meet your new ‘friend’?” Ead answers, “Yes! Stay here. I’ll go get him.”
3. Theresa stops him. “Before you go, can I see one of your rifles? Doesn’t have to be loaded.”
4. Ead hands her a rifle. “Yes! Shoot some Reds if you see them!”
