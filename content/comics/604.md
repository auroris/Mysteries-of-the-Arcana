1. Grey Viquirrel soldiers escort Chrystalline and Theresa into a curtained room. Theresa says, “This better be a damned good plan.”
2. A soldier calls into the darkness, “Sir! We caught prisoners! Sir!”
3. Ead Enschild says, “Good work, soldier. Let’s line them up against the wall so we can shoot them.”
4. Chrystalline objects, “You’ll do no such thing, Ead Enschild!”
5. Ead’s cigar falls from his mouth. “Chrystalline?”
6. Chrystalline embraces Ead while Theresa asks, “What is it with her and guys with fur?”
