1. In their room, Theresa faces Chrystalline. A caption reads “EARLY CHRISTMAS MORNING.” Theresa says, “HEY... UH, I GOT YOU A GIFT.”
2. Theresa shyly holds out a large rectangular gift. “I HOPE YOU LIKE IT.”
3. Chrystalline raises a rectangular box of her own. “I GOT YOU SOMETHING TOO, ACTUALLY.”
4. Chrystalline holds the box close while Theresa stands behind her with her hands clasped. “IT TOOK A LITTLE BIT FOR ME TO FIND IT. I WANTED IT TO BE PERFECT.”
