1. A bolt of light strikes the floor between the open doors with a *KATHOOM*.
2. Now on Earth, William looks around. “Now...”
3. He reaches toward a pocket at his hip. “...I must see where it is that I have been summoned.”
4. William stands beside a row of machines on a city street while pedestrians pass in the distance.
5. He holds a handwritten note reading “Derrin’s pawn shop” and “555-8074,” followed by a partly cropped address line, “[illegible].” “A pawnbroker? I must wonder if he found it...”
