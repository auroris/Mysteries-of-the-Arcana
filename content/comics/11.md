1. A blurred view of an unfamiliar ceiling fills Theresa's vision as she wakes. “Weird dream.”
2. The wooden ceiling comes into focus. “Not my ceiling...”
3. Theresa lies beneath the covers of a pink bed. “Not my room!”
4. She sits up and looks around the unfamiliar room, thinking, “Where the hell am I?”
5. A white spacesuit stands beside a bathtub filled with luminous blue liquid. In an inset close view, Theresa recoils. “What the fuck!?”
