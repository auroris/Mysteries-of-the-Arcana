Six overlapping instant photographs form a collage. Theresa swings a stick while blindfolded beside a hanging piñata; William, wearing a party hat, offers her a cupcake with a lit candle; and Theresa sings into a microphone while holding Chrystalline's hand as Chrystalline raises a cupcake. Partly obscured karaoke lyrics behind them read “I'M DREAMING, PLEASE DON...,” “...AKE ME... DON'T WANNA SEE,” and “...HE SUN...AIN.” Mandrake lies amid a vast heap of brightly wrapped candy; Theresa regards a tall green birthday cake; and Theresa opens a large gift while Chrystalline and Kludge watch. There is no dialogue.
