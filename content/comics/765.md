1. The five companions advance single-file through complete darkness, visible only by William’s blue blade and the colored light around Chrystalline. From behind the others, Melody asks, “See anything?”
2. Kludge shades his eyes and peers into the dark. “Nothing, no sign of...”
3. Kludge lowers his hand as his eyes widen. “... There!”
4. The group approaches the enormous pink Wands pillar, now split by bright cracks. Kludge calls from off panel, “The explosion fractured the wands pillar!”
