1. Chrystalline pilots the small saucer while Mandrake hovers behind her. Chrystalline says, “FIRST, WE RETURN TO THE GALAXRIA. THEN WE FIND THAT LITTLE THIEF. THEN...” Mandrake speaks in picture symbols showing a fist, an upward arrow, and a film reel.
2. Chrystalline smiles at Mandrake. “EXACTLY!”
3. Chrystalline continues flying. “I HATE BEING STOLEN FROM.” Mandrake speaks with an eye picture symbol.
4. Chrystalline grins. “PAYBACK WILL BE SWEET.” Mandrake’s eye picture symbol gains an exclamation mark.
5. Mandrake swats Chrystalline’s head with a *WHAP!* as Chrystalline turns. Chrystalline cries, “IT WILL BE SLOW AND PAINF... OW! WHAT?!?”
6. A larger spacecraft closes in behind their saucer. Chrystalline says, “OH.”
