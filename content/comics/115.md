1. Chrystalline waits while William surveys the facility. “And?”
2. William points toward it. “Most troubling. Yonder camera and guardian, of course.”
3. William indicates the roof in a sketch of the facility. “Yonder roof. A laser grid detection system.”
4. William points to the approaches in the sketch. “Rear and starboard sides. Mines spread from five to fifteen feet out.”
5. Chrystalline and Theresa listen to William’s report. William continues, “Inside. Three BioHunters. A second guardian. Gun emplacements. A fine pesticide mist floats in the air.” Chrystalline says, “Challenging.”
6. Chrystalline smiles. “However, I came prepared. I’ve a plan.”
