1. The Wanderer strikes the Transformer’s skull with a bladed weapon. Melody narrates, “This war? More vicious.”
2. The Wanderer stands over the Transformer’s detached skull, which is impaled on a staff. “The results? The same.”
3. The defeated Transformer is pulled into a glowing opening while the Wanderer stands above. “The victors imprisoned the defeated.”
4. A lone silhouette crosses the battlefield. “And the war continued.”
