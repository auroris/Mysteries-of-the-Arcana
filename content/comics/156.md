1. Theresa stands beside The Surgeon. “We’ll grab dog boy and BbuzZ. Use that road thing.”
2. Chrystalline kneels beside Melody. “The Royal Road connects to but one specific location in each Arcana. We must exit where we entered.”
3. Theresa asks, “What about the glowy symbol thing you did in the tunnel?” Chrystalline answers, “I’ve... only just begun learning the art of dreamwalking. I know but a few formulas. None lead away from here.”
4. Theresa throws up her arms. “Fan. Freaking. Tastic. What do we do?” The Surgeon’s display reads “03:50.”
5. Chrystalline bows her head. “I don’t know.”
6. Theresa rubs her forehead. “Okay. Get your mom. I’ll grab the robot.”
7. Theresa looks determined. “We’ve got a plane to catch.”
