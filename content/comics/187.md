1. Theresa walks through the forest beside Chrystalline. “Wait. Are you saying you and your dad are the only people who have met Dreamers? Or that you’re the only Dreamers people have met?” Chrystalline smiles. “As my mother likes to say....”
2. A monochrome story panel contains several circular vignettes. Chrystalline narrates, 

“Once upon a time.... 

A baby appeared from nowhere, right on the Inn’s bar. For once, even Circe was surprised.” 

Circe holds the infant over the bar. “The baby had pointed ears. A sign of the legendary Dreamers.” 

Theresa interrupts, “Thought that meant you were an elf.” 

Chrystalline replies, “Telling a story here.” Theresa says, “Sorry.” 

Circe lifts the baby while tiny figures look on, then helps the young, pointed-eared child stand beside the bar. Chrystalline continues, “Circe raised the young Dreamer at the Inn, helped by the many adventurers who spent time there.”
