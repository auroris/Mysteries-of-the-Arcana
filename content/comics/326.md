1. William stands over the fallen skeleton with sword drawn.
2. Theresa lowers her smoking pistol. William says, “’TIS DEAD. EXCELLENT SHOT!”
3. Theresa replies, “NOW WHO ISN’T FUNNY? OKAY. I MADE A MISTAKE, SO LET’S DO THIS QUICK. SEE IF THERE’S ANY GOO IN THE BOX CAR.”
4. Walking away, Theresa says, “WHEN THE HORRORS COME AFTER US, WE WON’T BE ABLE TO OUTRUN THEM ON THE ANT, BUT MAYBE...”
