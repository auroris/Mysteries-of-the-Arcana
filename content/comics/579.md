1. Theresa sits hunched at the workbench with Chrystalline standing behind her. “You had little choice, Theresa,” Chrystalline says.
2. Theresa snaps, “*I know!*”
3. She struggles to articulate the rules her father taught her. “A gun is a tool, not a...” “You never point a gun at someone unless...” “One pull of a trigger can end someone’s...”
4. Theresa spins around and shouts at Chrystalline, “*Thou shalt not kill, okay?!*”
5. She buries her face in her hands. “Thou shalt not kill.”
