William kneels in meditation before the glowing Knight of Swords Arcana card. Chrystalline explains, “Each Key possesses a power beyond the ability to open the Road. These powers, often subtle, draw on the very essence of the Arcana themselves. William’s power can give him knowledge of the enemy.” Theresa asks, “Seriously? That’s his power?” At the lower right, small caricatures of Chrystalline and Theresa kneel together. Chrystalline answers, “Yes. What did you expect?” Theresa replies, “I dunno. He’s a dog. Super fire-hydrant marking?”
