1. Theresa and William ride side by side on giant ants. Theresa murmurs, “Mmm.” William answers, “Mmm.”
2. They ride along the road in silence.
3. Theresa glances toward William. “So.” William answers, “Aye.”
4. The two riders pass a ruined house.
5. Theresa scowls at William. “Screw this. Why did you hit me with the damn brick?”
