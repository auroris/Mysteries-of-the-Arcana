2. The saucer dives between several reaching limbs with a *WHOOSH!*
3. The saucer banks away from another tentacle with a *SHOOM*.
4. A tentacle reaches after the fleeing craft.
5. One tentacle strikes the saucer with a *CLANG!* and *WHOOSH!*, jolting the craft.
