1. Theresa walks along the ridge. “Why are we risking our necks for this stuff, anyway?”
2. She scratches her head. “I mean, what does Circe need with monster making goo?” William looks at his sword hilt. “’Tis my fault she requires it.”
3. Silhouettes trace William’s devolution from the upper right to the left, then downward and to the right. He narrates, “A short time after coming to the Inn, I... ...*changed.*” William begins standing at full height as himself, drops to all fours, becomes progressively more dog-like, and finally reverts to an ordinary dog drinking from a water dish as Circe pours in Vitamin X. “’Twas reasoned I required small but regular doses of Vitamin X.”
