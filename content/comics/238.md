1. Kludge and Melody read their list at the table. Kludge reads, “... ESSENCE OF PASSION.” Melody protests, “BUT THAT CAN ONLY BE FOUND IN THE...” Kludge continues, “... STATELY PLEASURE-DOME.” Melody snaps, “TENDER!”
2. Chrystalline reads another list while Mandrake sits on her shoulder. “WE CAN PURCHASE ALL THIS AT THE GALAXRIA IN THE FOUR OF COINS.” Mandrake answers in symbols: a smiling face, a blue curled shape, and an exclamation mark.
3. Theresa and William examine their list. William says, “GOAT-FROGS ART NATIVE TO TEMPERANCE.” Theresa asks, “AND AFTER THAT? THE VITAMIN XTREME?” William answers, “MY HOME ARCANA.”
