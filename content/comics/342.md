1. Her father paces. “DURING MY LAST FEW DREAMWALKS I... FELT SOMETHING. LIKE A MALEVOLENT FORCE SEARCHING FOR ME.” Chrystalline asks, “ARE YOU SURE?”
2. He replies, “DREAMWALKING IS UNIQUE TO THE DREAMERS. OF THOSE WE KNOW, ONLY YOU AND I CAN USE THE SIGILS TO TRAVERSE THE ARCANA. NEITHER KLUDGE NOR YOUR MOTHER WERE ABLE TO LEARN THE ART. EVEN CIRCE ADMITS DREAMWALKING LIES BEYOND HER ABILITIES.”
3. With his back to her, he says, “YOU AND I ARE THE LAST DREAMERS. NO ONE KNOWS WHAT LED TO THE DISAPPEARANCE OF THE OTHERS.” Chrystalline asks, “ARE YOU SAYING...?”
4. He answers, “YES. IT MAY BE DREAMWALKING IS WHAT LED TO THEIR DESTRUCTION.”
