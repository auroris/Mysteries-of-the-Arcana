1. Theresa and William walk past a ruined wooden building. “NO OFFENSE BUT YOUR HOME SU...” William interrupts, “HOLD ON.”
2. William stops Theresa with an armored hand. “SOMETHING’S COMING.”
3. Theresa looks down the road through binoculars. “LOOKS LIKE RIDERS...”
4. Through the binoculars, six armored figures ride giant ants. Theresa says, “YEP. SIX OF ’EM WEARING ARMOR LIKE YOURS AND RIDING FREAKING ANTS!”
5. Behind Theresa, William raises a brick over her head. Theresa asks, “FRIENDS OF YOURS, DOGBOY?”
