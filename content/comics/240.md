1. Theresa and William walk past a ruined wooden building. “No offense but your home su...” William interrupts, “Hold on.”
2. Theresa reaches into her green overshirt for a monocular. William says, “Something’s coming.”
3. Theresa looks down the road through the monocular. “Looks like riders...”
4. Through the monocular, six armored figures ride giant ants. Theresa says, “Yep. Six of ’em wearing armor like yours and riding freaking ants!”
5. Behind Theresa, William raises a brick over her head. Theresa asks, “Friends of yours, dogboy?”
