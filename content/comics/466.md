1. William points. “I dislike her.”
2. Theresa leans over the truck’s hood. “Who, Ruby? She seems okay. A little chatty, maybe.”
3. William grimaces. “Her voice doth cause me discomfort.” Theresa asks, “Really?”
4. Chrystalline hangs through the cab window. “Me as well.”
5. Chrystalline raises one hand. “William and I both hear at higher frequencies than you, Theresa.”
6. Chrystalline continues, “Ruby might well be a good person but her voice...” William finishes, “...Grates on the nerves like auditory poisoned ivy.”
