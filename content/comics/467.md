1. William strikes the truck with a *WHUNK*. “In truth, she doth ask many a question.”
2. Theresa carries a mug. “So did I when I first got here. Hell, I still do.” She points toward the floor. “Don’t leave that on my floor.”
3. Chrystalline puts an arm around Theresa. “You enjoy having someone about who knows even less than you do.”
4. Theresa looks back as William bends toward the floor. “It’s nice not being the new...”
5. The room goes black amid a *CLICK*, *CRASH*, *CLANG*, and *BANG*.
6. In the darkness, one voice says, “What the fuck?” Another cries, “Ow!”
