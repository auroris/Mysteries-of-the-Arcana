1. Mandrake appears behind Kietra, broadcasting a pictogram of herself popping from a purple box with an exclamation mark.
2. Kietra looks over her shoulder and screams, “GAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAH!!!”
3. Mandrake braces and hauls on the rope tied to Kietra.
4. Mandrake pulls hard with a *YANK*.
5. Kietra drops into the darkness with a *FWUMP*.
6. Mandrake waves, broadcasting a smiling face, a four-leaf clover, and an exclamation mark.
