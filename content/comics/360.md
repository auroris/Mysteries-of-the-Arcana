1. Chrystalline and Theresa speak over one another. Chrystalline says, “I have something to speak with you about.” Theresa says, “Look, we need to talk. I’ve been thinking.” Chrystalline says, “I have given it much thought.” Theresa offers, “You go first.” Chrystalline replies, “Please, after you.” Theresa insists, “No. I insist. Go ahead.” Chrystalline snaps, “Spitfire!” Theresa answers, “For fuck’s sake.”
2. Chrystalline rubs her forehead. “May I go first, please?” Theresa replies, “Heh. Yeah. Go ahead.”
3. Chrystalline says, “I owe you an apology. Quite a large one, actually.”
