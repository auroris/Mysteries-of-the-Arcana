1. The tangled new creatures tumble past a mass of green limbs. One cries, “Ha-cha!” Melody narrates, “Death.”
2. A black birdlike creature jerks its head up beneath an exclamation mark.
3. A crocodilian creature stares beneath an exclamation mark.
4. An octopus-like creature looks toward a small blue-and-yellow being on one tentacle; an exclamation mark hangs above them.
5. The creatures melt and decay. “The blood of a member of the four families could birth life. Birth reality!”
6. The blue-and-yellow creature clings to the slumped octopus-like being. “But their creations could not last.”
7. The last bodies dissolve into black stains. “They wasted away. From birth to non-existence in mere moments.”
