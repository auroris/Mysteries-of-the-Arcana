1. A story montage shows Chrystalline’s father and his companions struggling against a mound of fallen rock. Chrystalline narrates, “During one adventure, the mouth of a cavern they were exploring collapsed.” The elf and the red-haired woman shout at one another. “Tempers flared and a moment of anger...” They embrace and kiss. “...transformed into a moment of passion.”
2. Chrystalline and Theresa walk through the forest. “They escaped, of course. And nine months later, I was born.”
3. Theresa puts a hand on Chrystalline’s shoulder. “Your folks don’t get along?” Chrystalline replies, “Like fire and high explosives.”
