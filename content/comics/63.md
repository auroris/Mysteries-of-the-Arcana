1. Theresa’s bullet strikes the Prophet’s rotor and throws sparks.
2. The damaged Prophet crashes at the group’s feet with a *THWUNK*. Chrystalline covers her mouth while Theresa and William stare down at it.
3. William bares his teeth and points at Theresa. “Idiot! ’Twas only a prophet! Harmless!”
4. Chrystalline folds her arms and explains, “Individual aspects of the Machine Intelligence specialize, performing only their function. Nothing more.”
5. Chrystalline continues as Theresa looks back at the wreck. “Prophets fly about spouting propaganda. The Intelligence takes interest only when one needs repair.” Theresa says, “So...”
6. In a close view, Theresa’s eyes widen. “It will know someone destroyed the prophet. It will know we are here.”
