A handheld device labeled “Kludge” displays “Notes on the H.Man.” Kludge’s notes read:

“On the surface, the Hanged Man seems like a world fairly reminiscent of medieval Europe, though with a stronger middle class and no actual government to speak of. Decisions are made by the consensus of the citizenship.

“Everyone goes to sleep promptly at sunset. Everyone. At that point residents are somehow transported from what they call the ‘Here and Now’ to the ‘Once Upon a Time’.

“The Once Upon a Time seems to be not so much a separate reality as a series of shared story dreams played out over and over again with the people in the Hanged Man taking on the various rolls. What’s odd is all the stories are fairy tales from my world. I have to wonder if the Grimm Brothers didn’t pay this arcana a visit.

“I just asked Melody what roll she took on in the Once Upon a Time, since the Hanged Man is her birth arcana. Now I’m going to go ask Circe for a steak for my black eye. Obviously a touchy subject.”
