1. Theresa and Chrystalline wait together at the table. “Wait,” Theresa says. Chrystalline starts, “So...”
2. “Quiet!” Theresa orders. Chrystalline tries, “But...”
3. “Sssshh!” Theresa insists. “Theresa!” Chrystalline protests.
4. They wait in silence.
5. Theresa says, “Okay. They’re gone. Let’s go.”
6. “Where?” Chrystalline asks. Theresa replies, “Wherever you’re dragging us to next.”
