1. Chrystalline and Mandrake walk through the metal corridor.
2. They approach another sealed door.
3. Chrystalline raises a finger and smiles. “Shall we leave this wretched place? Here is my...”
4. Her face crumples and she covers her mouth. “...I mean...”
5. Chrystalline asks Mandrake, “Do you have any ideas for how we might escape?”
