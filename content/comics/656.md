1. Chrystalline studies the pink flower. Theresa says, “Huh.” Chrystalline explains, “It opened for me. Keyed to the DNA in my breath? Or my voice, perhaps.”
2. Theresa asks, “How come I can’t read this?” Chrystalline points to text covering the petals. “There are ways around the Inn’s translation enchantment. This is a code not a language. My father taught it to me.”
3. Theresa examines her mechanical glove. “Okay. So what’s it say?”
4. Chrystalline replies, “There’s a great deal of information here.”
