A handheld device labeled “Kludge” displays “Notes on the Ace of C.” Kludge’s notes read:

“Solids don’t exist in the Ace of Cups. Let me rephrase that. Solids can’t exist in the Ace of Cups. If you travel there you turn into a sort of viscious goo that travels around a world made up of various liquids, slimes, and oozes. It is a most curious sensation.

“Apparently, when Blue first left the Ace of Cups (in the company of Quest, Chrys’s father) he felt a similar sensation. Being a liquid being in a world built to accommodate solids left him quite confused as to how to behave. That’s why he spends most of his time in that suit. It allows him to remain liquid but interact with the solid world as a solid.

“Perhaps even more interesting than what happens to your body on the Ace of Cups is what happens to your emotions. They, too, become a liquid - if you get angry - for example, your anger turns into blobs of hot, red liquid. The angrier you are the hotter the liquid. Eventually, as your temper dies down the anger blobs sort of evaporate unless absorbed.

“Communication between the sentients of the Ace of Cups is done by emotion exchange. They have an entirely empathic language. If someone is happy a pinkish blob breaks off and is absorbed by someone else present to share and spread the emotion.”
