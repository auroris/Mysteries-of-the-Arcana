1. At a table in the Inn, Chrystalline introduces Theresa to Kludge. “Uncle Kludge, Theresa. Theresa, Uncle Kludge. He can sort out the basics for you.”
2. Chrystalline hurries away, her cloak trailing behind her. “Must run now ta!” Kludge slides a blue mug across the table to Theresa. “Have some cocoa moo. I think you need it more than I do.”
3. Theresa and Kludge appear in silhouette before a row of Arcana cards. Kludge explains, “Let me assure you. You aren't dreaming or crazy. You've stepped into a whole new world. Literally. See, there's more than one universe. In fact, there are at least 78.” Theresa asks, “Like in comic books? Alternate dimensions?” Kludge continues, “No. A dimension is just a measurement used to judge an object's position in spacetime. These are entire universes with their own dimensions. Length, width, etcetera. They also aren't alternate. You won't meet any ‘what if?’ versions...” In a circular inset, Kludge looks toward her. “Why are you staring at me like that?”
4. Theresa and Kludge face each other across the table. “Just curious. What did you do back home?” Kludge clasps his hands. “I was a physics professor.”
5. Theresa smiles into her blue mug. “Thought so.”
