1. Chrystalline gestures as she walks through the jungle with Theresa. “I grew up in the Inn. As part of my education, I traveled the Arcana with either mother or father.”
2. A green blade activates in Chrystalline’s hand with a *ZNNNNNNN.* “Occasionally, they enrolled me in a boarding school or similar institution. It rarely worked out.”
3. Chrystalline holds the blade across her shoulders. “Were it not for Mandrake and William, I would have no friends my own age.”
4. Chrystalline swings the blade through jungle growth with a *SHOKK!*
5. Theresa reaches for the blade. “Give me that!” She snatches it with a *SWIPE!*
6. Theresa chops through the growth with a *THWACK!* “You hack like a blonde.” Chrystalline folds her arms and watches.
