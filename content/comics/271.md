1. Chrystalline pulls herself from the wreck while Mandrake hovers nearby. Chrystalline says, “When we catch that thief, torture shall ensue.” Mandrake speaks in picture symbols showing a gray alien plus a strapped chair equaling a smiling face.
2. Chrystalline and Mandrake run toward a low domed building.
3. A large sign identifies “Uncle Mork’s trading post and fine used spacecraft” above the building.
4. Inside, shelves hold rows of colorful figurines while an obese grey alien waits behind the counter.
