1. Theresa watches the Surgeon. “[REVELATION] This unit differs from other surgeon units.”
2. The Surgeon’s display reads “00:57.” “[FACT] This unit desires experience beyond its designated function.” “[FACT] This unit desires to survive despite being flagged for termination.”
3. Theresa looks down with a guarded expression.
4. Her expression softens as she looks upward.
5. Theresa starts to reply. “Yeah, well, keep cutting people up and you’ll...” She spots something below. “The tree!”
6. A lone tree comes into view through the green haze below the Deliverer. Theresa calls from off panel, “That’s the drop zone!”
