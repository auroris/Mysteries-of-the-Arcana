1. Young Chrystalline climbs a stack of wooden barrels. Mandrake broadcasts a pattern of smiling faces, suns, and three exclamation marks from off panel.
2. Mandrake sits behind a barrel and broadcasts a red prohibition symbol.
3. Young Chrystalline pauses against the barrels as a pale hand taps her shoulder.
4. Circe offers Chrystalline a wrapped candy bar.
5. Chrystalline reaches the candy bar toward Mandrake, who peeks from behind a barrel on the shelf.
