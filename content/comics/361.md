Chrystalline stands with her arms folded against a black background while images of her father, the Arcana, and Theresa’s ordeal appear beside her. She explains:

“WHILE MANDRAKE AND I WERE OUT ON MISSION, I... RAN INTO MY FATHER, I SUPPOSE YOU CAN SAY. HE HAS BECOME CONVINCED THAT SOME HORRIBLE, NEBULOUS THING IS OUT THERE, SEEKING THE FINAL DESTRUCTION OF DREAMERS. FATHER WAS IN HIDING. WANTED ME TO JOIN HIM. HE DEVISED THIS PLAN TO USE THOSE WHO TRAVEL THE ROYAL ROAD AS BAIT. MOTHER. YOU. EVERYONE.

“LISTENING TO HIM, I REALIZED, I WAS JUST LIKE HIM. DOING WHAT I WANTED WITHOUT A THOUGHT FOR ANYONE ELSE. ASSUMING I KNEW BEST. I SHOULD NOT HAVE TAKEN YOU TO THE ACE OF CUPS AND FORCED YOU TO CONFRONT YOUR PAST. I TOLD MYSELF AT THE TIME THAT I WAS DOING SO FOR YOU BUT THAT IS UNTRUE. I DID IT FOR MYSELF BECAUSE I WAS IMPATIENT. I WANTED YOUR FULL ATTENTION SOONER, RATHER THAN LATER.

“IT WAS WRONG OF ME AND I AM SO SORRY. PLEASE, FORGIVE ME. PLEASE.”
