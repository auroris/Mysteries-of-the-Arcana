1. The bare feet of several white-clad alien guards crowd through a metal passage.
2. One guard peers around a red-lit corner, startled.
3. More guards press into the passage behind the first.
4. Reflected in a guard's dark eye, Kietra and Aleena raise their pistols.
5. Their shots strike the guards with a *KZZAT* and a *SPLOOT*.
6. From behind the guards' weapons, Kietra and Aleena stand together at the far end of the red corridor, pistols trained forward.
