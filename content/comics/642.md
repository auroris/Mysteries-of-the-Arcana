2. Mister Mist says, “It’s ironic, really. All that hard work. Erasing the acorn trees. Inciting the clans to war.” Mister Eerie adds, “Getting them to dig trench after trench, hoping they would stumble across your daddy’s treasure trove.”
3. Theresa asks, “You taught the Viquirrels the ugliest form of warfare in history as part of a treasure hunt?”
4. Mister Eerie snaps, “Don’t interrupt, *mutt!*”
5. Mister Mist asks, “Now, where was I?” Mister Eerie answers, “Oh, yes. Irony.”
