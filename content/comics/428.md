1. A crowned fiery family ruler lies dismembered beside streams of lava. Melody narrates, “The four families and newcomers alike were immortal.”
2. His severed limbs rejoin his body. “When cut down, they always rose again.”
3. The restored ruler rises with his fists clenched. “It seemed the war would never end.”
4. A winged, horned newcomer watches streams of light cross a green landscape. “Until the newcomers discovered a solution.”
