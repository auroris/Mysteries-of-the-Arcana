1. The group crouches behind a rock overlooking the facility. Theresa holds the green acid gun, its canister filled with green goo. Chrystalline asks, “Ready?” Theresa answers, “Ready.” William says, “Aye.”
2. Theresa rises behind the rock and aims the green-goo gun while her 9mm remains holstered. Chrystalline calls from off panel, “Go!”
3. Theresa fires with a *PAKT!*
4. The projectile streaks toward the facility’s mounted camera with a *WOOOSH!*
5. It bursts with a *SPLAT!* and covers the camera in green goo.
