1. Chrystalline approaches Theresa. “Are you alright?” Theresa checks her nostrils for a nosebleed. “Just... give me the acid gun the BbuzZ loaned us.”
2. Theresa produces the acid gun from her pendant with a *ZNNNNN!* A separate *BOOM!* comes from William’s fight with the robots. “William has engaged the enemy.”
3. William grapples with a large red robot while black silhouettes of additional robot appendages frame them. Chrystalline narrates, “With the camera obscured by paint, the robots should only know of William’s presence. That leaves us free to infiltrate the compound unnoticed.”
4. Theresa fires the acid gun with a *PSHHHHH!*, dissolving a circular cut through the facility wall. “You’re telling me the plan I already know why?” Chrystalline replies, “To reassure myself. I can’t fail my mother, Theresa.”
5. The circular section of wall falls inward with a *WHUMP!* Theresa and Chrystalline stand in the new opening. Chrystalline says, “I just can’t.”
