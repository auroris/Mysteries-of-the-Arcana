In a wood-paneled room, Theresa studies an elaborate brass timekeeping device on a cabinet. Its exposed gears, numbered rings, hoops, pointers, and suspended spheres rise above two illuminated circular dials. She rests one hand on her hip and curls the other near her mouth in concentration. There is no dialogue.
