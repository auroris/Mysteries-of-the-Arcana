1. Chrystalline points above Theresa’s head. Theresa says, “Huh?”
2. Theresa looks up. “Oh!”
3. The glowing, three-dimensional word “Grow” appears in the air.
4. “Grow” blurs downward toward Theresa.
5. It passes into her body through a glowing ring.
6. Theresa’s boot strikes the ground with a *THUD*.
