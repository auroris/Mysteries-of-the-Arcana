1. William taps the brick against the back of Theresa’s head with a *bap*.
2. Theresa rubs the back of her head. “OW!”
3. Theresa turns on William. “WHAT THE HELL?” William replies, “APOLOGIES. THY HEAD TIS THICKER THAN I IMAGINED”
4. William puts one hand on Theresa’s shoulder and raises the brick again. Theresa struggles against him. “WHAT? WAIT! WHAT ARE YOU D...”
5. Against blackness, the brick strikes with a *BAP!!!*
