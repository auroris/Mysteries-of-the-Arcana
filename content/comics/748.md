1. In the reflection of Chrystalline's oval mirror, Theresa lies curled asleep on the bed in lingerie and grumbles, “*>Grumble<*”
2. Chrystalline turns toward the bed in surprise. “Oh.”
3. In a small inset, Chrystalline presses a wall control with a *CLICK*. An overhead view reveals Theresa asleep on the turquoise mattress beneath several translucent reading panes while Chrystalline stands at the foot of the bed.
4. Chrystalline sits beside Theresa and touches her shoulder. “Wake up, birthday girl.”
5. Theresa pushes herself up on one elbow. “Where've you been?” Chrystalline leans close. “In your room. It seems we both had the same idea.”
