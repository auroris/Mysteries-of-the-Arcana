1. Theresa leans back against the pew and raises one hand. “No! Yes! I mean... yes. I have a place to stay.” The priest stands beside her. “Good. May I join you?”
2. As the priest moves to sit beside her, Theresa grips her crutch. “Sure. I mean, yes. Please, do. Thank you.”
3. The priest sits quietly beside Theresa and looks ahead.
4. Seated together in the pew, the priest turns toward her. “If you don’t mind my asking, what’s on your mind?” Theresa answers, “Girls kissing other girls.”
5. Theresa covers her face with one hand. “Pendego!”
6. Theresa lowers her hand and looks away. “I mean, what do you think about it?” The priest repeats, “Girls kissing other girls?”
