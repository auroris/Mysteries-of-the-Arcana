1. A caption reads “NOW.” A white goat-frog with curled horns rests against a tree, purring *PURRRRUP PURRRRUP*.
2. In a close view, the goat-frog’s yellow eye widens as its purr changes to *PURRRRRR~UPP!?*
3. The goat-frog explodes with a *KA-FWOOM!*
