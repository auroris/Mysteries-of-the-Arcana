1. Theresa follows Chrystalline up a wooden staircase. “Earlier, you told me you should be dead by now. What did you... William!”
2. William blocks the stairs while Theresa peers over Chrystalline's shoulder. He quotes her promise: “‘I promise I will not attempt to rescue my mother without assistance.’” He continues, “Didst thou truly think either Circe or myself would be so addlepated as to believe such tripe?”
3. William folds his arms and lectures Chrystalline. “’Tis time thou placed these childish fancies in the toy box, where they belong. Thy mother's captors art deadly. To battle them ’tis not the work of women! Even if thou werest male, thou would not be properly trained for such a task. ’Tis doubly so for your inexperienced companion, despite her mannish manner. Return to thy room. I've no wish to see thine end.” In an inset, Theresa glares. “He's not a jack-al. He's a jack-ass.” Chrystalline answers dryly, “Agreed.”
