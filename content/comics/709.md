1. Mandrake looks back over her shoulder as she flies through the dark woods.
2. A wolf swipes at the edge of one wing, leaving bright contact and slash marks.
3. In a close view, the wolf glares over its nose and bared teeth.
4. Mandrake looks up at a wolf poised above her in the grass.
5. Tiny at the center of the flowered meadow, Mandrake is surrounded by a pack of snarling wolves.
