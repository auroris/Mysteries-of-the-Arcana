1. Mandrake moves through a rubble-strewn alley as a tied, red-glowing sack follows behind her with a *BUZZZZ*.
2. A large black boot lands in a puddle with a *SPLASH*. Mandrake broadcasts a raised palm, a mask-like face, and an exclamation mark.
3. Mandrake flies away, pulling the glowing sack behind her.
4. Mandrake stops before a dark figure and broadcasts an exclamation mark.
5. A horned attacker grins and aims a mechanical weapon at Mandrake. She raises one hand and broadcasts a raised palm, a mask-like face, and an exclamation mark.
