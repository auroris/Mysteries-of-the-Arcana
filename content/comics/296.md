1. Chrystalline grips the saucer’s controls while Mandrake watches the pursuing ship. Chrystalline says, “INFORM ME IF THEY BRING WEAPONS ONLINE.”
2. Hatches open across the front of the larger ship.
3. A mass of segmented mechanical tentacles emerges from the hatches.
4. Mandrake speaks in picture symbols showing writhing tentacles and an exclamation mark. Chrystalline looks back. “YOU’RE KIDDING...” In a chibi inset, she throws up her arms. “TENTACLES?!? DO I LOOK LIKE A BIG-EYED ANIME GIRL?”
