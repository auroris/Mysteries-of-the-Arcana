1. A boot passes through a glowing grid as the lights return. Theresa says from off panel, “It could be nothing.”
2. Crouching beside her pistol and pendant, Theresa continues, “It was just a blackout, right? Maybe the explosion we heard was just a blown transformer or something.”
3. Chrystalline replies, “Except the power has returned, Theresa.” Theresa suggests, “Backup generator?”
4. William looks at his claws. “Nay. ’Tis danger. I feel it in my very bones.”
