1. Kludge points past Theresa toward a featureless white boundary. “That is the Void.”
2. He puts a hand on Theresa’s shoulder and stops her at its edge. “You almost walked straight into it.”
3. Theresa stares up at the white boundary. “What is it?”
4. Kludge kneels and examines his equipment. “Honestly? We don’t know.”
