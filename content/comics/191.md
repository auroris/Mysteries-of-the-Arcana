1. Chrystalline walks behind Theresa. “Mother and father have scoured the Arcana, searching for Dreamer lore.” Theresa chops through the jungle with a *THWACK!*
2. Theresa rests against the blade. Chrystalline continues, “Except for father’s reconstruction of the Dreamwalking technique, they’ve had little success.”
3. Chrystalline clasps her hands. “Even that was... We’re here!” Theresa looks up. “Huh?”
4. At the edge of a rocky chasm overlooking the ocean, Chrystalline points to the opposite cliff. “The spot where I shall tame my new cloak!” Theresa rubs her aching shoulder. The small turtle crawls down the cliff, and a fin rises from the water below.
