1. Chrystalline points at the ground. “She was standing there.” William kneels to examine it. “I shall track. Go ensure thy... friend summons no further trouble.” Theresa searches the horizon behind them.
2. Chrystalline rests a hand on Theresa’s back from behind. “Are you well? You’ve not spoken in a while.” Theresa replies, “Haven’t much felt like talking.”
3. Theresa looks away while Chrystalline follows. “Perhaps if I explained?” In an inset, a small caricature of Theresa covers her face as she thinks, “Now she wants to explain!”
