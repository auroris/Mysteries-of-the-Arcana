1. Theresa shades her eyes while standing among dead trees and black mountains. “This place seems so lifeless. Creepy.” Chrystalline and William confer behind her.
2. Chrystalline explains the terrain to Theresa as they walk. “Uninhabitable land equals fewer biologicals. The Machine Intelligence uses the environment itself as a weapon.”
3. William asks, “Where?” Chrystalline points ahead. “Just over a click in that direction. Let us go.” Theresa watches beside them.
