3. Theresa shades her eyes. “HMM? NEVERMIN... WAIT. PEOPLE UP AHEAD.”
4. A smiling woman and man in tennis clothes run toward them carrying rackets. William says from off panel, “NOT PEOPLE. *HORRORS.*”
