1. Mandrake stands with her hands on her hips beside the chair and candleholder. Two bursts contain “...” while a third contains a small bundled shape, a neutral face, and an exclamation mark.
2. Mandrake looks aside and projects the candleholder, a barred circle, and a question mark.
3. The candleholder looks worried beneath a glowing equation: a dark round symbol crossed by a slash, an equals sign, a spiderweb, another equals sign, and a tombstone marked “R.I.P.”
4. Mandrake raises one finger and smiles as she projects a light bulb and an exclamation mark.
5. In silhouette, the living range and candleholder move toward an intense circular portal. Mandrake and the chair are silhouetted within the light while the wolf-man is flung away to the right.
