1. Theresa and Chrystalline pass a sealed metal door. Theresa gestures as she asks, “I’VE BEEN MEANING TO ASK: HOW COME EVERYONE, WELL ALMOST EVERYONE, SPEAKS ENGLISH? BUT...” Chrystalline answers, “HARDLY ANYONE DOES, ACTUALLY.”
2. Colored crystals hover above a dotted magic circle. Chrystalline explains, “AN ARTIFACT IN THE INN PLACES A LONG-TERM TRANSLATION ENCHANTMENT ON ANYONE WHO ENTERS. THE ENCHANTMENT TRANSLATES COMMUNICATION INTO A FORM YOU CAN UNDERSTAND.” Theresa asks, “WHY DOESN’T IT WORK ON MANDRAKE?”
3. Chrystalline explains as they walk through a dark hall, “IT DOES. THE PICTOGRAMS YOU SEE ARE NOT THE ONES MANDRAKE ACTUALLY BROADCASTS. THE ENCHANTMENT TRANSLATES THEM INTO SYMBOLISM YOU CAN UNDERSTAND.”
