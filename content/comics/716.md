1. The wolf-man stops beside a burning wall torch with Mandrake clenched in his fist. An exclamation mark appears above the torch while Mandrake projects musical notes, a barred circle, and a final small symbol.
2. He faces a heavy wooden door blocked by a crossbar. Two exclamation marks flare above him as Mandrake repeats her symbol sequence.
3. The wolf-man lifts the crossbar away with one hand. Three exclamation marks appear while Mandrake's sequence remains below.
4. He opens the door and gestures into the black room beyond. Mandrake projects her face, a round crossed object, an hourglass, lips, and the smiling wolf's face.
5. Mandrake flies through the doorway and shuts the door behind her with a *SLAM*.
6. In the darkness, several pairs of white eyes surround Mandrake's small body and translucent wings.
