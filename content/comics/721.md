1. Mandrake looks uncertain and projects three question marks.
2. The candleholder scowls and flexes four waxy arms. A glowing sequence above it shows the smiling wolf's face, an angry face, and an exclamation mark.
3. The large range narrows its eyes and opens a door full of flame. Above it appear the smiling wolf's face, the wolf wreathed in fire, and an exclamation mark.
4. The chair's back curls into an angry face. Above it appear the smiling wolf's face, a tangled scrawl, and an exclamation mark.
5. Mandrake leans backward in midair among the surrounding objects and projects a barred circle followed by the smiling wolf's face.
