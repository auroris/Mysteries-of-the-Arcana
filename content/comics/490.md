1. Theresa examines William’s shoulder. “The bullet just grazed you but you should...” William says, “Nay. We’ve a problem that must be solved.”
2. Chrystalline interrupts, “Callate, dogboy.” William says, “Two problems, as I see it. ’Tis true. Our home doth remain invaded and yon sniper...”
3. Mandrake flies nearby as William continues, “...Could shoot anyone coming through the Royal Road.”
4. Chrystalline holds up her pendant. “Yes. Mother could return home at any time.”
