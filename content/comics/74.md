1. Theresa sits in an orange-walled tunnel and pulls vines from her legs. “Ow.”
2. Theresa stands and rubs her backside, pistol in her other hand. “That’s gonna leave a bruise.”
3. A *CRUNCH!* sounds behind Theresa. She looks back. “Who’s there? Chrys?”
4. Theresa aims at an enormous green plant creature with a pitted torso and leafy limbs. It carries a long spraying device connected by a hose to a canister. Small insects hover above it.
