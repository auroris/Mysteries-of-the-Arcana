A composed memory image closes on Ead Enschild’s face beside three red-tinted scenes: the ruined Acorn Grove, Red Viquirrels attacking a Grey, and an unidentified figure giving a Grey Viquirrel a rifle. Ead narrates, “During the gathering time the Red Clan destroyed the Acorn Grove. We confronted them. They attacked us with strange new weapons. Happily, a new friend gave us new weapons as well!”
