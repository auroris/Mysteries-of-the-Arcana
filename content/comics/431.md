1. The Wanderer stands on a barren mountainside holding a weapon. Melody narrates, “...All they knew was war.”
2. The Seeker climbs through the mountains with its staff. “With their opponents vanquished, the newcomers searched.”
3. The Seeker gazes into a deep crater. “Searched for excitement. For purpose.”
4. The Wanderer sits on a cliff. “For new challenges.”
5. The Transformer looks over the Wanderer’s shoulder. “All they found was each other.”
