1. Chrystalline considers Theresa’s question. “Excellent question. Where should we go?”
2. She searches through cards bearing different symbols and crosses out one possibility. “No.”
3. Chrystalline rejects another card. “No.”

5. Theresa asks, “Which Arcana is that?” Chrystalline holds up the selected card. “The Hanged Man.”
