1. A rounded door announces *DING*.
2. The door slides open with a *SHUFF* as a silhouetted figure enters.
3. A metal peg leg taps the floor with a *tink*.
4. A large hook hangs at the figure’s side.
5. A skull-and-crossbones cape makes a *rustle*.
6. A colorful parrot pirate in a blue-and-gold coat and plumed hat poses dramatically. “I BE *FEATHERBEARD,* CAPTAIN OF THE *GOLDEN PARACHUTE!* WHAT CAN I DO FOR YE?” A small companion on his shoulder echoes, “FEATHERBEARD! PARACHUTE!”
