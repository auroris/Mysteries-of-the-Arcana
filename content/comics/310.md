1. A rounded door announces *DING*.
2. The door slides open with a *SHUFF* as a silhouetted figure enters.
3. A metal peg leg taps the floor with a *tink*.
4. A large hook in place of the figure’s hand.
5. White feathers *rustle* above the skull-and-crossbones brim of a broad pirate hat.
6. A colorful parrot pirate in a blue-and-gold coat, a red cape, and the feathered hat poses dramatically. “I be *Featherbeard,* captain of the *golden parachute!* What can I do for ye?” A small grey alien companion on his shoulder echoes, “Featherbeard! Parachute!”
