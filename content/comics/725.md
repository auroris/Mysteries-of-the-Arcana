1. The wolf-man's body is folded into the open compartment of the living range.
2. The range watches with one wide eye as its door shuts with a *CLUD*.
3. Thick trails of steam rise from the range.
4. Mandrake, the small stove, the candleholder, and the chair face the range as it releases several puffs with a *BEELCH*.
