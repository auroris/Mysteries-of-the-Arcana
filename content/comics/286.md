1. William and the male horror continue fighting while the blinded female horror wanders behind them. William raises his black blade as the man says, “I need to submit my action plan to the boss!”
2. The racket meets William’s blade with a *TNK*. “We should sit down over a nice cup of joe.”
3. William blocks the next swing with another *TNK*. “Does someone need a hug?”
4. William fails to block the next swing. The horror drives the racket into his side with a *THUD*, knocking the sword hilt from his hand. William cries, “Oof!!” The horror says, “I’m giving up red meat!”
5. William’s sword hilt lands beside Theresa’s hand with a *CLUNK*.
