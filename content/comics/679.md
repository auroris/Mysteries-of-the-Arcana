1. Theresa waves from the far end of the corridor while Chrystalline watches her leave. “Be back soon!”
2. Theresa walks away on her crutches.
3. Snow falls over a deserted city street as Theresa makes her way from the ruined building toward a church across the road.
4. Theresa climbs the church steps and pauses at the weathered door. “Huh.” A sign reads “Mass sun 10:30” and “St briahids.”
5. Theresa stands at the back of the church’s vacant nave, facing the distant altar. “Empty.”
