1. Chrystalline rests a hand on Theresa's shoulder. “As am I. I must rescue my mother and those I normally count on will not aid me.”
2. Chrystalline slides open a door. “Come, I will explain what happened while I make preparations.”
3. In a sepia-toned memory, Chrystalline and her mother, Melody, cross a barren landscape. Chrystalline gestures ahead while Melody shades her eyes and looks toward the sky. Chrystalline narrates, “My mother is a tale teller. She wanders the Arcana, collecting stories. We had just finished recording stories at a refugee camp and were leaving when my mother heard the buzzing of an aircraft...”
