1. Ruby, a red armored humanoid, approaches through an organic doorway.
2. Baron Sir commands, “Ruby! Report!”
3. Ruby says, “Target Circe inside. Target Blue inside.”
4. Baron Sir asks, “And the artifacts?” Ruby answers, “Unknown.”
5. Baron Sir stamps his foot with a *THWOMP*. “They must be there. They simply must!!”
6. He grips a knife. “Very well. I shall perform this task myself. Guard the door!” Ruby replies, “Affirmative.”
