1. Kludge puts an arm around Chrystalline and Theresa. “Good work, you two.”
2. He turns away. “Come on. We’re not done yet.”
3. Theresa points toward the orange device. “What’s that thing?” Kludge explains, “A relay. The inn’s computer network is hard-wired. No wi-fi access makes it harder to hack.”
4. The relay glows. Kludge continues, “This little beauty gives an off-site hacker access to our network.” Theresa asks, “So, we unplug it right?”
5. Theresa reaches for the plug. Kludge answers, “No.”
