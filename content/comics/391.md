1. Theresa stares at Chrystalline. “I THOUGHT YOU WERE RUNNING BEHIND.”
2. Chrystalline faces her as Mandrake flies away behind them. “BEHIND? WHY WOULD YOU THINK...?” Theresa answers, “MANDRAKE.”
3. Chrystalline smiles. “AH. I MENTIONED MY DESIRE FOR A BIG ENTRANCE. I SUSPECT SHE TRIED TO GIVE ME ONE.” Mandrake darts away in the background.
4. Theresa blushes and looks aside. “OH. WELL. YOU LOOK REALLY... NICE.”
5. Chrystalline smiles.
6. Chrystalline leans close to Theresa. “AS DO YOU.”
