1. Chrystalline strides down the Inn's wooden hallway while Theresa follows and repeatedly tries to interrupt. Theresa begins, “But...” Chrystalline continues, “...speaking to me as if I were a child...” Theresa tries again. “Wait...” Chrystalline presses on, “...I have walked the Arcana since I was small...” “Listen...” “I am certainly capable of rescuing my own mother from the clutches of...”
2. Theresa grabs the front of Chrystalline's cloak. “Shut the fuck up and tell me what's going on! I'm supposed to be dead! Not in the Outer Zone over the damn rainbow! No more living slime guys in spacesuits. No more lesbian elf kisses. No more absent minded professors or guys with dog heads. I want answers!!!”
3. Theresa and Chrystalline stare directly at each other in silence. Theresa still grips Chrystalline’s cloak.
4. Theresa's expression softens. She lowers her gaze and releases Chrystalline.
5. In a close view, Theresa turns her back while Chrystalline covers her forehead with one hand behind her. “Sorry. I'm just... frustrated.”
