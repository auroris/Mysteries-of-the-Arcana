1. Theresa and Chrystalline sit beside William as the hooded storyteller declares, “I SOUGHT THE STORY!” The audience responds, “WE STAND READY!”
2. Chrystalline smiles while Theresa watches the storyteller. “I FOUND THE STORY!” The audience answers, “WE WILL LISTEN!”
3. The storyteller raises both fists amid golden light. “I CAPTURED THE STORY!” The audience responds, “HEARTS AND MINDS OPEN!”
4. The storyteller lowers her head and grips her hood. “ONCE UPON A TIME...”
5. She removes the hood, revealing Melody’s long orange hair and eye patch. “IN THE BEGINNING...”
