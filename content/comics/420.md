1. A dark silhouette rises as an egg cracks open in three successive views. Melody narrates, “FROM THE DREAMS OF LIFE, NEW POWERS AROSE.”
2. A pale, red-eyed figure in a starry garment carries a bindle. “THE WANDERER.”
3. A small green hooded figure peers into a hand mirror. “THE SEEKER.”
4. A skeletal soldier holds a scythe bearing a butterfly. “THE TRANSFORMER.”
5. A further indistinct figure emerges through white folds. “AND OTHERS. EACH UNIQUE, POWERFUL, THE PURE ESSENCE OF AN IDEA.”
