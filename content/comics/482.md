1. Mandrake arrives broadcasting pictograms of Chrystalline, Theresa, and William with an exclamation mark.
2. Theresa and Chrystalline call, “Mandrake?” William asks, “Dost thou have intelligence on the situation?”
3. In a close inset, Mandrake looks alarmed. She sends a long pictogram report: a lit bulb and unlit bulb separated by an ampersand; a spiky-haired face, a sleeping purple face, another ampersand, a gear, and a screwdriver; Mandrake, an ampersand, Baron Sir, Red Hair Zombie, and a bespectacled woman; then running figures, a barred door, and a sign reading “EXIT.”
4. Chrystalline blows a bubble while Theresa stares.
5. Theresa gestures toward Mandrake. “Translate that for me, please?” Chrystalline’s bubble bursts with a *POP*.
