1. Theresa sits on the rocky ground holding her head as Chrystalline approaches. “Theresa...”
2. Chrystalline reaches toward Theresa. “I... I had no idea.”
3. Chrystalline gently reaches for Theresa’s head. “Your journal... I expected... your father...”
4. Theresa brushes Chrystalline’s hand with her fingers. Chrystalline asks, “Are you alright?”
5. Theresa remains bowed over. “I don’t know. I... wait.”
6. Theresa looks up in tearful fury. “You knew?”
