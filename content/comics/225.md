1. Theresa sits on the path with one hand over her face as Chrystalline approaches. “Theresa...”
2. Chrystalline reaches toward Theresa. “I... I had no idea.”
3. Chrystalline gently touches Theresa’s hair. “Your journal... I expected... your father...”
4. Theresa catches Chrystalline’s wrist. Chrystalline asks, “Are you alright?”
5. Theresa remains bowed over. “I don’t know. I... wait.”
6. Theresa looks up in fury. “You knew?”
