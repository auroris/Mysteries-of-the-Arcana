1. The Golden Parachute flies through a field of stars, centered in a targeting reticle. Aleena asks over the communication link, “CAN YOU SEE HIM?”
2. Captain Featherbeard stands before a broad window, also centered in the reticle. Kietra replies, “YEP. I CAN SEE HIM. IDIOT'S STANDING IN FRONT OF THE WINDOW PONTIFICATING ON THE DAMN STARS, JUST LIKE YOU SAID.” Aleena asks, “CAN YOU MAKE THE SHOT?”
3. Kietra sits at a large, blue-lit weapon station while a small hologram of Aleena stands before her. “NOW, WHEN HAVE I EVER MISSED?”
4. Aleena looks back with a doubtful smile. “WELL...” Kietra cuts in from off panel. “SHUT IT!”
