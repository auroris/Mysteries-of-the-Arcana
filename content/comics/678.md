1. Theresa stands in the ruined corridor wearing her green jacket and leaning on her crutches. Chrystalline holds one crutch steady. “Ready to travel home then?”
2. Theresa takes hold of the crutch. “Actually, there’s somewhere I need to go.” Chrystalline begins, “I suppose a small detour wouldn’t be so—”
3. Theresa raises a hand between them. “Alone.” Chrystalline recoils. “Sorry. But you just got out of the wheelchair!”
4. Theresa walks away on her crutches. “It isn’t far. I’ll be okay.” Chrystalline watches from behind. “But... Very well.”
5. Chrystalline remains alone in the corridor, looking after Theresa. She thinks, “I’ll just wait here then.”
