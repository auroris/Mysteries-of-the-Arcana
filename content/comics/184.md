1. Chrystalline climbs a rocky slope. “I wonder, who is Debby?”
2. Theresa climbs below her. “No one.”
3. Chrystalline pulls herself onto the ledge. “You have called out her name twice, now.”
4. Chrystalline sits on the rock. “Once, in the Devil.” A grey image recalls Theresa crying out there. Chrystalline continues, “Once, just now.”
5. Chrystalline stands. “Clearly she is someone.”
