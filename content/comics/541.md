1. A lightning-shaped opening appears over the Royal Road crystals with a *KRASH!!* Kludge explains, “We call anyone who can activate and use the Royal Road a Key. Since you activated the Royal Road to get here, that make YOU a Key.”
2. An arrow labeled “Ancient, mythical ‘god piece.’” points to a light glowing in a LEGO astronaut’s chest. Kludge continues, “Some say Keys have this power because each of us holds a piece of an ancient, mythical god deep within our soul.”
3. Kludge shrugs. “Is that true? Meh. I guess it could be. But, for whatever reason, we Keys can use the Royal Road to move between Arcana.”
4. Kludge gestures. “And that’s not the only power being a Key gives us!” The footer reads “KLUDGE’S GUIDE PT 6.”
