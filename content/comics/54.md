1. William gestures between Theresa and the glowing Road while Chrystalline watches. “’Tis simple. Standeth upon the stone. Visualize thyself and those thou wishest to travel with thee. Demand the Road transporteth thee.”
2. Theresa backs away with one hand raised. “Yeah...I’m gonna go with his way. You really don’t want to hear me sing.”
3. Chrystalline argues with William while Theresa stands behind him. “Mother’s method works quite well, thank you very much!” William answers, “Not everyone hath an hour’s time to openeth the gate.”
4. Theresa turns away as Chrystalline raises her arms behind William. “It does not take that long!” William replies, “Aye, true. It takes only half that time.” Theresa says, “I’ll be over here. Let me know when you’re done.”
