1. Against a golden collage of religious art, the priest regards Theresa steadily. “You may have noticed my church isn’t in the nicest part of town. In that confessional I have listened to horrible sins. I’ve listened to the sins of those addicted to poisoning their own body. To the sins of those who have sold that poison. To the sins of people forced by circumstance to sell their bodies and to the sins of those who hold those poor people in bondage. But I have also christened and baptized, married and buried because, despite the hardships, there is love here. Love is, I think, God’s greatest gift to humanity. So no. I don’t think any action born of real love is wrong. Or evil. Or sinful. I would never be so arrogant as to gainsay a gift of God.”
2. Theresa clutches the front of her jacket while the priest sits behind her. “I suspect there’s someone who has loved you unconditionally.”
3. In a close view, Theresa’s eye glistens against the stained-glass colors. The priest asks from off panel, “Do you think that person would be proud of who you are right now?”
4. The face of an unidentified dark-haired man appears beside an image of a bearded religious figure.
