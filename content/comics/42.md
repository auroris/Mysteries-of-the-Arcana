1. Theresa gives William a pointed smile. “With that kind of optimism, how can we lose?”
2. William strikes his forehead with a *FWAP!!* “By the moons, girl! We embark to wage war upon an enemy long practiced in the art of slaughter!”
3. William and Theresa bare their teeth inches from each other's faces. William: “War! Not play fighting with swords of wood. If thy mouth can babble forth quips thou clearly dost not understand the price we are apt to pay!” Theresa fires back, “You don't know shit about me or what I understand, so stuff your damn hole!”
