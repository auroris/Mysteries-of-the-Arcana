1. Theresa looks away from the Void. “Well, come on. We’ve got more measuring to do.” Faint, illegible whispers come from the white boundary behind her.
2. Kludge turns toward Theresa as the whispers continue. “Theresa?”
3. Theresa looks over her shoulder toward the Void. “There’s something...” More faint, illegible whispering issues from it.
4. A distorted image of Quest appears within the white boundary. Theresa drops to the ground before it. “What the fuck?!”
