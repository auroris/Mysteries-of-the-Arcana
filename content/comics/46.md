1. Theresa clenches her fists and shouts, “Can we just get the damn supplies!?!” In an inset, she covers her mouth while Mandrake and Chrystalline stare. Theresa adds, “You know... for the mission.”
2. Chrystalline reaches into Mandrake's pink light. “Our provisions are all contained within this, Theresa.”
3. A close view shows Chrystalline holding up a small circular pendant on a cord. “A decorative, digital matter storage unit.”
4. Theresa folds her arms and looks skeptical. “A what now?”
5. Chrystalline smiles over her shoulder. “Watch this.”
