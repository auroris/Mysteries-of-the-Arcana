1. Theresa asks Chrystalline, “Do you know any other way in?” Chrystalline protests, “But my father’s warning! The baleful gaze!”
2. Theresa counts the trapped residents on her fingers. “Surgeon. Blue. Circe. Kludge.”
3. Chrystalline warns, “Dreamwalking nauseates non-dreamers! The last time you fainted!”
4. Theresa takes Chrystalline by the shoulders. “I know what to expect this time. I’ll deal. Chrys, if you have another option...”
5. Chrystalline lowers her head. “No.”
6. She admits, “You are correct. We have no other options.”
