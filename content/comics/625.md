1. Chrystalline addresses Mister Eerie and General Isc. “General Isc, please! The ground is covered in snow. You should be hibernating, not fighting!”
2. Isc says, “The Acorn Grove is *gone.* There is not enough food for us all to get through the winter.”
3. He adds, “Our friend gave us a way to fix things. What have you given us?”
4. Chrystalline says, “He’s not your friend!” Mister Eerie replies, “Of course I am.” Isc adds, “Of course he is!”
5. Theresa asks, “If he’s such a good friend, why did he give you the most crap guns possible?”
