1. Uncle Mork slaps the counter. “*Only* five hundred credits!”
2. Mandrake speaks in picture symbols showing a prohibition sign followed by a stack of coins. Chrystalline presses a finger to her lips. “Hmmmm...”
3. Chrystalline looks over shelves crowded with figurines. “I notice you collect dolls.” Uncle Mork replies, “Sure do! One hundred and countin’! All rare!”
4. Chrystalline closes one hand around Mandrake as Mandrake projects a haybale and an exclamation mark. Chrystalline says, “Then I propose a trade.”
5. Chrystalline holds Mandrake up like a doll. “My doll possesses anti-gravity for simulated flight. ...And a holographic pictogram projector.” Mandrake projects a skull, a musical note, a spiral, lightning, an asterisk, and several exclamation points.
6. Chrystalline smiles. “Do we have a deal?”
