A solemn composite commemorates military service. In the foreground, a grieving person kneels, lays a flower at a grave, and rests one hand against its marker. The marker is engraved “M.R.S.” and has other faint, unreadable text. Behind them, a uniformed service member salutes before a rippling American flag. The footer reads “Remembering.”
