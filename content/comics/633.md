1. Chrystalline addresses the Viquirrel leaders on the battlefield. “Gentlemen, we are here today to make peace.”
2. She says, “Snow lays on the ground. Your people are tired and need to hibernate.”
3. Chrystalline continues, “Without the peace to hibernate, your people will grow sick and die.”
4. Standing between Ead Enschild and General Isc, she concludes, “Be you Red or Grey, you are all Viquirrels. You must make peace to survive!”
