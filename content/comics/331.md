1. Rubbing her bruised head, Theresa says, “Okay. Okay. I can make this work. Load up the truck! I just need...”
2. She removes a wire-and-gem pendant from her pocket. “Buy me some time!”
3. Theresa works with the pendant, a Vitamin X bottle, and wires while William loads the ant mount into the truck's bed.
4. William draws his sword. “Buy thee time.”
5. A large, bizarre crowd charges toward them. In an inset close view, William’s red eyes narrow. “Aye.”
