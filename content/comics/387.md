1. Someone knocks at the closed door. *KNOCK KNOCK!*
2. Theresa looks up while dressing. “JUST A SECOND!”
3. Reaching for the handle, she thinks, “THIS IS NOT A DATE. THIS NOT A DATE. THIS IS *NOT A DATE!*”
4. The door opens with a *SQUEEK!*
5. Theresa looks out. “OH.”
6. Mandrake hovers in the doorway, broadcasting a smiling face, a sun, and an exclamation mark.
