1. Her father lists the scheme’s participants over small images: “I knew of their scheme. The alien thief. The crooked merchant. The rigged escape pod. The defective flying saucer.” The saucer makes a *PUT PUT* and *BEEEWWWWWWW*.
2. He continues, “I let them capture me. Then I risked sending a quiet word to Circe, asking her to send you here.”
3. Chrystalline says, “I see. You have worked our net, wove?” He replies, “Yes. You and I hide here together. Cut off all contact and focus all our efforts on discovering the truth of this thing.”
4. Chrystalline recoils. “What? No!”
