1. Chrystalline asks Mandrake, “WHY?” Mandrake answers in picture symbols showing Chrystalline’s face, a “SALE” sign, Mandrake’s face, and an exclamation mark.
2. Chrystalline replies, “...THAT PLAN *WORKED!* YOU *ESCAPED FROM MORK* AND...” Mandrake fumes behind her beneath a scribble symbol.
3. Mandrake snaps her fingers with a *SNAP*.
4. Mandrake’s head vanishes into a bright streak with a *SNAP*.
5. Mandrake’s arm vanishes with a *SNAP*.
6. The rest of Mandrake vanishes past Chrystalline in another streak with a *SNAP*.
