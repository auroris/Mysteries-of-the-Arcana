1. Chrystalline asks Mandrake, “Why?” Mandrake answers in picture symbols showing Chrystalline’s face, a “Sale” sign, Mandrake’s face, and an exclamation mark.
2. Chrystalline replies, “...That plan *worked!* You *escaped from Mork* and...” Mandrake fumes behind her beneath a scribble symbol.
3. Mandrake plants one hand on her hip and begins an emphatic, sweeping finger-snap gesture with a *SNAP*.
4. As she snaps her hand across her face, her head vanishes into a bright streak with another *SNAP*.
5. She continues the gesture down her body, and her arm vanishes into a streak with a *SNAP*.
6. Mandrake completes the sweep and disappears entirely, leaving a bright streak across Chrystalline with a final *SNAP*.
