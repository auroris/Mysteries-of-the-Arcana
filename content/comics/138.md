1. Keira Wood looks down at her pencil. “Umm...” An unidentified voice asks, “Who dares destroy my creation?”
2. A radiant, bald figure in glasses appears beneath the label “J. Gray.” “This comic is unsanctioned!”
3. J. Gray folds his arms. “Keith, you have broken the cardinal rule. You never, ever kill a main character! Not even in a filler strip.”
4. J. Gray looks toward Keira and Chrystalline. “Now, I suppose I’ll have to clean up your mess...” Chrystalline asks, “Why is his head all glowy?” Keira answers, “Because no one knows what he looks like. Not even me.”
5. J. Gray restores Theresa with a huge *RESURRECT!* Keira asks Chrystalline, “Why are you talking to me? Didn’t I just kill your girlfriend?” Chrystalline replies, “She wasn’t putting out anyway.”
