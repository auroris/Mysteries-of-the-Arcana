1. Baron Sir raises his lantern and a glowing control. “Ruby, restore power. Initiate lock-down.” Ruby answers remotely, “Affirmative, Baron sir.”
2. Baron Sir pulls a ring from a small orange device with a *KLINK*.
3. A metal shutter closes with a *SLAM*.
4. Baron Sir tells Blue Hair Zombie, “Guard them.” Blue Hair Zombie replies, “Gruh.”
5. Baron Sir plugs the orange relay into a network device.
6. He presses a control with a *CLICK*. “Aleena, I have plugged in the relay. You should have access to their network. Proceed.” Aleena answers remotely, “Got it.”
