1. The two clans stand separated across the barren ground. General Isc says, “The acorn trees are all gone!”
2. Mister Mist says, “Between the stores of the Reds and Greys there are only one clan worth of acorns available.”
3. Mister Eerie says, “They could split the acorns but that would be asking them to choose which of their adorable people would starve to death.”
4. Mist and Eerie loom over Chrystalline. Together they ask, “Now, what sort of monster would do that?”
