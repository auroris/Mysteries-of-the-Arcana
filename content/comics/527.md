1. Theresa sits alone at a table with a plate and mug. Chrystalline asks, “May I join you?”
2. Wearing a pink robe and carrying breakfast, Chrystalline approaches the table. Theresa looks up. “Huh? Oh. Yeah. Go ahead.”
3. Chrystalline notices Theresa’s untouched food. “Not hungry?” Theresa replies, “Guess I’m not.”
4. Theresa looks down at her hands.
5. Chrystalline rests her hand over Theresa’s. “Do you wish to talk about it?” Theresa replies, “No. Yes. I don’t know... maybe.”
