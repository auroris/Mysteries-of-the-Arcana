1. Featherbeard raises a hand. “YE’LL BE NEEDING TO *SURRENDER* NOW.” His companion echoes, “SURRENDER!” Chrystalline shouts, “*NEVER!*”
2. Featherbeard raises his hook. He and his companion call, “*SURRENDER!*”
3. Chrystalline begins, “*DEATH FI...*” A mysterious voice in a purple bubble interrupts, “JUST SURRENDER, *DEARHEART.*”
4. Chrystalline freezes, then cries, “*HOW DARE YOU!*”
5. Featherbeard looks puzzled. “SORRY?” Chrystalline snaps, “YOU CALLED ME *DEARHEART!*”
