1. Chrystalline follows Theresa across the wasteland. “You are what we call a key.”
2. Chrystalline raises one finger as Theresa scans ahead. “Each key possesses an intimate connection to a specific Arcana.”
3. Chrystalline continues while Theresa keeps watch. “They gain power through this connection.”
4. Chrystalline says, “The Devil symbolizes not an evil entity but...” Theresa interrupts by clapping one hand over her mouth. “Will you shut up!?!”
5. Seen through a green, hexagonal scan pattern, Theresa whispers into Chrystalline’s ear, “I’m trying to make sure we don’t walk into an ambush!”
