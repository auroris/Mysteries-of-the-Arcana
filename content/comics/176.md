1. Chrystalline points out to sea while Theresa holds the gong frame.
2. Theresa shades her eyes. “Is...”
3. “...that island moving?” Theresa asks. A distant island rushes across the water. *Swoooooosh!*
4. The forested island approaches at tremendous speed. *Swoooooosh!*
5. From above, the moving island dwarfs the small sandy island.
6. Knower of Truths, a colossal turtle carrying the forested island on its shell, stops with its face beside Theresa and Chrystalline.
