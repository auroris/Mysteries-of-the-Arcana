1. Baron Sir clutches his head. “Horsefeathers!”
2. He points toward Surgeon. “Rip the robot’s arms off. All of them!” Red Hair Zombie answers, “Gruh!” Surgeon objects, “[Exclamation] No!”
3. A second face grows from Baron Sir’s neck as he holds two radios. “Kietra! Switch to lethal counter-measures! Aleena! Double-time! Retrieve those files!”
4. The extra face disappears. Baron Sir picks up a corked glass vial. “Now, let us see...”
5. He peers through the vial. “...What Mister Kludge has to say.”
