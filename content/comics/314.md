1. The purple voice says, “ENOUGH! CHRYSTALLINE, *SURRENDER!*” Chrystalline looks startled. “OH!”
2. She turns away from Featherbeard. “VOICE IS IN MY HEAD. SO SORRY. GIVE ME A *MOMENT.*”
3. Chrystalline says, “I REALLY *DISLIKE* THE IDEA OF SURRENDERING.” The voice replies, “I UNDERSTAND. BUT IT IS *IMPORTANT* YOU DO SO.”
4. Chrystalline asks, “...WHO *IS* THIS?” The voice answers, “*NOW,* CHRYSTALLINE!”
5. Her face falls into shadow. “*VERY WELL.*”
6. Featherbeard whispers to an anchor-marked guard, “WATCH THIS ONE. I BE THINKIN’ HER A FEW ZEROES SHORT OF A PRICE TAG.”
