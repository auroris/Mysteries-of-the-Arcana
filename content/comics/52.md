1. Chrystalline draws blue-white symbols in the air with one hand. “Certain, special individuals may walk the Road, traveling from another Arcana to this one...”
2. The symbols resolve into a ring and smaller glyphs as Chrystalline continues, “...and from this Arcana to any other. Today, you learn to walk the Royal Road.”
3. Theresa stands in the magenta light of one of the pillars of the Royal Road and raises one hand. “Lucky me.”
4. Chrystalline takes both of Theresa’s hands. “Come. I shall teach you my mother’s method for opening the Road.”
