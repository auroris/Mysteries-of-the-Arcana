1. Theresa meditates with her eyes closed and her pistol held upright. The glowing Devil Tarot card appears behind her. She thinks, “I can do this. I can do this. I can...”
2. Her eyes snap open. “...do this. I know how to kill it!”
3. Theresa and Chrystalline shelter behind the block as shots streak past with an *ATATATATAT!* and *ATATATATAT!* Theresa says, “I just need to get behind it. Can you distract it?”
4. Chrystalline grips the clasp of her cloak and smiles. “Leave it to me.” In a small simplified inset, Theresa says, “That was easier than I thought.” Chrystalline replies, “Eh. I think the story’s running long.”
