1. Four alien guards are struck in the head or chest by Kietra and Aleena's shots. Two impacts make a *SPLOOT* and two make a *SPLAT*.
2. Kietra and Aleena fire from opposite sides of a row of brightly colored escape pods with two *KZZAT*s.
3. Kietra tumbles backward between two rushing escape pods while firing both pistols repeatedly, with two *KZZAT*s followed by repeated *AT*s. One pod passes close with a *SHLOOP*.
4. Aleena clenches the fist of her armored gauntlet.
5. Segments separate along the gauntlet's forearm, revealing blue light and arcing electricity.
6. A blue energy blade extends from Aleena's gauntlet with a *SHWOPT*.
