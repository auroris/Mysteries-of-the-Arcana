1. Baron Sir leans into Kludge’s face. “Am I understood?”
2. Kludge replies, “Yeah, sure.” Baron Sir corrects him, “I believe you mean ‘yes Baron Sir.’”
3. Baron Sir leans closer until he and Kludge are nearly nose to nose.
4. Kludge turns his head away. “...Yes, Baron Sir.”
5. Baron Sir says, “Good man. Now, I know the storyteller has gone off wandering but I wonder... where are the children? The pointy-eared one, the boyish one and the mongrel? My intelligence suggested they would be here with you.”
