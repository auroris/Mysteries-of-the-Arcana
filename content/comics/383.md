1. Chrystalline stands by shimmering water beneath a bright, turbulent sky, her long hair blowing in the wind.
2. Sitting at the water’s edge, she reads, “IW WANTED TO KNOW IF THE COMIC WILL BE SHOWCASING EVERY POSSIBLE ARCANA. THAT WOULD BE 72 DIFFERENT UNIVERSES.”
3. In the water, Chrystalline smiles as fish swim around her. “THE ANSWER IS PROBABLY NOT. THE ARCANA VISITED BY THE CHARACTERS IS DICTATED BY THE STORYLINE. THAT BEING SAID, J HOPES TO DEFINE ALL 72 ARCANA BEFORE THE END OF THE COMIC, WHICH PROBABLY WON’T BE FOR YEARS.”
4. Chrystalline swims away with webbed fingers raised. “NOW, IF YOU’LL EXCUSE ME, I’M FEELING LIKE FISHGIRL TONIGHT.”
