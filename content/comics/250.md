2. Above the pod doors, the small gray alien eases open an overhead hatch with a *CREEEEEAK* and peers out.
3. The alien jumps down from the hatch behind Chrystalline.
4. He shoves Chrystalline bodily into the escape pod with a *FWUMPH!*.
