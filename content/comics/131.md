1. Chrystalline sits behind the block and throws her cloak into the air while the sentry continues firing. *ATATATATAT!*
2. With her eyes closed, Chrystalline whistles a tune, producing a trail of purple musical notes.
3. Her music magic animates the cloak and sends it flying down the corridor as the robot’s orange shots follow it. Holes appear in the fabric amid an *AT AT AT AT AT!*
4. The animated cloak sweeps around the robot as sustained fire tears more holes through it.
5. The riddled cloak drapes over the robot’s head and sensors while it keeps firing.
