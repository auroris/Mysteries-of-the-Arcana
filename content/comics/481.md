1. Theresa asks William, “Where’s your sword?” William replies, “’Tis safely stored away in my quarters.”
2. Theresa starts, “But?” William answers, “I trust not the vagaries of the storage devices.”
3. Chrystalline explains, “He does have a point. Damage to the unit or a corrupted sector on the solid state storage drive could mean the loss of an item forever.”
4. Chrystalline turns away. “I try to avoid storing precious items in my digital storage device.”
5. Mandrake arrives as Theresa shouts, “Why didn’t you tell me all this before I put my gun in mine?!?”
