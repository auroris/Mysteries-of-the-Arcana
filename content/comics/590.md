1. Chrystalline begins, “Well, thank y—” but Triple Signpost interrupts, “There is an outsider!”
2. Each of Triple Signpost’s three faces delivers part of the warning: “I cannot see its past.” “I cannot see its present.” “I cannot see its future.”
3. Chrystalline says, “Well, that *is* disturbing.”
4. Chrystalline bows. “Thank you for your time. We shall be going now.”
5. Chrystalline asks, “Outsider?” Theresa explains, “Someone from another Arcana. One of us.”
