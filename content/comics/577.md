1. Chrystalline enters the workroom dressed for cold weather as Theresa concentrates on the pistol. “Sorry about what?” Theresa startles. “Gah!”
2. Theresa flails and sends the pistol flying into the air. “What the hell, Chrys?”
3. Chrystalline replies, “I entered via the very noisy lift, Theresa. I assumed you heard me.”
4. Theresa resumes working on the pistol. “Oh. Guess I was distracted.”
