1. Theresa stares at an upside-down letter with comically wide eyes. “I CAN’T READ THIS LETTER!”
2. Chrystalline leans over Theresa’s shoulder and turns the sheet around; its writing reads “XXX?”
3. Chrystalline yanks the letter away from Theresa with a *KER-YOINK!*
4. She hands it back right-side up. “THAT ISN’T SO BAD. GO AHEAD, READ IT.”
5. Theresa holds the sheet, which reads “NOT XXX!” and sticks out her tongue. “FINE.” Printed text beneath the panels asks, “I’VE HEARD THAT CHRYS HAS A TATTOO. WHERE IS IT AND WHAT IS IT OF?”
6. Chrystalline lifts her shirt to reveal a small heart tattoo surrounded by dark markings on her lower abdomen. “I ONCE HAD TWO TATTOOS BUT I REMOVED ONE AFTER BREAKING UP WITH WILLIAM.”
