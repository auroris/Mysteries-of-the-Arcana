1. Chrystalline tells Theresa, “Take a moment, then join us.” She walks away while Theresa remains seated on the barren ground, thinking, “Icandothis. Icandothis. Icandothis.”
2. A sustained *BZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ* comes from above. Theresa looks up. “Icandothis. Icandothis. Ican... What the hell?”
3. A loud-speaker helidrone Prophet hovers before Theresa with a *BZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ*. “You cannot prevail. The Intelligence controls all. You should kill yourself now. You cannot prevail. The Intelligence controls all. You should kill yourself now.”
4. Theresa aims her pistol at the Prophet while William and Chrystalline react behind her. William calls, “Nay!” Chrystalline cries, “No!” Theresa says, “Son of a...”
5. Theresa fires with a *BLAM!*
