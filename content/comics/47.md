1. Chrystalline holds the pendant over her open palm as green-white bands of light form between her hands with a *ZNNNNNNNNNNN*. Theresa, Mandrake, and William watch.
2. A faint wire-frame shape begins to form within the bands of light.
3. The wire frame resolves into the outline of a pistol.
4. The pistol begins filling in as a gray object.
5. Theresa’s pistol lies fully materialized.
6. Theresa hugs the pistol to her chest while Chrystalline rests a hand on her arm. “My gun!” Chrystalline explains, “Circe stored your weapon away when we arrived. I asked Mandrake to retrieve it for you.”
