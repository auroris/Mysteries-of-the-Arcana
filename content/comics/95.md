1. Sitting in the tent, Theresa reaches toward a dark, shriveled fruit on the ground. “Huh?”
2. Theresa lifts the fruit and studies it in her hand.
3. The fruit disintegrates into black dust in Theresa’s face with a *PFFT!* She recoils. “What the hell?”
4. Light enters through a small opening in the tent as Chrystalline speaks from outside. “That was an un-poisoned apple. They draw toxins from the body.”
5. Chrystalline pulls open the tent flap and steps inside. “You saved my life again. You saved all our lives.”
