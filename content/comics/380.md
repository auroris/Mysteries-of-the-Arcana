Theresa and Chrystalline sit together on an orange couch as Chrystalline reads, “DP ASKS: ‘HOW ARE THE PEOPLE OF VARIOUS ARCANA CHOSEN?’ I THINK HE’S CURIOUS ABOUT KEYS.”

She explains, “TO REFRESH EVERYONE’S MEMORIES, KEYS ARE PEOPLE WHO ARE CAPABLE OF USING THE ROYAL ROAD. EACH HAS AN AFFINITY FOR A SPECIFIC ARCANA AND GAINS A POWER BECAUSE OF THAT. FOR EXAMPLE, THERESA IS A DEVIL KEY. SHE CAN SENSE WEAKNESSES. WILLIAM IS A KNIGHT OF SWORDS KEY. HE CAN SENSE INFORMATION ABOUT ENEMIES.”

Chrystalline presses a finger to her lips. “WE’LL BE DISCUSSING THIS IN FULL NEXT CHAPTER BUT SINCE YOU ARE ALL SUCH WONDERFUL FANS, I’LL GIVE YOU A PREVIEW.”

“THE ARCANA AREN’T JUST PLACES. THEY ARE ALSO ENTITIES. PIECES OF THEM... FRAGMENTS OF THEIR SOULS... HAVE BECOME SCATTERED ACROSS THE MULTIVERSE. THESE SOUL FRAGMENTS LODGE IN LIVING BEINGS LIKE THERESA AND WILLIAM, GIVING THEM POWERS. SO, IN ESSENCE, ALL KEYS HAVE A PIECE OF A SLEEPING GOD INSIDE THEM. NEAT, ISN’T IT?”
