1. A caption reads “A short bit later.” Keira Wood sits eating a sandwich. Chrystalline asks from off panel, “Where’s the Mountain Dew?” Theresa answers from off panel, “In the fridge, duh!” Keira asks, “So, what can I do for you, Theresa?”
2. Theresa leans against the desk. Chrystalline calls from off panel, “I’m going to get a soda, anyone want one?” Theresa says, “Well, I was wondering exactly what the deal is with you repeatedly abusing me in the wallpapers?”
3. Keira hesitates. “Umm... well... you see...” Theresa continues from off panel, “And the fact that no matter what, I always end up wearing the dress... or scantily clad... or hell, even in the Christmas wallpaper you have me in the elf outfit when you and I both know that Chrys would be far better suited for it. I mean, c’mon, Keith, what the hell is up with that?”
4. Keira points at Theresa and casts a spell. “Avada Kedavra!” Theresa recoils. “Gah!”
