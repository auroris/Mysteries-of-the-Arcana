1. Chrystalline glares at Theresa. “That thing tortured my mother!” Theresa says, “It might have intel we need to survive.”
2. Chrystalline turns away. “Survive? Mother’s a musician! How can she survive like this?”
3. Theresa grabs Chrystalline’s arm. “*Grow up!*”
4. Theresa confronts her. “You’ll be bringing her home broken, but at least you’ll be bringing her home!”
5. Theresa’s expression hardens. “Some of us didn’t get that chance.”
