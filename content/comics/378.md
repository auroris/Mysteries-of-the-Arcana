Chrystalline reads, “I WANTED TO KNOW WHICH ARCANA HAVE MADE AN APPEARANCE IN *MYSTERIES OF THE ARCANA* THUS FAR.” She replies, “GOOD QUESTION!” Theresa looks distracted by a thought of the two women kissing as Chrystalline explains, “THE ANSWER’S A BIG ONE SO THIS ONE’LL BE SPREAD OUT OVER TWO PAGES. WE’LL START WITH THE ONES FROM THE KLUDGE’S NOTES EXTRA AND MOVE ONTO ARCANA FROM THE COMIC ITSELF.”

- “THE MOON IS MANDRAKE’S HOME ARCANA. IT IS A WORLD THAT MIXES CYBERPUNK AND FANTASY WITH FAERIES WHO HAVE CYBERNETIC IMPLANTS, FORM MEGACORPORATIONS, AND JACK INTO CYBERSPACE. EVERYONE THERE TALKS IN PICTURES. IT’S REALLY ANNOYING. JUST LIKE MANDRAKE!”
- “THE KNIGHT OF SWORDS IS A WORLD OBSESSED WITH PUZZLES. START A CONVERSATION THERE AND YOU’LL LIKELY END UP WITH A PUZZLE TO SOLVE. ANY SIMILARITY TO A VIDEO GAME SERIES STARRING A PROFESSOR AND HIS ASSISTANT IS PURELY COINCIDENTAL. HONESTLY!”
- “MY MOTHER WAS BORN IN THE HANGED MAN. DURING THE DAY IT SEEMS TO BE A SIMPLE MEDIEVAL VILLAGE. AT NIGHT, HOWEVER, EVERYONE SLEEPS AND IN A SHARED DREAM THEY TAKE ON FAIRY TALE ROLES. WHEN MY MOTHER IS THERE, FOR EXAMPLE, SHE BECOMES THE RED RIDING HOOD’S GRANDMOTHER! CHRYS SAYS SHE BECOMES A FAIRY TALE PERSON THERE, TOO, BUT WON’T TELL ME WHO.”
- “IN THE PAGE OF CUPS, EVERYTHING IS LIQUID INCLUDING THOUGHTS AND EMOTIONS. IF YOU’RE HAPPY, YOUR HAPPINESS BECOMES A PLEASANT PINK LIQUID THAT CAN BE ABSORBED BY OTHERS TO SHARE. BLUE IS FROM THERE, WHICH EXPLAINS WHY HE’S SO FREAKING WEIRD.”
- “IN THE THREE OF CUPS, EVERYONE HAS FOUR ARMS AND TWO FACES. EVERYTHING IS CENTERED AROUND CRAFTING AND THE UTILITY OF DESIGN REIGNS SUPREME. FUNCTION OVER FORM. SOUNDS LIKE SENSIBLE PEOPLE TO ME!”

Chrystalline waves. “THAT’S IT FOR TODAY! MORE TOMORROW!”
