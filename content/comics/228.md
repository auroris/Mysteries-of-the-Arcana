1. A close view shows Chrystalline’s eye. “If one approaches the Knower of Truths without a specific quest in mind...”
2. Theresa lowers her head. “Yeah. It makes you face your inner demons. I get it.”
3. The two women stand silhouetted at the edge of the path beneath a purple sunset. Chrystalline says, “I wanted to share your pain.” “I believed it would make you feel better.”
4. Theresa clenches a fist in Chrystalline’s clothing. “You were right.”
5. Chrystalline tumbles backward through a glowing blue portal while Theresa stands over her.
6. Chrystalline lies on the ground beyond the portal as Theresa looks down. “I do feel better.”
