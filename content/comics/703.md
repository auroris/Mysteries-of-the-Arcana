1. Kietra and Aleena stand before a heavy hatch. Aleena checks her wrist. “HERE WE ARE. HOW MUCH TIME LEFT?” Kietra replies, “NOT MUCH. BEST WE GET ON WITH IT.”
2. Aleena presses a control beside the hatch. “ROGER THAT.”
3. The hatch shuts between them with a *CLNK* and a *SCREEET*.
4. Kietra and Aleena approach a broad-shouldered client silhouetted in the dark chamber beyond. The client greets them. “LADIES. PERFECT TIMING.” Kietra replies, “YOU HIRED THE BEST.”
5. The client is a long-haired blond elf dressed in purple. “OF COURSE. LET'S MOVE ONTO THE NEXT STAGE OF OUR PLAN, SHALL WE?” The closing text reads “THE END.”
