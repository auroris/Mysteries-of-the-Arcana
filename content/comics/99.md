1. Theresa fumes while Chrystalline summons food from her pendant. “Don’t let him trouble you.” Theresa mutters, “Stupid sexist jackass.”
2. Chrystalline takes both Theresa's hands. A bowl of red berries now sits upon the ground. “Come. Eat.”
3. Chrystalline offers the berries. “BbuzZ knows the facility holding my mother. In the morning, we’ll recon and formulate a plan.”
4. Theresa eats a berry. “Good. I still feel a little woozy. These berries are pretty...”
5. Chrystalline rests her head on Theresa’s shoulder with a contented “*Mmmmmmmm*.” Theresa stiffens, holding the bowl. “...ummm... What are you doing?”
