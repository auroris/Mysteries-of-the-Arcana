1. Theresa walks away while Chrystalline follows. “Don’t let him trouble you.” Theresa mutters, “Stupid sexist jackass.” Chrystalline raises her glowing pendant, and red berries appear in green light.
2. Chrystalline offers Theresa a bowl filled with the red berries. “Come. Eat.”
3. Theresa takes the bowl. Chrystalline says, “BbuzZ knows the facility holding my mother. In the morning, we’ll recon and formulate a plan.”
4. Theresa eats a berry. “Good. I still feel a little woozy. These berries are pretty...”
5. Chrystalline rests her head on Theresa’s shoulder with a contented “*Mmmmmmmm*.” Theresa stiffens, still holding the bowl. “...ummm... What are you doing?”
