1. William kneels beside a saddle and demonstrates its stirrup to Theresa. “STEP HERE, THEN SWING THY LEG...” Theresa interrupts, “I KNOW HOW TO RIDE!”
2. William looks at Theresa. “AS THOU SAYEST.”
3. Theresa and William mount two giant ants among the other tethered insects. Theresa asks, “SHOULDN’T WE, I DUNNO, SCATTER THE OTHER ANTS OR SOMETHING?” William replies, “THESE ART HONORABLE KNIGHTS. T’WOULD BE A DISSERVICE TO DEPRIVE THEM OF SUCH MAGNIFICENT MOUNTS.”
4. Theresa looks uncertain. “OOOOKAY. SO, HOW DO WE KEEP THEM FROM FOLLOWING US?”
5. William points across the wasteland as they ride. “WE JOURNEY TO THE KEYSTONE OF DESTRUCTION. NO SANE SAPIENT WOULD FOLLOW.”
6. The two ants carry them away from the settlement. Theresa says, “FAN-FREAKING-TASTIC.”
