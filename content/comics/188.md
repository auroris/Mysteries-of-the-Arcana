A story montage follows Chrystalline’s father Quest and his companions, Melody and Kludge. Chrystalline narrates, “The boy grew into a man, gained friends and allies. Together, they embarked on many adventures.” 

The three companions charge into action with swords and crossbow. “Of course, they didn’t always get along.” Quest and Melody argue while Kludge stands between them.
