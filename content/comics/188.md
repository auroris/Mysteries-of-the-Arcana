A story montage follows Chrystalline’s blond, pointed-eared father and his companions. Chrystalline narrates, “The boy grew into a man, gained friends and allies.” He poses with a sword while portraits of a dark-haired man and a red-haired woman float behind him. “Together, they embarked on many adventures.” The three companions charge into action with swords and a firearm. “Of course, they didn’t always get along.” The elf and the red-haired woman argue on opposite sides of a tree while the dark-haired man stands between them.
