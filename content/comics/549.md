1. Kludge pushes a measuring wheel through the darkness while Theresa follows with a sparking blue device. “As you know, the Fool measures only a few square miles in size.”
2. Theresa walks ahead of Kludge. “Contained in those few square miles are the Royal Road, the inn and the shattered remains of a forgotten civilization.” She replies, “I know all this.”
3. Kludge stops and gestures with the measuring device. “Every once in a while, I like to take measurements to update my map of the Fool.” Theresa asks, “Okay?”
4. He catches Theresa by the arm as she walks onward. “This way, we know if the Void has grown any.” She asks, “What’s the Void?”
