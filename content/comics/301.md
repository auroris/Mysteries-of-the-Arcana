1. Under a starry sky, Theresa walks away from a fallen blonde woman as William follows in armor. “Thou seemest troubled.”
2. Theresa looks away. “I’m fine.” William replies, “Actions of battle often seem more worrisome once the fog of war hath lifted.”
3. Theresa looks back toward William. “...Blowing up robots was easy enough, but these... ...things are so much like people.” William says, “’Tis harder when an opponent both speaks and bleeds.”
4. Looking down at the fallen woman, Theresa asks, “What’s their deal?”
