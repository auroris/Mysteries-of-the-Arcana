alt: 'Theresa uses the fallen plant creature’s acid gun to weaken the tunnel ceiling until she blacks out.'

author: 'J Gray'
artist: 'Keira'

1. Theresa picks up the fallen plant creature’s acid gun and aims it upward through the green haze. It sprays with a *PSHHHHH!!* She thinks, “Gotta do this quick.”
2. She sprays acid over a long crack in the tunnel ceiling. The gun goes *PSHHHHH!!* while the dissolving rock makes a *HISSSSSS!!!!*, weakening the ceiling until the passage can collapse.
3. Theresa strains to keep the acid gun raised. Its spray continues with a *SHHHHH!!* She thinks, “Come on!”
4. The cracked ceiling and acid gun darken at the edges of Theresa’s vision as the rock dissolves with a *HISSSSSS!!!!* and the gun continues with a *PSHHHHH!!*
5. Theresa sags over the acid gun as its spray fades to *SHHHHH...* She thinks, “Stupid... thing... takes... forever...”
6. Theresa passes out, and her vision goes completely black.
