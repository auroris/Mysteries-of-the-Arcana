1. Theresa keeps one hand over Chrystalline’s mouth. “I messed up. That makes it my responsibility to...” Behind them, William is pulled straight down with a *WOOSH!*
2. Chrystalline points behind Theresa. Theresa turns to her. “What?”
3. Chrystalline is pulled downward beside Theresa with a *VROOM!* Theresa turns toward the empty place where William stood. “What happened to dog boy?”
4. Green vines whip around Theresa’s ankles with a *FWIP! FWIP!*
5. Theresa looks down at the vines. “Shit.”
6. Theresa is yanked downward in a vertical blur with a *VROOM!*
