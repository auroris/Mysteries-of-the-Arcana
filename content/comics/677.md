1. A green jacket lies on the floor of a crumbling corridor beside a square pillar.
2. The jacket remains where it fell as two approaching impacts sound off panel: *KA-KRASH! KA-KRASH!!*
3. Theresa’s boots enter the corridor and stop beside the jacket. “¡Gracias a Dios! I was worried it would be gone.”
4. Chrystalline reaches down toward the jacket. “Let me get that for you.”
5. Chrystalline hands the jacket to Theresa, who stands with her crutches. “Thanks.”
