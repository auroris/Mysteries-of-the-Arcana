1. Lying on a ridge with binoculars, Theresa says, “THEY’RE COVERING EVERY ENTRANCE.”
2. William appears briefly as the Knight of Wands Tarot card. Theresa asks, “HOW MANY?”
3. William counts on his armored fingers. “IN AND AROUND YOUNDER FACTORY? SIX-HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FOUR. NO OTHER DEFENSES. THEY REALLY DON’T NEED THEM.”
4. Theresa and William look toward the factory.
5. Theresa says, “CRAP, THAT’S A LOT.” William replies, “AYE.”
