1. Lying on a ridge with binoculars, Theresa says, “They’re covering every entrance.”
2. William uses his key ability to learn about the enemy. His Knight of Swords arcana card is visible. Theresa asks, “How many?”
3. William counts on his armored fingers. “In and around younder factory? Six-hundred and sixty-four. No other defenses.” Theresa comments, “They really don’t need them.”
4. Theresa and William look toward the factory.
5. Theresa says, “Crap, that’s a lot.” William replies, “Aye.”
