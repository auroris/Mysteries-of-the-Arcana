1. Theresa crawls across the ground as William’s armored boot enters the foreground.
2. Theresa glances sharply to the side.
3. A loose rope whips around William’s armored ankle with a *FWIP!*
4. Theresa grabs the rope.
5. Theresa yanks it hard with a *YANK!*
6. The rope tightens around William’s boot.
7. The taut rope pulls Theresa toward William.
8. Theresa pins William to the ground and presses a broken stake against his throat. “GIVE ME ONE REASON WHY THIS STAKE SHOULDN’T MEET YOUR BRAIN.”
