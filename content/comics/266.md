1. Freed from the ground, Theresa crouches as an armored figure approaches behind her.
2. Theresa glances sharply back at the figure.
3. She whips the rope and wooden stake still tied to her wrist around the figure’s armored ankle with a *FWIP!*
4. Theresa seizes the rope.
5. She yanks it hard with a *YANK!*
6. The rope tightens around the armored boot.
7. Theresa sweeps the figure off his feet and lunges after him.
8. The opponent is William. Theresa pins him to the ground and presses the stake against his throat. “Give me one reason why this stake shouldn’t meet your brain.”
