1. Chrystalline smiles. “Thank you.”
2. Theresa replies, “Welcome.”
3. Chrystalline touches her chin while Theresa clasps her hands behind her head; neither speaks.
4. At the door, Chrystalline says, “I suppose I should leave you to your work, then.”
5. Theresa reaches after her. “Wait.”
