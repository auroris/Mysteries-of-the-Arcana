1. Armed Red Viquirrels confront the women. Theresa says, “I’m getting tired of this.”
2. A soldier calls into the darkness, “Sir! We caught prisoners, sir!”
3. General Isc says, “Good work, soldier! Let’s line them up against a wall so we can shoot them.”
4. Chrystalline objects, “You’ll do no such thing! We are your guests!”
5. General Isc asks, “Guests?”
6. Chrystalline folds her arms. “Rules of war. You can’t shoot during a cease-fire.”
