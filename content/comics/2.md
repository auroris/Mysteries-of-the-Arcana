An open photo album belonging to Theresa burns around four instant photographs. Her father carries young Theresa piggyback across grass, then teaches her to shoot at a target. A third photograph shows him standing next to a duffel bag and a bus, and the last shows Theresa receiving a folded flag at his military funeral. Theresa narrates, “I didn’t leave a note.”
