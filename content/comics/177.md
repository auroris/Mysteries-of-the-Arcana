1. Theresa grips her gun. “Giant turtle. Good thing or bad thing?”
2. Chrystalline gestures reassuringly. “Good thing. Worry not.”
3. Chrystalline bows before the giant turtle. “We greet you, Knower of Truths.”
4. Knower of Truths answers through the small turtle, “I greet you, last of the dreamers.”
5. The small turtle relays further words. “Speak your quest.”
6. Theresa shades her eyes. “Are we talking to the big turtle? Or the little one?”
