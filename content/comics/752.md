1. Chrystalline and Theresa sit apart on the bed. Theresa hugs her knees to her chest. “A LOT'S... CHANGED SINCE I CAME HERE.” She looks toward Chrystalline. “I'VE CHANGED, BUT YOU NEED TO UNDERSTAND, THIS STILL ISN'T EASY FOR ME.”
2. A close view isolates half of Theresa's troubled face against darkness. “I KNOW THIS IS HARD FOR YOU TO UNDERSTAND, BUT WHERE I COME FROM, WHAT WE HAVE? WHAT WE FEEL TOGETHER? ALL MY LIFE I'VE BEEN TOLD...” Three red inset memories show a woman speaking at a podium, a cleric wearing a cross and stole, and a young man looking back from a crowd. Partly obscured red text behind the first speaker includes “WRITING CAMPAI...” and “SUPPORT... OPPOSITI...” Theresa continues, “THAT THIS IS WRONG.” “THAT THIS IS SINFUL.” “THAT THIS IS DANGEROUS.”
