1. Chrystalline approaches Theresa, who is on her hands and knees. “Are you alright?”
2. Theresa reaches toward the burn across her back. “I just got lasered across the spine. How do you think...”
3. Theresa’s eye widens as she notices movement down the corridor.
4. Theresa pushes Chrystalline down with a *PUSH* “Get down!”
5. They crouch behind a low block while another sentry robot approaches along the corridor. Chrystalline says, “I am capable of independent movement, you know.” Theresa answers, “Shhh!”
