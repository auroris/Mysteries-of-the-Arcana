1. Melody looks up as a large aircraft flies overhead. A circular close view magnifies its underside, where box-topped, spike-shaped drop pods deploy. Chrystalline narrates, “At first, I thought they were bombing us.”
2. Two drop pods detach above the distant Chrystalline and Melody with a *PLOCK! PLOCK!*
3. The drop pods plunge point-first toward the women. Chrystalline narrates, “I wish we had been so fortunate.”
4. A drop pod slams into the ground beside them with a *FWOOM!*, knocking Chrystalline and Melody from their feet.
