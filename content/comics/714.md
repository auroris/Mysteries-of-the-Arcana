1. Still held in the wolf-man's fist, Mandrake looks aside and projects three question marks.
2. The wolf-man points across the meadow toward a distant stone fortress. Mandrake projects her face, an arrow, and the fortress.
3. The wolf-man smiles and touches his chest. Above him, Mandrake's symbols show a woman's face, an arrow, a horseshoe-shaped object, a rain cloud, a wolf in profile, and the smiling wolf's face.
4. The wolf-man rubs his brow while Mandrake projects two rows of symbols: her face, an arrow, and the fortress; then her face, lips, the smiling wolf, a gesturing figure with a trailing wisp, an equals sign, a flying fairy, a rain cloud, the smiling wolf, and a question mark.
5. He looks down at Mandrake in baffled silence as a bright burst beside him contains “...”
6. Mandrake closes her eyes and holds still while her antennae oscillate.
