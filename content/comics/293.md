1. Melody and Kludge sit with drinks in a bubbling outdoor hot tub.
2. Melody looks toward Kludge. “I WORRY ABOUT HER.” Kludge asks, “HMM?”
3. Melody holds her drink against the edge of the tub. “SHE INHERITED IT FROM ME. THIS TENDENCY TO BECOME QUICKLY INFATUATED WITH SOMEONE.” Kludge replies, “AH. CHRYS.”
4. A small stone gargoyle approaches behind Kludge carrying a pitcher. Melody explains, “IT COMES FROM BELONGING TO A CULTURE THAT SPENDS HALF THEIR LIVES PLAYING OUT FAIRY TALES IN DREAMS.” Kludge asks, “SHALLOW BUT INTENSE LOVE AT FIRST SIGHT?”
5. The gargoyle refills Kludge’s glass. Melody says, “ESPECIALLY WHEN CHARMING PRINCES RESCUE US FROM CERTAIN DOOM.” Kludge adds, “OR CHARMING PRINCESSES.”
6. The gargoyle pours Melody another drink. Melody says, “THERESA’S SUCH A BABY BUTCH, SOMETIMES IT CAN BE HARD TO TELL...” Kludge chuckles. “HEH.”
