2. The binocular view sharpens on the windowless facility, a wall-mounted camera, and the patrolling sentry. Theresa assesses it. “Solid architecture. Cameras. A single sentry. Can the outer security truly be so simple?” BbuzZ replies, “Remote. Isolated. Small outpost.” William says, “Aye. Even so, tis unlikely to be so simple as that.”
