1. The travelers appear as dark silhouettes crossing the barren plain. Theresa says, “Weird. Does this place seem, I dunno, any more... real to you guys? You know, sharper? More vivid?”
2. Chrystalline walks behind Theresa as she explains, “Each person who can make use of the Road resonates with one specific Arcana. Not always their own.” Theresa asks, “Oh. What Arcana are we in, anyway?”
3. Chrystalline answers from off panel, “The Devil.” In a close view, Theresa’s eyes widen.
4. Theresa staggers and reaches toward the ground. “I-I need to sit down.”
5. Theresa sits on a rock with her face in both hands. “Oh God.”
