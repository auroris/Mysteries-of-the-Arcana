1. A bolt of pink lightning forks down from a golden vortex and splashes across the darkness.
2. The lightning fades, revealing Mandrake seated atop the living range with the candleholder, chair, and other enchanted objects gathered below the vortex.
3. In a warm wood-paneled room, Mandrake floats toward an elderly woman in a purple robe as the candleholder and range enter below her.
4. The smiling woman takes one of the candleholder's hands while the range watches from behind them.
