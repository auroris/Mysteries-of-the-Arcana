1. Chrystalline works inside the truck’s cab. “Poor Mandrake. Managing the inn all by herself cannot be easy.” Theresa asks from outside, “Now?”
2. Theresa leans into the engine compartment with a wrench. “Not yet. Circe’s really sick, huh?”
3. Reclining in the cab and blowing bubble gum, Chrystalline says, “Yes. Blue tends to her as we speak.” Theresa asks, “Now?”
4. Theresa bends deeper into the engine. “I’ll tell you when. At least it’s just the regulars around us. Kludge, Blue, Surgeon, and that new chick, Ruby. That makes Mandrake’s job easier.”
5. Theresa lets the hood fall with a *WHUMP*. Chrystalline calls from the cab, “Okay! Try it now.”
