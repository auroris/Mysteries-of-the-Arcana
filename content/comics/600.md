1. A caption reads “Later.” Theresa and Chrystalline climb a steep section of the giant tree’s bark. “Are we there yet?” Theresa asks. Chrystalline answers, “Actually, yes.”
2. Theresa helps Chrystalline over the edge. Chrystalline says, “We just need to cross this...”
3. Chrystalline shields her eyes and looks ahead. “Oh!”
4. They stare across a vast battlefield where Red and Grey Viquirrel forces exchange repeated bursts of *TATATAT* across the bark. Theresa says, “Oh. *That* kind of war.” Chrystalline adds, “This just became much more complicated.”
