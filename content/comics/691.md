1. Chrystalline and Theresa sit with steaming mugs at a café table while Mandrake hovers between them. Chrystalline finishes her story. “And we have been the best of friends ever since.”
2. Theresa leans toward Chrystalline. “A candy bar? Really?”
3. Chrystalline rests one hand beneath her chin. “Well, can you deny the power of chocolate?” Theresa looks back at her.
4. Chrystalline waits while Theresa drinks from her mug. “... No.” Mandrake broadcasts a candy bar, its blue wrapper, and an exclamation mark. The closing text reads “The End.”
