Theresa and Chrystalline sit close together on an orange couch. Theresa announces, “WE’RE BACK WITH MORE ARCANA DESCRIPTIONS. WE’LL JUMP RIGHT IN!”

- “PAGE ONE INTRODUCED THE HIEROPHANT, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE UNIVERSE MOST MOTA READERS ARE FROM. AND MY HOME ARCANA, WHICH MAKES IT ALL SORTS OF AWESOME. EXCEPT FOR, YOU KNOW, ME PROBABLY BEING WANTED FOR MURDER THERE.”
- “NEXT, WE VISITED THE FOOL. IT SERVES AS THE CROSSROADS OF THE ARCANA BECAUSE IT IS HOME OF THE ROYAL ROAD. IT IS ALSO THE LOCATION OF CIRCE’S INN AND, THUS, OUR HOME. THE WHOLE ARCANA IS JUST A FEW BLOCKS FULL OF RUINED BUILDINGS. THE INN AND THE ROYAL ROAD ARE THE ONLY THINGS STANDING.”
- “WHO CAN FORGET THE DEVIL? AN ARCANA WHERE A MACHINE INTELLIGENCE SEEKS TO EXTERMINATE ALL ORGANIC LIFE? MY FIRST ADVENTURE WITH CHRYS AND DOG BOY! ALSO THE HOME OF THE SURGEON. CHRYS’S MOM LOST HER ARM AND EYE THERE.”
- “CHAPTER TWO TAKES PLACE ENTIRELY IN THE ACE OF CUPS, AN ARCANA WHERE GIANT TURTLES SWIM UPON EVEN BIGGER TURTLES. IT IS HOME TO THE KNOWER OF TRUTHS. THAT ONE’S ON MY ‘DO NOT EVER VISIT AGAIN’ LIST. LET’S MOVE ON BEFORE I GET MAD AT YOU AGAIN.”
- “A NUMBER OF NEW ARCANA WERE INTRODUCED IN CHAPTER THREE. FIRST, THE LOVERS, A WORLD WITH NO QUANTIFIED SPECIES BECAUSE ALL CREATURES ARE CROSS-FERTILE WITH EACH OTHER. I GOT SWALLOWED BY A GOAT-FROG IN ORDER TO GET ITS BEZOAR IN THAT ONE.”
- “THEN SHE AND WILLIAM TRAVELED TO THE TOWER. WILLIAM’S HOME ARCANA IS A POST-APOCALYPTIC WASTELAND BORN FROM THE IRRATIONAL PURSUIT OF ETERNAL YOUTH. THAT’S THE ONE WITH THE YUPPIE ZOMBIES AND ALL THE ANIMAL PEOPLE.”
- “MEANWHILE, MANDRAKE AND I VISITED THE FOUR OF PENTACLES, AN ARCANA WHERE NOTHING IS FREE. SERIOUS B MOVIE SCIENCE FICTION THEME THING GOING THERE.”
- “MOTHER AND UNCLE KLUDGE VISITED THE QUEEN OF WANDS IN THAT CHAPTER. A WARM, RELAXING, TROPICAL ARCANA. THEY GOT ALL THE LUCK.”
- “WHILE POOR BLUE AND SURGEON ENDED UP IN THE SEVEN OF SWORDS, A WORLD OF IMPULSE AND LITTLE FORETHOUGHT.”

A tiny costumed Theresa concludes, “THAT’S ALL OF THEM! SEE YOU TOMORROW!”
