1. The horned attacker glances aside as Mandrake broadcasts two gray shaker-like containers and a question mark from off panel.
2. Mandrake looks down while green slime holds her arms and torso.
3. The neon jester image shines on the wall.
4. Mandrake looks forward, her red eyes wide.
5. A closer view centers the neon jester’s smiling face.
6. The red-crested attacker grins as Mandrake broadcasts a yellow bottle beside her own face.
7. Green-white lightning strikes the slime-covered Mandrake while both attackers watch.
8. The slime collapses into a small suspended mass between the attackers. Mandrake broadcasts a pair of bent legs and a question mark, then an exclamation mark.
