1. Chrystalline stands pressed against Theresa, who has her arms around Chrystalline’s waist. Theresa asks, “A theory?”
2. Chrystalline says, “Yes. We must be wary of the baleful gaze. After all, it might see me as we travel the Royal Road.” Theresa replies, “Uh-huh. And?”
3. Chrystalline rests her head against Theresa’s shoulder. “Perhaps if we are close... touching as much as possible while using the Royal Road it might mask my presence?”
4. Theresa says, “Fine. Whatever.”
5. Chrystalline smiles up at her. “But watch the hands.”
