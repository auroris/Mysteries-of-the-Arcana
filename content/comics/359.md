1. Chrystalline approaches the truck. “YOUR NEW VEHICLE IS QUITE... MASSIVE. WILL IT REQUIRE MUCH WORK TO RUN PROPERLY?” In an inset close view, Theresa wipes grease from her hands. “DUNNO. KLUDGE IS HELPING ME RESTORE HIM BUT... HE DOESN’T WORK THE WAY A TRUCK SHOULD WORK. EXCEPT THE BATTERY WATER, THERE’S NO LIQUIDS. NO OIL. NO GASOLINE. NO BREAK FLUID. NOT EVEN WINDSHIELD WIPER FLUID.”
2. Chrystalline says, “KLUDGE SAID SOMETHING ABOUT ELECTROSTATIC ENERGY BUT...” Theresa replies, “YEAH. IT’LL TAKE A LOT OF WORK.”
3. They fall silent again.
