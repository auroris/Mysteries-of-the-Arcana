1. Chrystalline leans over the Inn's bar toward Circe. “I need those supplies to rescue my mother!”
2. Circe raises one finger. “Excellent. Hopefully, you won't be an idiot and try to rescue her without them. Wait for your father, Chrys.”
3. William stands beside Circe with a mug in one hand. “Heed Circe's wisdom, Chrystalline, and wait for thy father's return.” “Without his abilities, thou hast little chance for success.”
4. Chrystalline confronts William across the bar. “I suppose you consider him the right man for the job?” William answers, “Aye, just so.”
5. Chrystalline folds her arms while William drinks from his mug. “And you wonder why we are no longer together.”
