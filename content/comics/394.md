1. Theresa and Chrystalline approach a security scanner. Theresa looks down at its mechanism. “I’m always amazed by how much security this place has.”
2. Yellow scanning light passes over them with an *MMMMMMMMMMM*. Chrystalline explains, “Indeed. The Inn’s scanners search for weapons, explosives, and other harmful elements on the way in. Purloined items on the way out.”
3. Facing forward as a security camera looms behind her, Chrystalline continues, “Only full-time residents of the Inn, such as ourselves, are permitted to carry weapons inside. Every portal in or out of the Inn is kept locked at all times. Every room is monitored for fire, carbon monoxide, and weapon’s fire. There are cameras with full color and audio monitoring all public spaces. Detailed profiles are kept on each guest.”
4. Theresa leans into view behind Chrystalline. “Who are you talking to?”
5. Chrystalline points toward the security camera. “The security cameras. Circe reviews the footage obsessively and I wish to show her I remember her boring security lectures.” Theresa answers, “Oh.”
