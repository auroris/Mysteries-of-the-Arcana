Theresa stands in the subway maintenance area with her eyes closed and her pistol lowered at her side. A luminous Arcana card rises behind her, labeled “The Devil.” The card depicts Theresa seated on a wooden, winged throne, with two smaller versions of her chained beside it: one in a black dress and one in a white shirt and black slacks. Theresa narrates, “Suddenly, I knew the robot’s weakness.” “I didn’t understand how or why, but I knew deep down inside, in my very core.” “I knew the same way I knew how to blink or breathe.” “Instinct...” “Epiphany.”
