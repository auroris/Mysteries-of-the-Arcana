1. One escape-pod doorway stands empty. Chrystalline says from off panel, “One escape pod missing. Our thief fled the station.”
2. Mandrake speaks in picture symbols showing Mandrake, Chrystalline, a round destination, and a question mark while Chrystalline thinks.
3. Chrystalline inserts a card into the “5 Credits” slot.
4. Chrystalline opens the remaining pod. “We must follow.”
5. Inside the escape pod, two dirty, stained seats have holes cut through their fabric. The cockpit glass is cracked in several places and repaired with tape, while multicolored wires hang beneath the controls.
6. Chrystalline recoils in shock at the pod’s condition as she climbs inside. Mandrake speaks in picture symbols showing a skull and crossbones, a mousetrap, and an exclamation mark. Chrystalline asks, “Perhaps Circe won’t miss her goods?”
