1. Green lightning strikes a barren plain beneath dark mountains with a *KA-KRASH!*
2. Theresa steadies herself after arriving as lightning flashes behind her with another *KA-KRASH!*
3. Chrystalline shades her eyes and searches the horizon. “Where is William?” Behind her, Theresa touches her lip. “I guess I forgot to visualize him. Oops.”
4. A white bolt drops vertically between them with a *THOOM!*
5. William looms over Theresa. “Thou. Art. Not. Funny!”
