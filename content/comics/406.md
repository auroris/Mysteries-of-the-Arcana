1. Four hands, each bearing a family sigil, reach toward one another. Melody narrates, “They were the least of the four families and in many ways the wisest.”
2. Their bleeding hands close together. “Only by combining their essences could creation last.”
3. Within an explosion of blood and glowing sigils appears a luminous turtle filled with stars. “And, thus, the first Arcana was born.”
