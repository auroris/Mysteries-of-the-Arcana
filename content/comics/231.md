1. Knower of Truths swims through a blue-violet expanse with a forested island on its shell.
2. The view pulls back until the turtle and island are small.
3. Many turtle-islands swim through the same vast expanse.
4. The entire blue-violet realm appears as a bounded cosmic sea while a black dragon, Circe’s unnamed brother, looks over its edge. The closing text reads “THE END.”
