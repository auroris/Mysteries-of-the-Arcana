1. A series of blows strike William’s face, drawing blood.
2. A kick snaps his head backward.
3. The crowd swarms him, punching and kicking from every side.
4. William falls to one knee. A blonde woman says, “SOMEONE’S UP PAST HIS BEDTIME!” A dark-haired woman adds, “I LOVE CHOCOLATE!”
5. William’s sword drops from his hand with a *Cluck!* He weakly says, “I. FEAR. THEE. NOT!”
