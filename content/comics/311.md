1. Chrystalline folds her arms. “RELEASE ME *AT ONCE!*”
2. Featherbeard replies, “AFRAID I *CAN’T* DO THAT, LASS. A *LITTLE BIRDIE* TOLD ME YE’D BE COMING AND THAT YE’D BE WORTH A *HANDSOME* PILE OF CREDITS, TOO!” His tiny shoulder companion echoes, “PILE OF CREDITS!” Chrystalline protests, “YOU *KNEW* I WOULD BE HERE? *IMPOSSIBLE!*”
3. Featherbeard describes Mandrake. “A BLONDE LASS WITH POINTY EARS AND A PURPLE, FLYING DOLL. WHERE BE YER DOLL, ANYWAYS?” The small companion repeats, “WHERE’S THE DOLL?”
4. Chrystalline hesitates. “UMM... I... LOST HER? *IT!* I LOST *IT!*”
5. Featherbeard gestures to armed guards. “WELL, NO MIND. COME ALONG *PEACEFUL-LIKE,* NOW, OR ME BOYS’LL *DROP* YE LIKE A BAD *CREDIT CHIT.*”
