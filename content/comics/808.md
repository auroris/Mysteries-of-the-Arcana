1. The pawnbroker looks back from the shelves. “Yes, price. Everything has a price, mister blacke.”
2. In a close view, he continues, “And I want to be able to leave my shop, instead of sending others in my stead.”
3. William glares. “Truly thou dost babble ceaselessly. I’ll have thy price now, pawnbroker.”
4. The pawnbroker folds one arm across his chest and points toward William’s pendant. “Indeed, mister blacke. My price is that trinket around your neck.”
