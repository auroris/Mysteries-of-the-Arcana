A handheld device labeled “Kludge” displays “Notes on the 3 of C.” Kludge’s notes read:

“Back home, we tend to prize athletic ability above everything else. The sentients of the Three of Coins would never dream of competing at sport. Instead, all their competitions revolve around crafting. Smithing. Weaving. Carving. Coopering (barrel making). That sort of thing.

“There’s no native magic to speak of but the sentients haven’t evolved technology past the point of spinning wheels, looms, and wagons. The very concept of automated assembly of goods is not only foreign to them it is repugnant.

“I commented upon a painting hanging on the wall at a castle I was visiting. My host thanked me for the compliment. He said the painting did a very good job of enhancing the superb craftsmanship of the frame.

“The sentients of the Three of Coins are short, stocky, and hairy. I’d call them dwarves except they are only moderately humanoid. They are quadrupeds with four arms (positioned at twelve, three, six, and nine o’clock) and two faces, one on each side of their head. They talk through whichever mouth happens to be facing you at the time.

“It is common for these folk to carry on two conversations at the same time. One for each mouth.”
