1. Small segmented metal spheres bounce across the floor with a *THUNK* and *THUNK*.
2. Kludge crouches beside Surgeon. “What was that?” A sphere opens with a *WHRR RRRRRR*, then detonates in a *FLASH* and *BANG*.
3. Kludge and Surgeon fall with a *THUD* and *THUD*.
4. The white-haired, mustachioed Baron Sir enters carrying a lantern, flanked by the Red Hair Zombie and Blue Hair Zombie.
