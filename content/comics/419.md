1. Hundreds of tiny colored rings mingle across a black field. Melody narrates, “All those lives on all those Arcana, their dreams rising and colliding.”
2. Green, brown, blue, and yellow spheres press around a growing red center. “Twining. Merging.”
3. The red sphere swells between them. “Gestating.”
4. Melody narrates, “Giving birth.” A pale round form bursts free with a multicolored *POP*.
