1. Her father asks, “TELL ME, HOW MANY TIMES HAVE YOU DREAMWALKED SINCE LAST I SAW YOU?”
2. Chrystalline counts on her fingers. “THREE... NO, FOUR TIMES. WHY?” He asks, “FROM WHERE TO WHERE?”
3. Chrystalline traces glowing Tarot sigils in the air. “FOOL TO HIEROPHANT. HIEROPHANT TO DEVIL. DEVIL TO HIEROPHANT. HIEROPHANT TO FOOL.”
4. He asks, “DID YOU NOTICE ANYTHING UNUSUAL ABOUT THE TRANSIT?” She replies, “NO, BUT DURING MOST OF THEM I WAS RATHER PREOCCUPIED. FATHER, WHAT IS GOING ON?”
