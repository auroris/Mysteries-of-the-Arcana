1. Theresa rises from the bed and scans the room. “Umm...hola?”
2. She waves a hand in front of the spacesuit's visor. “Okay, girl. Don't panic. It's just a statue or something... A weird, freaky statue.”
3. Theresa grips the edge of the glowing bathtub and peers toward it. “Don't mind me, okay? I'm just gonna peek in the tub...”
4. Looking down into the liquid, Theresa recoils. “What the fuck?!?”
5. The injured Chrystalline floats beneath the blue liquid as bright ripples spread across its surface.
