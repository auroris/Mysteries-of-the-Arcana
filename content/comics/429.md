1. On an alien landscape, a winged newcomer grapples with a robed family ruler. Melody narrates, “They could not be ended but they could be captured.”
2. The newcomer forces the ruler into a glowing opening in the ground. “Locked in the center of their own Arcana.”
3. The ruler lies suspended inside golden crystal. “Jailed for eternity.”
