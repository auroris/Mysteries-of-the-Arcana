1. Chrystalline bends over and reaches toward the path as her cloak whips away with a *WIPE*. Knower of Truths asks through the small turtle, “And where do you think you are going?”
2. Chrystalline looks back. “Home?”
3. Chrystalline stands with her arms crossed while the small turtle faces her. Knower of Truths says through it, “You still have a quest to complete, Last of the Dreamers. Tame your cloak.”
4. The cloak pulls Chrystalline down onto her hands and knees with a *WHUMP!*
5. Chrystalline lies face-down on the stone path. “Fuck.”
