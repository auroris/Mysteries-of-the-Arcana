2. Chrystalline looks back. “Home?”
3. Chrystalline stands with her arms crossed while the small turtle faces her. Knower of Truths says through it, “You still have a quest to complete, Last of the Dreamers. Tame your cloak.”
4. Chrystalline collapses to her hands and knees with a *WHUMP!*
5. From high above, the hapless Last of the Dreamers remains bowed on her hands and knees while the small turtle crawls away. “Fuck.”
