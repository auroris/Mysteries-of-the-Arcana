1. Masked, uniformed figures gather around a large saucer-shaped machine inside the factory.
2. Elsewhere, Chrystalline speaks to Mandrake. “...AND WHILE YOU FLY ABOUT DISTRACTING THE PIRATES, I WILL SEEK A MEANS OF ESCAPE.”
3. Mandrake answers in picture symbols showing “10,” a hammer striking a shape, a skull, and an exclamation mark. Chrystalline smiles. “YOU’RE QUITE AGILE, MANDRAKE! THEY WON’T HIT YOU!”
4. Mandrake responds with a prohibition sign and an exclamation mark. Chrystalline looks uncertain. “MANDRAKE...?”
5. Mandrake angrily speaks in picture symbols showing a prohibition sign, Chrystalline’s face, a die, another prohibition sign, Chrystalline’s face, a blue spotted rectangle, and an exclamation mark.
