1. Masked, uniformed grey aliens gather around Chrystalline’s saucer.
2. In the cockpit, Chrystalline speaks to Mandrake. “...And while you fly about distracting the pirates, I will seek a means of escape.”
3. Mandrake answers in picture symbols showing “10,” a blaster, an exclamation mark, an image of Mandrake filled with holes, and an exclamation mark. Chrystalline smiles. “You’re quite agile, Mandrake! They won’t hit you!”
4. Mandrake responds with a prohibition sign and an exclamation mark. Chrystalline looks uncertain. “Mandrake...?”
5. Mandrake angrily speaks in picture symbols showing a prohibition sign, Chrystalline’s face, a pair of dice, another prohibition sign, Chrystalline’s face, a blueprint plan, and an exclamation mark.
