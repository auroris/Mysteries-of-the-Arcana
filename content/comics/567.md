1. Chrystalline gestures to herself. “I will go as well.”
2. Melody objects, “That would be a bad idea.” Chrystalline snaps, “And why is that? I rescued you didn’t I?”
3. Chrystalline glares aside as Theresa tells her, “Chrystalline! *Think!*”
4. Theresa stands between the seated women. “Kludge and your mom are going because of their experience.”
5. Theresa continues, “William’s the muscle. Mandrake’s the one who knows the way since she’s been there before.”
6. She concludes, “We’re not going because you’re a target and the inn’s the safest place for you.”
