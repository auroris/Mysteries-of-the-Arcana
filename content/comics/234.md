1. A caption reads “Not so long ago...” At the Inn, Theresa perches on a ladder while William leans on the bar. Melody, Kludge, and Chrystalline sit at a table. “Why are we here? Why does Circe need all of us?” Theresa asks the room.
2. Chrystalline hunches over the table, trying to make herself look small as she begins to answer. “Well... every once in a while...”
3. Theresa’s eyes flash angrily.
4. Uninterested in Chrystalline’s answer, Theresa turns to William and asks him directly, “Why does Circe need all of us?” William throws up his hands. “Put me not in the midst of thy quarrel!”
