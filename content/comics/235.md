1. Circe appears beside Theresa. “I will explain.” Theresa recoils. “Gaaah!!”
2. Theresa and William stare at Circe. Theresa asks, “Where did you come from!?”
3. Circe speaks with her eyes closed. “This Inn serves as the crossroads of the Arcana.”
4. In a close view, Circe continues, “You sleep here, eat here, keep your stuff here. Now...”
5. Circe flicks a long paper bill across Theresa’s face. “...The bill has come due!”
