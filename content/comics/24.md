1. Melody calls out to Chrystalline.
2. Chrystalline lies on the ground and traces travel sigils with one hand as a BioHunter approaches.
3. Melody blows a song into a whistle, and glowing musical notes form around her. Chrystalline narrates, “Mother's song-magic bought us time.”
4. Melody's song magic expands into a bright circular shield, holding the BioHunters at its perimeter. Inside the shield, Chrystalline continues drawing the glowing travel sigils on the ground. She narrates, “Our escape, however, was up to me.”
