1. William grips the pendant at his chest and gestures with his other hand. “’TIS NOT MINE TO GIVE, AND EVEN STILL HOW SHALL I RETURN TO YON FOOL.”
2. In a close view, the pawnbroker smiles. “THAT’S NOT MY PROBLEM, ESPECIALLY IF YOU REALLY DO WANT THIS.”
3. William looks toward him in profile. “TRULY I DO BUT...”
4. The pawnbroker stares back. “NO. NO EXCUSES, NO ARGUMENTS. DECIDE. NOW.”
