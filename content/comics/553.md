1. Theresa reaches toward the stark white boundary of the Void. “It’s so white it’s like... nothing.” Kludge catches her wrist. “Don’t touch it.”
2. She pulls her hand back. Kludge explains, “Anything that touches the Void vanishes. Poof. Gone forever.”
3. Kludge holds up the measuring wheel’s handle. “My theory, to put it simply, is that we and the Arcana are reality. The Void is anti-reality.”
4. In a close view, he continues, “The Void completely surrounds the Fool but you don’t notice it until you’re just inches away from it.” His equipment sounds *BWEEP BWOOOP*.
5. Kludge strikes the handheld control with a *KTHWAK*. “Work you damn thing. Melody thinks the Void is the space between the Arcana but I haven’t seen any evidence of that.”
