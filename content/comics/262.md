1. Theresa wakes with blurred vision. A vague shape extends beyond her raised hand. “Mmm?”
2. Her vision clears, revealing a rope tied around her wrist and secured to a wooden stake. “Huh?”
3. Theresa makes a fist and tests the rope and stake. Neither yields. “What the hell?”
4. Theresa looks around and discovers that she is tied spread-eagle beside a campfire, with each wrist and boot secured to a separate stake. “Crap.”
