1. A dark-skinned hand and a bloodied skeletal hand reach toward a glowing blue void surrounded by faint symbols. Melody narrates, “THE VOID WAS *EVERYTHING*.”
2. The void hangs alone against a nearly white field. “THE VOID WAS *NOTHING*.”
3. The void resembles a small blue eye shedding a tear. “THE VOID WAS *LONELY*.”
