1. Chrystalline points at Mister Mist. “I don’t *know* you.”
2. Mister Mist leans close. “Oh, but I know you, Chrystalline Questchild.”
3. He puts an arm around Ead. “Just as I know my friends, the Grey Clan! I couldn’t just leave them to the deprivations of the Red Clan, right Ead?”
4. Ead replies, “Yes! Without our friend Mister Mist the Reds would have killed us all!”
5. Mister Mist asks, “And what kind of monster would allow that?”
