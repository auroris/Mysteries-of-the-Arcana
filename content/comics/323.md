1. Theresa climbs to the top of the ridge with binoculars. “Right. Let’s find us some monster making goo.”
2. Looking through the binoculars, she says, “Getting inside’s gonna be next to impossible, but this was a factory, so maybe...”
3. A red freight train crosses the industrial grounds, with armored vehicles parked alongside it. Theresa says, “Bingo!”
4. Theresa scrambles down the ridge. “Worth checking out.”
5. William follows her while Theresa calls, “Let’s go!”
