1. In an inset close view, the cassette player makes a *CLICK* as Chrystalline approaches Theresa. Theresa says, “So...”
2. Rubbing the back of her neck, Theresa asks, “Did you... um... get everything? From Circe’s list, I mean.”
3. Chrystalline replies, “Oh, yes! We had to deal with pirates and ended up dangling a little thief by his ankle over a ledge but... yes. Mission accomplished. Absolutely. Yes.”
4. They stand in awkward silence while Theresa studies her wrench.
