1. Chrystalline says, “Words matter, Theresa. Even in war.”
2. Theresa answers with a *=>SIGH<=*.
3. Theresa holds the small stone in her palm. Chrystalline says, “It’s like this thing.”
4. Theresa asks, “You said this was a word of power but you know what it really is?” Chrystalline answers, “A rock.”
5. Theresa raises it. “Just a rock that’s too damned heavy in my pocket.” Chrystalline reacts with a *=>GASP<=*.
6. The stone drops with a huge *RUMBLE*.
