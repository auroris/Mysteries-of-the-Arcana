2. Chrystalline leans toward her. “Ready for our date?”

4. William steps between them. “Chrystalline. Man-woman. Best hurry or thou wilt be late.”
5. William walks away across the inn’s lobby while Theresa and Chrystalline sigh. “*Sigh*”
