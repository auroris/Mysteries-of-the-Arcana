1. Held by the collar, Kludge says, “That’s why you look familiar! You’re from the Knight of Swords, aren’t you?”
2. Baron Sir asks, “What?”
3. Kludge grins. “They really love their puzzles there.” Baron Sir stammers, “P-puzzles??”
4. Kludge gestures eagerly. “I know a *great* puzzle. A real head scratcher! Wanna hear it?” An unidentified source makes a *THUNK*.
5. Baron Sir looks tempted. “H-hear it? Y-your p-p-puh-puzzle?”
