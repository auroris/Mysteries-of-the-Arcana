1. Kludge steeples his fingers across the table from Theresa. “Even here, the students are smart asses.” Theresa holds her blue mug. “Sorry. Go on... professor.”
2. Kludge folds his hands beneath his chin. “Where was I?” He turns back toward Theresa. “Oh yes. Names. People tend to call their home universe simply ‘the universe.’ After all, they only have evidence of a single reality. We who travel between universes, however, need more precise terminology.”
3. Blue in its spacesuit, a red dragon, several small colorful creatures, and other beings occupy the Inn around Theresa and Kludge. Kludge explains, “The one thing every universe has in common, for reasons open to debate, is the Tarot. Because of this, we've borrowed the Tarot's nomenclature. The collective term for all these universes, for example, would be Arcana. Each individual Arcana is named after a separate Tarot card.” Theresa responds, “Wait. My priest always says stuff like Tarot cards are the work of the Devil. If that's the one thing every universe has in common... then... where does God fit into all this?”
4. Kludge gestures toward the bar behind Theresa. “Well, the mythological, that is to say the theological implications are such that, um, you see... Oh! Look over there! Chrys is arguing with Circe!” Theresa turns. “Buh?”
5. Kludge vanishes from his chair with a *ZWIP!* Theresa remains at the table holding her blue mug. “...Jerk.”
