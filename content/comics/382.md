1. Theresa, drawn as a small bespectacled figure before a green field of symbols, introduces the question: “XADE WANTS TO KNOW THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN USING THE ROYAL ROAD AND DREAMWALKING.” A larger figure in dark glasses and a white coat reaches toward a wall of symbols.
2. The white-coated figure touches the wall. “THE ROYAL ROAD IS A DEVICE, SORT OF LIKE AN ELEVATOR. IF YOU’RE A KEY, YOU CAN ‘PRESS THE RIGHT BUTTON’ AND ACTIVATE IT TO TAKE YOU AND YOUR FRIENDS WHERE YOU WANT TO GO.”
3. Theresa lowers her sunglasses and admits, “DREAMWALKING, MEANWHILE... ... I HAVE NO IDEA. CHRYS, WHAT THE HELL IS DREAMWALKING?”
4. Chrystalline, dressed in white, raises one finger. “ELEMENTARY, MY DEAR THERESA! IN DREAMWALKING EVERY ARCANA HAS A FORMULA. AN ADDRESS, IF YOU WILL. YOU START WITH THE ADDRESS OF YOUR STARTING ARCANA AND THEN ALTER IT, THROUGH WILLPOWER, TO THE ADDRESS OF THE ARCANA YOU WISH TO TRAVEL TO. DOING SO CORRECTLY WILL TRANSPORT YOU, AND ANYONE YOU ARE TOUCHING. SIMPLE, REALLY.”
5. Theresa replies from the lower corner, “YEAH. SIMPLE.” Chrystalline adds, “OF COURSE, ONLY DREAMERS CAN DO IT...” Theresa finishes, “THEY’RE VERY SPECIAL SNOWFLAKES.”
