1. Theresa and Chrystalline continue up the stairs. Theresa asks, “Are you sure he won't squeal?” Chrystalline answers without looking back, “He made a promise. His honor will not allow him to betray it.”
2. Chrystalline rests one hand on Theresa's shoulder and smiles knowingly. “Trust me. I know him well.” Theresa looks aside. “He's a pig. Why the hell would you want to know him well?”
3. Theresa turns toward Chrystalline, suddenly suspicious. “Wait... how do you know him so well?”
4. The women exchange a silent look; Chrystalline rests one hand on her hip.
5. Theresa sticks out her tongue while Chrystalline smiles. Theresa: “Oh. Ick!”
