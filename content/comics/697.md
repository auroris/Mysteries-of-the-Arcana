1. Kietra and Aleena twist and duck between red energy bolts while firing back. Seven blasts sound in rapid succession: *KZZAT*, *KZZAT*, *KZZAT*, *KZZAT*, *KZZAT*, *KZZAT*, *KZZAT*.
2. Aleena keeps her pistol raised as bolts pass above and in front of her with two more *KZZAT*s. “How many did you count?”
3. Kietra glances aside with both pistols raised as two bolts cross the panel with two *KZZAT*s. “Not enough.”
4. Aleena looks toward her while another bolt passes with a *KZZAT*. “Not enough for what?”
5. Kietra smiles as two more bolts pass with two *KZZAT*s. “A fair fight.”
