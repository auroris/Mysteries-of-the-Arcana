1. Theresa crouches beside the turtle as Chrystalline points at its shell with the gong mallet. “The ‘carvings’ occur naturally. Those sigils represent the four suits of the minor arcana.” Four circular close views show the symbols as she identifies them: “Wands.” “Cups.” “Pentacles.” “Swords.”
2. Chrystalline holds up the mallet. “In a young turtle, the four elements balance almost perfectly. As they age, this changes.”
3. Theresa looks up. “Changes how?”
4. Chrystalline strikes the gong with a *BONG!!*
5. Chrystalline smiles. “You’ll see.”
