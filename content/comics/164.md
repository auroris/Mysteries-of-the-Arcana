1. The Deliverer accelerates into the sky over dark mountains. Three inset views track the aircraft shrinking into the distance. The Surgeon asks, “[QUERY] The biologicals calculate an improvement in the odds for survival with the successful execution of this plan?” Theresa replies, “Um, yeah.” The Surgeon follows up, “[FOLLOWUP QUERY] How?” “You’re just gonna have to trust me, okay?”
2. The Surgeon’s display reads “01:45” as Theresa faces the machine. “[ADMISSION] This unit possesses little choice. All other options conclude in unavoidable termination.” She asks, “You’re part of this machine intelligence thing, right?”
3. Theresa leans toward the Surgeon, her eyes wide. “Why are you helping us? Why does it matter if you’re blown up?”
