1. William activates his armor’s electrical cleaning discharge. Blue electricity crackles over the bloodstained armor with *Clik HSSSSSS* while Theresa retrieves her pistol. William asks, “Pray tell..." Theresa finishes: "What the hell that was? Their weakness.”
2. Theresa checks her pistol. “Turns out these things are vulnerable to germs. But only germs from outside Arcana, and only on the inside.”
3. William looks toward their remaining ant mount. “I see. So, given a choice of possible infection vectors...”
4. William gestures while Theresa scowls. “...Thou chose to kiss the blonde, female horror?” Theresa snaps, “Shut up, dog boy.”
