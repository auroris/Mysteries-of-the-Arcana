1. The obese grey alien shopkeeper spreads his arms in welcome. “Welcome to Uncle Mork’s! Whatcha’ll need?”
2. Chrystalline scratches the back of her head. “...Greetings. Could you tell us when the next transport to the Galaxria arrives?”
3. Uncle Mork leans on the counter. “Why, sure! Weekly shuttle’ll pop by in three days’ time.”
4. Mandrake speaks in picture symbols showing an ellipsis, a sun, a sun at the horizon, and an exclamation mark. Chrystalline looks startled. “Nothing sooner? We must return to the Galaxria quickly or...”
5. Uncle Mork gives a thumbs-up. “Lucky for you I got me a nice selection of gently used spacecraft! Well, some spacecraft.”
6. Uncle Mork presses a control beneath the counter with a *CLICK*. “Just one, actually, but she’s a beaut!”
