1. Blood runs from the watery figure’s mouth. Melody narrates, “PAIN.”
2. A single drop of blood falls. “LOSS.”
3. The drop hits the ground and splashes. “SURPRISE.”
4. A pile of strange new beings combines traits of sharks, crocodiles, insects, fish, reptiles, frogs, birds, and an octopus. Blood pools beneath them. “DISCOVERY.”
