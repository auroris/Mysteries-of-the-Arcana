1. Chrystalline says, “For... for years we have sought and struggled to discover something of our people. Their culture. Their history. Their fate. Now, though...”
2. She lowers her head. “...Now, though...”
3. Her father says, “Now, it seems the price of that knowledge might be our destruction. Yes.”
4. He explains, “When it became clear that something was seeking me I decided to, as Kludge says, lay low. I noticed the baleful gaze on me was less intense during travel to and from the minor Arcana.” Chrystalline asks, “So, you hid here? Playing prisoner for these pirates?”
