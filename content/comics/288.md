1. The female horror shoves Theresa away while Theresa keeps hold of William's sword hilt. Female Horror: “How about some cool, refreshing orange juice?”
2. Theresa tumbles across the ground with a *SHUFFL*.
3. The female horror winds up for another swing while Theresa pushes herself upright and reaches for William’s sword hilt. The Female Horror encourages, “Maybe you’ll win miss congeniality!” Theresa urges William's sword, “Come on... work!”
4. The female horror’s body rapidly begins to melt. “I love shoes!”
5. Theresa activates William’s black blade with a *Click* and *BZZZZZ!*, looking accomplished. “Yes! Eeww... but yes!”
