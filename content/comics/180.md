1. Theresa and Chrystalline walk across the wet surface of Knower of Truths’s shell. Chrystalline looks back at Theresa. “Worry not. We shall discover your quest.” Theresa replies, “Eh. How bad can a quest in a clothes store be?”
2. Chrystalline stops and turns. “Store?” Theresa raises one hand. “You said...”
3. Theresa recalls Chrystalline saying, “I must fetch a new cloak. Please, come with me!”
4. Chrystalline explains, “Oh! No. I seek a living cloak. I must tame it, not purchase it.”
5. Chrystalline walks ahead. “A shop atop a giant turtle. Whoever heard of such a fanciful thing?”
6. Theresa walks ahead. “It isn’t any more ‘fanciful’ than taming a living piece of cloth! Stupid blonde.”
