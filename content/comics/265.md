1. A close view shows the rope looped around Theresa’s fist.
2. Theresa’s thumb pins the rope against her hand with a *THUP!*
3. Theresa kneels between the two stakes tethered to her ankles and pulls both ropes taut.
4. The stakes shake in the ground with *RATTLE* *RATTLE* *RATTLE* as the ropes pull taut.
5. One wooden stake splits under the tension with a *SNISH!*
6. The other stake splits with a second *SNISH!*
