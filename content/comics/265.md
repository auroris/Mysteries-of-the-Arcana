1. A close view shows one wrist rope looped around Theresa’s fist.
2. Theresa pins the rope against her palm with her thumb. *THIP!*
3. With her feet beneath her, Theresa pushes herself upright and pulls both wrist ropes taut.
4. The two stakes shake in the ground with *RATTLE* *RATTLE* *RATTLE*.
5. One wooden stake tears free of the ground with a *SNISH!*
6. The other stake tears free with a second *SNISH!*
