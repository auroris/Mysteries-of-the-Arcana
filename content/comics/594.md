1. Theresa and Chrystalline walk arm in arm through the flowering garden. Humanoid figures made of foliage stand among the trees, holding red masks and flowers.
2. Theresa notices a flying masklike flower and looks sideways.
3. Her face and the surrounding flowers distort into overlapping, multiple-eyed shapes.
4. Theresa pulls away from Chrystalline and raises her hands in alarm. “What the hell is going on?”
