Theresa falls headfirst through streaming gray clouds with her arms spread and the feather in one hand. The Surgeon tumbles below her, and a blue oval shines far beneath them. Theresa narrates, 

“I had zero chance of beating the clock to the ground. I was okay with that. 

I didn’t want to kill myself anymore, but I didn’t mind dying. 

I’d saved lives. 

I’d done good.”
