1. Theresa follows Chrystalline through the room. Chrystalline folds her arms. “The rest of the tale, you know.” Theresa gestures toward her. “But why come to my, um, Arcana? Why not go straight there?”
2. Chrystalline approaches a desk and takes up a quill. “We had originally traveled to that Arcana from your own.” “Formulating a return trip takes less time.”
3. Chrystalline begins writing on a parchment. “You destroyed the robot when I could not.” “I know we barely know each other, but...” In a circular close view, her expression tightens. “Please. Please help me rescue my mother.”
4. Theresa pauses with one hand at her chin. Behind her, a muted memory shows her father's military funeral. In a circular close view, Theresa looks toward Chrystalline. “I'm gonna need my gun.”
