1. The female horror walks behind Theresa. “HONEY! NOT IN FRONT OF THE KIDS!”
2. A glowing memory of Theresa being kissed appears before her, marked “15.” Theresa thinks, “WEAKNESS. WEAKNESS. COME ON...”
3. In a close view, Theresa’s eye widens. “YOU’RE KIDDING.”
4. The female horror smiles and adjusts her headband.
5. Theresa grabs the woman’s head and kisses her.
