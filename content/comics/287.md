1. Blinded by her sweatband and oblivious to the problem, the female horror walks behind Theresa. “Honey! Not in front of the kids!”
2. Theresa consults her power. The Devil card, marked “15,” appears behind her as she thinks, “Weakness. Weakness. Come on...”
3. In a close view, Theresa’s eye widens. “You’re kidding.”
4. The female horror smiles and lifts the sweatband from her eyes.
5. Theresa grabs the horror’s head and kisses her.
