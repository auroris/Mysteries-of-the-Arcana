1. Aleena raises four gloved fingers. “BY THE NUMBERS. READY?”
2. A small screen displays a green eye beneath a targeting reticle. Kietra replies, “READY.”
3. Aleena counts, “ONE.” Kietra presses the weapon's red trigger with a *CLICK*.
4. A pink energy shot strikes Captain Featherbeard between the eyes. Aleena counts, “TWO.”
5. Kietra and Aleena face each other inside the slingshot chamber as vertical light surrounds their legs. Aleena counts, “THREE.”
6. Seen through the chamber window, both women begin to disappear into the vertical light. Aleena counts, “FOUR.”
