A handheld device labeled “Kludge” displays “Notes on the Fool.” Kludge’s notes read:

“After nearly a month of work, I’ve determined that the entire universe is no more than five square miles in size.

“Most of the Fool is ruined and urban with evidence of buildings, roads, and vehicles. The Royal Road sits exactly in the center of the arcana.

“Melody suggests that the Fool used to be much larger and teeming with life. She believes the activation of the Royal Road somehow destroyed everything but it’s immediate surroundings. No light equals no plants. No plants equals no food. The natives who survived the blast must have either escaped or died out.

“The only intact building in the Fool is the Inn. No one alive remembers when Circe opened the Inn and she won’t give me any details. I think she gets off on being so damned mysterious.

“I’m at a loss to explain how a universe this small has either an atmosphere or gravity.”
