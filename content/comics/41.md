1. Theresa and Chrystalline are small silhouettes at the Inn's lit window, surrounded by darkness. Theresa says, “It's so dark out there. Can't see squat. Except for that road thing. Whatever it is.”
2. Theresa leans through the window while Chrystalline stands behind her. “It is. The Fool has neither sun nor stars to provide light.”
3. Theresa and Chrystalline turn from the open window. “The Fool? That's the name of this Arcana?” William answers from off panel, “Aye, and ’tis a fitting name.”
4. William stands before them in full white armor with a sword at his hip. “For tonight it shall serve as the origin of what is surely a most foolhardy venture.”
