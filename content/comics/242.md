1. A caption reads “Meanwhile, in the Four of Coins...” The wheel-shaped space station GALAXRIA orbits an orange planet, its name glowing in blue letters between its two rings.
2. In a futuristic market, Chrystalline reads from a list while Mandrake sits beside her. “One fusion powered e-reader. Check. Five canisters dehydrated water. Check. Fifty pounds soylant protein shake mix. Check. Ten pounds pureed slan brain. Check. Twenty ears of triffid. Check. One spool of dragon thread?” Mandrake answers with a green check-mark symbol. In an inset close view, Chrystalline touches one finger to her lips. “We’ve acquired everything. Let us return home.”
