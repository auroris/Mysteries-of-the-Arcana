1. Melody faces the audience. “I SHARED THE STORY!” Theresa, Chrystalline, and William answer with the crowd, “WE HAVE LISTENED!”
2. Theresa and Chrystalline sit together as Melody calls from off panel, “REMEMBER THE STORY!”
3. Melody raises both arms before the flames. The audience answers, “WE HAVE LEARNED!”
4. Melody grips the edges of her hood. “SHARE THE STORY!” The audience responds, “HONOR THE STORYTELLER!”
5. Melody lowers her hood over her head. “THANK YOU! GO IN PEACE, FRIENDS.”
