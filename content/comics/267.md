1. Pinned beneath Theresa, William looks up at her. “T’would be a pity to ruin our perfect escape.”
2. Theresa stares down at him. “What?”
3. Theresa keeps the stake against William’s throat. William looks at the guard he knocked out and says, “We must make haste...”
4. An orange-furred guard lies bound and unconscious beside a tree. William continues, “... Before the guard I laid low awakens.”
5. Theresa leans close over William. He says, “On my honor, I wish you no harm.”
