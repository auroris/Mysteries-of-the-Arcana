1. Chrystalline helps Theresa sit up after the fall. Theresa scowls. “When we get back I’m so going to rip off her wings.” Chrystalline replies, “Be nice.”
2. Theresa, Chrystalline, and William face a magenta-lit gateway. Theresa asks, “What’s this road thing anyway?” William answers, “The gateway betwixt Arcana.”
3. The three walk between enormous cyan and magenta columns of light capped by luminous rings. Theresa looks up. “This looked a lot smaller from up in the tower. Less freaky, too.”
