1. Rows of dark figures divide from one into four. Melody narrates, “IN DESTRUCTION CAME CREATION. WHERE ONCE THERE WAS *ONE*, NOW THERE WERE FOUR.”
2. A circular tableau depicts four crowned pairs: an armored stone figure and a green plantlike figure labeled “MATTER”; a blue watery figure and an orange-haired merperson labeled “EMOTION”; a pale glowing figure and a fiery figure labeled “SPIRIT”; and a dark shadowy figure and a white ethereal figure, both holding swords, labeled “PSYCHE.”
