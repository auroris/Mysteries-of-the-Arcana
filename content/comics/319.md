Seen through binoculars, three scenes unfold in a shabby settlement:

1. Two men play cards on crates outside a damaged building. Theresa says from off panel, “NO.”
2. Two women sit on a torn green couch before a broken television. Theresa says, “NO.”
3. A family eats at a table while one woman carries an enormous insect-like creature and a baby raises its arms. Theresa says, “ABSOLUTELY NO.”
