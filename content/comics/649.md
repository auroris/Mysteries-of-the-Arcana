1. The women run through a blur of green forest. Theresa asks, “Where are we going? The on-ramp to the Royal Road’s the other way!”
2. Chrystalline pulls Theresa along with a *YANK*. “My father’s cache! I believe it to be nearby.” Theresa grunts, “Oof!”
3. Theresa protests, “Are you *nuts?* We’ve got to get out of here!”
4. Behind them, the pursuing Void Monster leaves a widening trail of white emptiness that consumes the trees. Chrystalline says, “We can’t fight that thing!”
