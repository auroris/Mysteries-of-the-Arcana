1. Triple Signpost says, “I see you, Theresa Mariochild.” Theresa answers, “Sweet?”
2. Triple Signpost warns, “Beware! I see grey and red at war!” Chrystalline replies, “The Viquirrels? Surely not! With snow on the ground they should all be hibernating.”
3. Triple Signpost says, “I see the acorn groves gone!”
4. Triple Signpost says, “I see blood spilled in rage!”
5. Triple Signpost says, “I see war spreading to the roots! The tree withers and dies!”
