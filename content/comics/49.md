1. Chrystalline smiles up while jumping feet-first into the darkness, holding the feather aloft. “The tighter you grip the feather, the slower you fall!”
2. William follows her into the darkness.
3. Theresa leans into the open window while Mandrake hovers behind her. “There's no way...”
4. Mandrake uses pink magic around Theresa's shoes to shove her through the opening. Theresa pitches forward. “Oomph!”
5. Theresa falls into the dark, screaming, “Aieeeeeeeeee!!!!” Mandrake projects a yellow smiling face followed by a green four-leaf clover: “Good luck!”
