1. Theresa asks, “The turtle called you the last of the dreamers. What’s that mean?”
2. Chrystalline gestures as she explains, “The dreamers are a mythical race. Legends tell of their ability to walk between the Arcana without using the Royal Road.” Theresa says, “Sounds familiar.”
3. Chrystalline continues, “The dreamers vanished long ago. None living can claim to have met one. Except...” Theresa asks, “Except...?”
4. Chrystalline looks back over her shoulder. “Except me... and my father.”
