1. Mandrake’s head vanishes behind William with a *SNAP*. He smiles. “Delightful.”
2. Mandrake’s upper body disappears with another *SNAP*. William continues, “Our survival dependeth upon three impulsive females.”
3. Her lower body vanishes with a *SNAP*. William makes a *SNORT*.
4. Mandrake’s remaining hand disappears with a final *SNAP*. William walks away. “At least they art competent.”
