1. Chrystalline works the controls as a tentacle curls around the cockpit. “Come on. Work!”
2. A mechanical tentacle clamps around the saucer with a *CLING!* “Damnation.”
3. More tentacles engulf the captured craft. Chrystalline says, “I think we’ll be late with our delivery to Circe.” Mandrake speaks in picture symbols showing Circe’s face and an angry red face.
4. The tentacles pull the saucer toward the larger ship. Chrystalline insists, “I am not afraid of Circe.” Mandrake speaks in picture symbols showing a pair of pants on fire and an exclamation mark.
5. The captured saucer is drawn into the ship with a *SHHHHHHHH* and *CLUNK*.
