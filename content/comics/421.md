1. Two members of the four families watch an indistinct newcomer emerge through white folds. Melody narrates, “THE FOUR FAMILIES WATCHED THE NEWCOMERS.”
2. The Wanderer turns away, revealing the stars across its garment. “THE NEWCOMERS HAD SUCH MYSTERY.”
3. A pale hand grips a rainbow-colored knife with an ornate gold hilt. “SUCH POWER.”
4. The Wanderer looks down at its own body. An inset shows drops of blood falling and splashing. “SUCH STERILITY. FOR THE NEWCOMERS COULD NOT CREATE.”
