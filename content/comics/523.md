1. Baron Sir enters a vast cavern filled with golden treasure. In an inset, he recites, “‘The vile beast has hidden the artifacts you seek inside her treasure horde. Can you find them?’ Quite the puzzle.” In the main view, he adds, “Quite the puzzle, indeed.”
2. Blue, a liquid being in a white spacesuit, holds up blue-stained hands. “My recovering patient is. Please to leave now, okay yes?” Baron Sir says, “Ah, the babbling goo. How delightful...”
3. Baron Sir repeatedly slashes Blue’s liquid arms. *SLISH SLASH SLISH SLASH SLISH*.
4. Blue’s severed liquid reforms around the suit.
