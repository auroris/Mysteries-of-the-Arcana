1. Chrystalline writes at a desk with a *SCRITCH SCRITCH SCRITCH* while Theresa leans over the top. “What's that you're writing?”
2. A close view shows Chrystalline's gloved hand placing the parchment in a capsule. “A note to a friend. We still need supplies and she will ensure that we are properly equipped for the journey.”
3. The pneumatic message tube whisks the capsule away with a *WHOOOOSH!* Theresa and Chrystalline look toward it. Theresa asks, “So... what were you doing in my Arcana, anyways?”
4. Chrystalline raises one knee to display a tall brown boot. “Shoe shopping. I purchased this pair. Aren't they wondrous?” Theresa gestures dismissively. “Yeah... they're something alright.” In a small inset, a caricature of Theresa throws both arms up against a green background. “I swear! Blondes and shoes!”
