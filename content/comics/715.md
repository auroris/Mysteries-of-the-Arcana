1. The wolf-man bares his teeth at Mandrake as a tangled scrawl fills a burst beside his head.
2. Carrying Mandrake in his fist, he approaches a tall wooden door set in a stone wall. She projects a barred circle and an exclamation mark.
3. As he walks along the wall, Mandrake projects two rows of symbols: her face, lips, the smiling wolf, a gesturing figure with a trailing wisp, an equals sign, and a flying fairy; then a rain cloud, the smiling wolf, and an exclamation mark.
4. Mandrake smiles with her eyes closed and projects a barred circle, two musical notes, another barred circle, and a final musical note.
