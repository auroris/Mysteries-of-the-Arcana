1. Theresa says, “Wish I knew what they were after.”
2. Chrystalline replies, “I believe Mother and Kludge know.” Theresa asks, “And?”
3. Chrystalline says, “I asked. I was told the information was available on a need to know basis.” Theresa replies, “Let me guess. We don’t.”
4. Chrystalline answers, “Apparently not. We may never know for sure.”
5. She adds, “Still, not everything is so terrible, is it?” Theresa says, “No. I guess not.”
