1. Chrystalline sits alone outside Theresa’s tent with her knees drawn up. The hovering BbuzZ ask, “You okay?” Chrystalline replies, “I only brought two tents.”
2. From high above, Chrystalline appears tiny between the two tents. “Dammit.”
3. Theresa lies awake beneath a blanket. She narrates, “I was exhausted but I couldn’t fall asleep.”
4. Across a jagged dividing border, Chrystalline also lies awake beneath a blanket. Theresa continues, “My mind kept racing back to a dumb quote daddy always said just before we went hunting.”
