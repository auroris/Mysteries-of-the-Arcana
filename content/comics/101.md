1. Theresa stands with her arms crossed while Chrystalline tries to explain. “I don’t understand. I know you’re attracted to me.”
2. Chrystalline follows Theresa. “You responded to the kiss.”
3. Theresa turns on Chrystalline. “*I’m not gay!*” In a circular inset within the panel, Theresa looks away and adds more quietly, “...I’m Catholic.”
