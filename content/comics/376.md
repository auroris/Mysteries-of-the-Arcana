1. Theresa holds Chrystalline’s head as they kiss.
2. Theresa jerks back, wide-eyed and blushing, while Chrystalline faces outward with a knowing smile. “Hello! We’re taking two weeks off of our adventures in order to answer your questions about *Mysteries of the Arcana!*”
3. Miniature versions of the two women stand together as Theresa adds, “Right. Ten pages of in-depth answers about the comic, the characters, and all that crap.”
4. Chrystalline looks bewildered as a slip of paper bears three handwritten symbols resembling an inverted question mark, the numeral 3, and a question mark. She begins, “JB wants to know why William and I broke up. Well...” William, wearing an apron, points toward her and orders, “Woman, get thou into the kitchen and maketh me a sandwich!!!” A small inset shows Chrystalline grinning and shrugging. The footer reads “And there you have it!”
