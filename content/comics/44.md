1. Mandrake, a purple-skinned fairy with bright green wings, hovers before the group while carrying a pendant in pink light. In inset close views, Chrystalline says, “And there she is.” Theresa stares. “Buh...” William folds his ears back. “Hmph!”
2. Mandrake projects a yellow smiling face followed by a shining yellow sun. Chrystalline greets her, “Happy day to you as well, Mandrake!” Theresa points at the symbols. “I think I just went officially certifiable. I'm seeing little pictures floating over the fairy's head.” William folds his arms. “Thy sanity may be in question, but thine eyes are not. ’Tis how her people speak.”
