1. Theresa looks up at Mister Eerie. “Excuse me?” He replies, “You heard me.”
2. Theresa takes General Isc’s rifle with a *SNATCH*. “Give me that before you hurt yourself.”
3. She holds the rifle across her body. “*This* is a crap gun.”
4. Against a black background, Theresa’s assessment surrounds the rifle: “The metal’s soft. Barrel probably warps after a few shots.” “The sights are welded on so you can’t adjust them.” “The ammo feed’s loose so it probably jams a lot.” “The machining’s uneven so you’ve probably got splinters breaking loose inside the gun.” “And don’t even get me started on the quality of the ammunition!”
