1. Chrystalline raises both hands. “I surrender.”
2. Featherbeard gloats. “Finally! Boys, walk her to the brig! Our other prisoner could use the company.”
3. Two guards escort Chrystalline down a dim metal corridor.
4. They push her through a heavy cell door.
5. Chrystalline smiles faintly. “Hello...”
6. A long-haired man with pointy ears sits inside the dark cell. She finishes, “...Father.”
