1. Theresa approaches Chrystalline and William at the bar. “Hey. What's going on?”
2. Chrystalline takes Theresa's hand and pulls her away. “Very little, apparently. We should be going.” Circe calls after them, “Promise me you will wait for your father's return, Chrys.”
3. Chrystalline pauses mid-step, still holding Theresa's hand.
4. A close view holds on Chrystalline's face as she looks back. “I promise... I will not attempt to rescue my mother without assistance.”
