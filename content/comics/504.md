1. A glowing red piece appears on Kietra’s rifle.
2. Another piece snaps into place with a *KA-CHICK*.
3. Kietra says, “Shit!” as another piece appears with a *KA-CHICK*.
4. A final component joins the weapon with a *KA-CHICK*.
5. Kietra crouches and asks, “Who’s there?”
6. A small unidentified voice asks, “We playing hide and seek?”
7. Kietra lowers a green visor over her eyes. “I can play hide and seek.”
