1. Theresa and Chrystalline sit face-to-face in the tent. Theresa asks, “What happened?” Chrystalline answers, “The poison infiltrated your system. You collapsed.”
2. In a close view, Chrystalline continues, “Not, however, before you finished what the BbuzZ started.”
3. In memory, rubble from the acid-weakened tunnel ceiling blocks the passage as gas approaches. Chrystalline explains, “The blocked passage slowed the progress of the gas long enough for us to escape.” Theresa asks from outside the memory, “What about BbuzZ?”
4. In memory, Theresa’s deep inhalation pulls the five BbuzZ toward her open mouth with a *HHHHH!* Chrystalline says, “You saved them as well when you took that deep breath.”
5. Theresa covers her mouth and raises a hand to stop Chrystalline. “Stop! I do not want to know.”
