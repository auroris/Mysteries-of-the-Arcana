1. The tennis-clad horrors run forward. The man calls, “Look, dear, new neighbors!” The woman responds, “Let’s go welcome them, dumpling!”
2. Theresa fires repeatedly from her ant mount with *BLAM! BLAM! BLAM!* while William dismounts.
3. One bullet bounces off the female horror’s upper chest with a *TUNK!*
4. Unharmed, the female horror keeps advancing as Theresa continues firing with *BLAM! BLAM!* “We should go to the spa together!”
5. The female horror swings her tennis racket and destroys the ant’s head with a *SPLUTCH!* “I love what you’ve done with your hair!”
