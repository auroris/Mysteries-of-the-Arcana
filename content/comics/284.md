1. The tennis-clad horrors run forward. The woman calls, “LOOK, DEAR, NEW NEIGHBORS! LET’S GO WELCOME THEM, DUMPLING!”
2. Theresa fires repeatedly with *BLAM! BLAM! BLAM!* while William draws his dark blade.
3. A bullet passes through the woman’s upper chest with a *TUNK!*
4. The woman keeps advancing as Theresa continues firing with *BLAM! BLAM!* “WE SHOULD GO TO THE SPA TOGETHER!”
5. The woman reaches toward Theresa as William’s black blade slices into her from behind with a *SPLITCH!* “I LOVE WHAT YOU’VE DONE WITH YOUR HAIR!”
