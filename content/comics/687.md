1. Mandrake floats in darkness and broadcasts a question mark.
2. Mandrake flies between a red crystalline gateway above her and a yellow crystalline gateway to the right.
3. Green-white lightning forks behind Mandrake with a *KA-KRASH!* She broadcasts an exclamation mark.
4. Kludge stands on a gray platform as translucent images of Melody and Quest appear on adjacent platforms amid two more bursts of lightning. *KA-KRASH! KA-KRASH!* Mandrake flies nearby and broadcasts three exclamation marks.
5. Kludge, Quest, and Melody step toward the yellow crystalline gateway. Quest and Melody speak in two balloons filled with unfamiliar glyphs as Mandrake hovers beside them.
