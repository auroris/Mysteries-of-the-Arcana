1. Theresa falls upside down through darkness, clutching the feather in both hands. “Holdittightholdittight holdittight...”
2. Still inverted, Theresa looks down in surprise. “I'm not dead?”
3. Theresa's feet drift toward the distant ground. “I'm not dead!”
4. Theresa lands seated in a small burst of light with a *CLSH!* “I don't want to be dead.”
5. Theresa sits up and smiles, holding the feather beside her face. “Sweet.”
