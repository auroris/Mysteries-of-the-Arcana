1. Theresa smiles with the priest standing just behind her. “Yes, sir. I think he would be.”
2. The priest rests a hand on Theresa’s shoulder. “I think that’s what really matters, isn’t it?”
3. The priest turns away from Theresa. “Now, let me leave you to your prayers.” Theresa rises from the pew. “Actually, I should go. Someone’s waiting for me.”
4. The priest raises a hand as Theresa walks down the aisle. “Then go with God, my dear.” Theresa limps away. “Oh. Ow. Ow. Think the walk here jacked up my leg again.”
5. The priest holds up his hand in blessing. A bishop in a mitre holding a crosier appears behind him. “Go with God.” The closing text reads “The End.”
