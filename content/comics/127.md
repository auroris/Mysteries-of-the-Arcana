1. Orange shots hammer the block sheltering Theresa and Chrystalline with a *RATATATATATAT!* and *ATATATATATAT!* Theresa says, “Dammit!”
2. Theresa leans over the block and aims her pistol. “I can’t figure out how to do it.” Chrystalline asks, “Do what?”
3. Theresa ducks down. “You know. Like with the robot I shot back home!”
4. Chrystalline looks toward her. “With powers such as yours, meditation often helps.”
5. A shot splashes against the top of the block with a *SPLOOSH!* Theresa says, “Meditation. In the middle of a firefight? Great.”
