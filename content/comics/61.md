1. Chrystalline rests a hand on Theresa’s shoulder as Theresa sits with her face covered. “Theresa?” Theresa replies from behind her hands, “I don’t think I can do this.”
2. In a red-tinted memory, a preacher pounds one hand against a pulpit. “Struck down for their unnatural acts...”
3. In another red-tinted memory, a woman gestures before a presentation board labeled “Letter writing campaign to support propositi...” “These people have the devil in them.”
4. Chrystalline kneels beside Theresa with an arm around her shoulders. Theresa says, “I’m in sync with the Devil. What does that say about me?”
5. Chrystalline wraps both arms around Theresa. “Nothing horrible. I promise. Please...”
6. In a red-tinted memory, a smiling young man stands before two shadowed figures. “We just showed her what God made bitches for.”
7. Chrystalline rests her head against Theresa’s shoulder while holding her. “Please. I need you right now.” Theresa answers, “...Okay.”
