1. Chrystalline lowers the towel from her hair and looks puzzled. “I do not understand what you mean.”
2. Theresa gestures toward the elaborate clockwork device. “Kludge built this to keep track of time back home.”
3. The device fills the panel, its exposed gears turning above a numbered display. Theresa continues from off panel, “If I'm reading it right, tomorrow's my birthday. I turn eighteen.”
4. Chrystalline folds her arms beside Theresa. “I see. Your age of majority.” Theresa rests a hand on her hip. “That. Only not as pretentious sounding.”
