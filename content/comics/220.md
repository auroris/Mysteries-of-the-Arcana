Theresa speaks with her eyes closed against a flat green background. 

“After my dad died, I moved in with Debby and her family.

We were already close. Best friends.

In her house, though, spending so much time together, we grew ... closer.

When I was with her things were perfect! When we were apart, the sin of it weighed on me like an anchor.

I needed to talk to someone about it. Normally, I’d talk to Debby but ... anyway. This guy at school, Josh, everyone assumed he was gay. I talked to him. Asked his advice. He didn’t have any advice. Just betrayal. The bastard.

Josh ran right to Cliff, our school’s crown prince of jocks. He hoped whispering our dirty little secret to Cliff would get the jocks off his back.”
