1. Kietra and Aleena look across the quiet corridor. Kietra says, “COME ON. OUR CLIENT'S A'WAITIN'.” Aleena counts, “SIX.”
2. They pause together at the edge of a dark doorway.
3. Bruised and weary, Kietra and Aleena walk down a red corridor.
4. Behind them, a wounded alien guard rises in a doorway. Kietra shoots backward from the foreground, striking the guard in the chest with a *KZZAT*.
