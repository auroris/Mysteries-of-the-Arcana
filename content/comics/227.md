1. Theresa faces Chrystalline with tears on her cheeks. Chrystalline explains, “I wanted to help you.”
2. Chrystalline closes her eyes. “I... I wanted to save you.”
3. A memory shows Theresa sitting isolated while other guests talk together at the Inn. Chrystalline says, “I have watched you at the Inn. While others laugh and converse, you stare into nothing, lost and isolated. So obviously in pain. Your pain was like a poison festering inside. I worried it would kill you, given time. I also knew you would not share your pain.”
4. Theresa looks aside. “So you brought me here.”
5. Chrystalline bows her head. “So I brought you here.”
