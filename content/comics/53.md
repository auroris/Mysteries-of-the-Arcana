1. From overhead, Chrystalline demonstrates the route around the circular Royal Road and its colored tiles. “Step, the first. Stand in the center of the circle. Bow to each guardian in turn, beginning with fire. Step, the second. Proceed forward and stand upon the first tile. Walk widdershins around the circle until you reach the tile that represents the Arcana you wish to travel to.”
2. Chrystalline places one hand on her chest. “Step, the last. Sing the aria of opening.” William interrupts from off panel, “Bah!”
3. William smirks down at Chrystalline. “Superstitious claptrap.”
