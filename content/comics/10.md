1. As Theresa holds the injured Chrystalline, pink energy spreads beneath them with an *R-RUMBLE!!!!!*
2. The purple carpet of an unfamiliar ornate room appears beneath Theresa and Chrystalline as pink energy erupts around them. *R-RRRRMMBL!*
3. They land in the ornate room while the remaining view of the subway maintenance area contracts into a smaller opening behind them. A cropped figure stands at the far right.
4. Theresa looks around while supporting the unconscious Chrystalline. “What the fuck?”
5. Darkness fills the panel. Theresa narrates, “That’s when I passed out.”
