1. Theresa stands in the garden with a circular inset showing her hand over her face. “Hope the damn place likes haiku.” Words appear in the air as she recites:

   > Walking in gardens<br>
   > Holding hand’s with pretty girl’s<br>
   > Makes the flower’s grow

2. Bands of bright, multicolored energy wrap around Theresa.
3. The energy vanishes. Theresa throws out her arms and asks, “What the hell was that!?!”
