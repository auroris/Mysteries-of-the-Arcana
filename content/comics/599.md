1. Theresa crouches and examines a small stone at her feet. “What is it?”
2. Chrystalline says, “It seems the garden enjoyed your poem. *That* is a word of power.”
3. Theresa picks up the stone while Chrystalline rests a hand on her shoulder. “What do I do with it?”
4. Chrystalline helps Theresa stand and takes her hand. “Keep it for now. You will know when and how to use it. Or so the Viquirrels say.”
5. Theresa says, “Talking signs. Poetry porn gardens. Magic rocks. The Arcana just keep getting weirder and weirder.”
