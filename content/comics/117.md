1. William charges from cover at great speed as the sentry fires orange shots with a *RATATATATATAT!* Grey angular afterimages trail him, conveying his speed.
2. The red sentry continues firing with a *RATATATATAT!* as William rapidly closes the distance, leaving more grey afterimages behind him.
3. William leaps at the sentry.
4. William drives the empty hilt of his sword into the sentry with a *THUNK!* in a blue-white burst.
