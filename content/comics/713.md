1. The wolf-man holds Mandrake in one fist and smiles down at her as she projects a smiling wolf head.
2. Mandrake looks puzzled and projects, in order, her face, the wolf's face, an open hand, and a tiny flying fairy.
3. The wolf-man turns his head aside. A bright burst beside him contains “...”
4. He leans close with one eye wide and his teeth bared while Mandrake projects her face, lips, the wolf's face, a small gesturing figure with a trailing wisp, and a question mark.
