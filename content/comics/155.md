1. Chrystalline lowers her head. “You are right.”
2. Theresa points at The Surgeon. “Moving on. You! Talk!”
3. The Surgeon’s red display shows “04:31.” “[Statement] Displaying time remaining until detonation of one hundred megaton nuclear device.”
4. Theresa leans close to the display. “One hundred?! That’s insane! I mean, you only need a few dozen kilotons to take out an entire city!”
5. An overhead view shows the compound, the Deliverer, and scattered robots outside. The Surgeon continues, “[Oblivious continuation] This unit calculates a thirty-three percent chance of survival if it and the biologicals immediately take flight in the Deliverer parked outside.” In an inset, Theresa shouts, “Thirty-three percent!? Screw that!”
