1. Theresa and William stare at one another at close range.
2. Theresa sits upright over William, holding the stake and rope still tied to her right wrist. “Fine.”
3. Theresa untangles William’s legs from the rope and stake. “I’m going to get my boots.”
4. Theresa stalks away while William sits up behind her. “You better have my gun by the time I get them on.”
