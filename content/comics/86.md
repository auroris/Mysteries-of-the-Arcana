1. The plant creature fires its acid gun at the tunnel ceiling with a *PSHHHHH!!* The rock dissolves with a *HISSSSSSSS!!*, weakening the ceiling so the passage can collapse and block the gas the Doomwalkers are pumping inside.
2. While the weakening plant creature continues firing upward with a *PSHHHHH!!*, Theresa braces it. “Come on! Let’s go!”
3. Still spraying the ceiling with a *PSHHHHH!!*, the plant creature pushes Theresa away. The BbuzZ say, “Slowing death. Go! Run!”
4. The plant creature collapses into goo at Theresa’s feet with a *PLUNK!* Theresa says, “Dammit!”
5. The five BbuzZ hover close to Theresa’s face. She thinks, “Deep breath.” She inhales with a *HUHHHHH*.
