1. The wolf-man stands inside the open cellar doorway and grins.
2. At his boot, a snake-headed living rug laughs, “HA HA!”
3. White fire climbs the back of the wolf-man's cloak from near its hem.
4. He bolts across the cellar with one foot on the rug's trailing edge. A tangled scrawl flares beside him.
5. Seen through the doorway, the cellar fills with a wall of white flame.
