1. Kludge stares at the image in the Void. “That’s...” Theresa points toward it. “Chrys’s dad, right?”
2. Theresa holds up a recorder behind Kludge. He calls, “Quest?”
3. Quest’s image flickers in the Void. “...will reach anyone. I’ve been captured by the enemy.”
4. His image distorts further. “Will try to escape. The enemy knows... me... Chrystalline.”
5. A close view of Quest’s face wavers through static. “Protect her. Please.”
