1. The Theresa-shaped green slime stands directly in front of Theresa and blocks her from Chrystalline’s view.
2. As the slime begins to dissolve, Theresa’s face appears behind it.
3. Theresa closes her eyes as the slime continues to dissolve into a band of green mist.
4. Only green wisps remain after the slime disappears. Theresa stands with her eyes closed. “Thank God.”
