Theresa continues speaking with her eyes closed against a flat green background. 

“I’m not sure where they grabbed Debby. I know they dragged her into the school locker room, shoved her face down on a bench and...

... and raped her. Three of them. They took turns with her. The assholes took turns.

Debby had asthma. They taped over her mouth and she had an attack and ...

And by the time someone found her and got her into the hospital it was too late. Her body survived but her mind ...

Debby’s folks are devout Catholics, like most of our town. They wouldn’t give up. Wouldn’t let her go.

But I knew Debby. I knew she wouldn’t want to be a shell living on a machine.

So, I did the research. I figured out the quickest, least painful way to kill her. I snuck in, injected the drug into her IV, waited until she flatlined, and ran. Ran to go kill myself.

That’s when I met you.”
