1. A chessboard holds pieces shaped like people. Black Dragon speaks through a seated, white-haired humanoid piece. “It’s my own fault, I suppose.”
2. The piece folds its arms. “I wasn’t thorough in my research and Baron Sir clearly overestimated his team’s abilities.”
3. A bright slash crosses the piece. An unseen voice says, “Your pawns defeated my pawns.”
4. The piece curls inward. Black Dragon replies, “I won’t be exploring your research into the creation of the Royal Road today, it seems.”
