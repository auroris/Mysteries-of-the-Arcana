1. William sits wounded against the wall. “Verily...” Theresa interrupts, “No time. We triggered the place’s self destruct.”
2. Theresa crouches beside him. William asks, “How large?” She answers, “Hundred megatons.”
3. William exclaims, “Ye gods!” BbuzZ says, “New tactic. Disturbing.” Theresa thinks, “Okay. Smart. Fast. Just like Dad taught me.”
4. Theresa studies the Deliverer. She thinks, “No VTOL. Won’t have time to land. Only fast way down. Impact would squash everyone but BbuzZ like grapes.”
5. Theresa looks at the scarab on Melody’s forehead. She thinks, “Unless...”
6. Theresa asks Chrystalline, “How many of those bug things did you bring?” Chrystalline answers, “Three. Why?”
7. Theresa points toward the Deliverer. “Sweet! Everyone, into the boxes!”
