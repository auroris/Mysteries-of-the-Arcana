1. Mandrake projects pictograms of herself, a handshake, Theresa’s face, and a question mark, asking, “Mandrake meet Theresa?”
2. Chrystalline gestures toward Mandrake, who hovers beside her. “Of course. Theresa, meet Mandrake. Our provisioner.”
3. Mandrake projects a yellow smiling face, a handshake, and Theresa's face, meaning, “Happy meeting Theresa.” Theresa gives an awkward wave. “Umm...nice to meet you, too, I guess.”
4. Mandrake projects Theresa's face, a red prohibition symbol, a green curved arrow, a flexed arm, and a question mark, asking, “Theresa not overly muscular?” Chrystalline takes Theresa's hands. “Not at all! You should have seen her when she rescued me. She was beautiful!”
5. Mandrake projects pictograms of Chrystalline’s face, a plus sign, Theresa’s face, a kiss, and a question mark, asking, “Chrystalline and Theresa kiss?” Theresa blushes.
6. Theresa looks aside, still blushing. Chrystalline answers from off panel, “Once. When the mission ends and she and I can spend more time...exploring.”
