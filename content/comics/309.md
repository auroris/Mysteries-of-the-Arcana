1. A control marked with a green circle activates with a *CLICK!* Mandrake speaks in picture symbols showing Mandrake’s face, an arrow, and a door.
2. Chrystalline rises from a closing pod. “WHAT ARE YOU DOING?!?” Mandrake answers in picture symbols with a smiling face, a four-leaf clover, and an exclamation mark.
3. Chrystalline sets her jaw. “VERY WELL. I CAN DO THIS ALONE.”
4. She stands at a railing and declares, “I *DEMAND* TO SPEAK TO YOUR *CAPTAIN!*”
5. Masked guards surround her, aiming glowing weapons that make *BZZZ*, *BZZ*, *BUZZ*, and *ZZZT*. She adds, “...PLEASE?”
