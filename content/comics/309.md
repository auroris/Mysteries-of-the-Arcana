1. A control marked with a green circle activates with a *CLICK!* Mandrake speaks in picture symbols showing Mandrake’s face, an arrow, and a door.
2. Chrystalline rises from her seat as the cockpit opens. “What are you doing?!?” Mandrake answers in picture symbols with a smiling face, a four-leaf clover, and an exclamation mark.
3. Chrystalline sets her jaw. “Very well. I can do this alone.”
4. She stands and declares, “I demand to speak to your captain!”
5. Guards surround her, aiming glowing weapons that make *BZZZ*, *BZZ*, *BUZZ*, and *ZZZT*. She adds, “...Please?”
